# Licensing scope

**Effective date:** 2026-09-22. **Licence:** MIT. **Authority:** explicit project-owner instruction to license the four project repositories under MIT and credit their contributors.

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) applies to the project-authored materials distributed in this repository. The copyright notice credits all contributors listed in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md), for their respective contributions. The licence text follows the [Open Source Initiative MIT text](https://opensource.org/license/mit).

| Material | Current terms |
| --- | --- |
| Programme prose, governance drafts, standards working drafts, specifications, manuals, technical reports and planning documents | MIT for project-authored content. A licence does not change a publication's maturity, adoption or authority. |
| Schemas, code, examples, validation fixtures and development tools | MIT for project-authored content. |
| Project-authored foundation manuscripts and associated research material | MIT is an additional grant for the project-authored content in the copies distributed here. Existing document notices remain preserved as part of their publication history. |
| Application infrastructure and its application submodules | Each repository has its own MIT `LICENSE`, `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and scope notice. |
| External standards, quotations, reproduced figures, third-party components and other externally authored material | Their respective rights and notices continue to apply; the project does not relicense them. |
| Private runtime records and material supplied by application users | Not covered by this repository licence merely because the software stores or processes them. |

The additional MIT grant for project-authored material does not remove attribution or revoke previously offered permissions. Historical PDFs, archived publications and preserved Git history are not rewritten to conceal earlier terms. When redistributing a project-authored PDF under the additional MIT grant, include `LICENSE`, `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and this scope notice with it so that the additional permission is available alongside the embedded historical notice. Externally authored material within such a document remains subject to its own terms.

This licensing decision implements the owner's repository instruction. It does not adopt the programme's draft governance instruments, establish a new legal entity, assign contributors' copyrights to a maintainer, or license the names and marks of referenced organizations. Future contributions follow the terms in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); permission for third-party contributions is not inferred from repository access alone.
