\GSPpreface{Foreword}
This document specifies the proposed mandate and working obligations of GR/TC 1. It is a Governance Working Draft and does not constitute the committee or approve a work item. Version 0.2 retains the original TC requirement identifiers and develops the operational steps, review records, pilot controls, and readiness evidence required to exercise the mandate.

Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/technical_committee_charter.tex}. Intended readers are the programme decision authority, prospective committee participants, editors, domain reviewers, affected people, implementers, and maintenance owners.

The Charter concerns committee performance and the evidence submitted for programme decisions. Programme authority, formal review periods, voting, and appeals remain governed by the companion programme Charter and Directives. Technical requirements are adopted through those procedures, not through this document's existence.

\GSPpreface{Introduction}
Developing infrastructure for warranted recognition requires coordination across several kinds of work. A coherent representation can remain evidentially weak; an accessible presentation can expose protected people; and a successful prototype can rely on an institutional authority that does not exist. Committee work therefore needs explicit responsibilities for source interpretation, requirements, practical procedures, implementation, multidisciplinary review, and evidence of effects.

The committee's organizing task is to preserve distinguishable histories of the situation under inquiry, evidence and judgment, and the categories or methods used to recognize it. Requirements begin with a bounded practical or normative problem. Specifications implement reusable infrastructure where that need is demonstrated. Pilots test the resulting practices and reveal costs, exclusions, loss, and failures as well as successful behavior.

One coordinated committee is proposed initially. Additional working groups are activated by actual scope and accepted participation rather than by a list of anticipated disciplines. Review of epistemic, ethical, institutional, legal, political-economic, archival, and technical issues remains distributed across competent and affected participants, with missing capacity reported.

\section{Scope}
\subsection{Committee subject}
This Charter specifies constitution, work-item control, assignment of functions, source and requirements discipline, multidisciplinary review, pilot management, publication progression, and maintenance coordination for GR/TC 1. The assessed subject is an identified committee activity or deliverable-development process within its recorded mandate.


\subsection{Limits and portfolio}
The proposed scope includes vocabulary, recording requirements, epistemic representation, evidence and interpretation, contestation and revision, institutional use, information models, exchange, presentation, preservation, access, and assessment.

\section{Terms and definitions}
\subsection{Work item}
An authorized bounded activity with a problem, scope, intended outputs, responsibilities, dependencies, review plan, and expected evidence. A preliminary proposal is not an authorized work item.

\subsection{Requirement baseline}
An identified edition of candidate provisions and their associated source, decision, dependency, and assessment records used for a specified review or exercise.

\subsection{Review finding}
A recorded assessment of a stated question within a reviewer's competence, identifying evidence, reasons, limits, effect on progression, and requested disposition.

\subsection{Blocking finding}
A finding that a mandatory procedural, authority, protection, rights, or evidential condition for the proposed progression remains unmet. Its materiality is recorded against that progression, not asserted by a label alone.

\subsection{Pilot}
A bounded exercise of proposed practices or implementations under a declared purpose, case, authority, permissions, evaluation method, and stopping arrangements.

\subsection{Independent evidence}
Evidence produced or reviewed with the relevant organizational, authoring, financial, and practical dependencies declared and assessed for the particular independence claim.

\subsection{Assessment method}
A specified means of evaluating an applicable requirement, including its inputs, procedure, expected evidence, result categories, and limits.

\subsection{Coordination issue}
A disagreement or dependency spanning deliverables or domains whose resolution affects scope, meaning, implementation, assessment, or authority.

\section{Mandate and work programme}
\subsection{Authorization and entry}

\req{TC-001}{The committee shall become active only through a recorded mandate under the adopted programme Charter. The mandate shall identify its convenor, responsible decision authority, participating capacities, accepted work items, review resources, and mandate review date.}
\req{TC-002}{The committee shall develop coordinated recording and practical procedures that support warranted, contestable, revisable recognition and preservation of consequential generative trajectories. Each accepted work item shall state its contribution to this purpose and the limits of its expected effects.}
\req{TC-003}{Committee work shall distinguish changes in the situation under inquiry, changes in evidence and judgment, and changes in categories or methods of recognition. A technical representation connecting these histories shall preserve their distinguishable meaning.}
\req{TC-004}{The committee shall retain the distinctions between theoretical metamodel, operational record ontology, epistemic representation, normative assessment, jurisprudential status, institutional governance, political economy, and implementation when defining scopes or allocating work.}
\req{TC-005}{The committee shall not claim authority to certify substantive historical truth, resolve a complete metaphysics, determine legal rights across jurisdictions, endorse a political choice on technical grounds alone, or mandate unrestricted disclosure of recorded material.}
\req{TC-006}{The committee shall preserve existing publication identifiers when refining scopes. A proposal to change a publication's class, merge or divide its scope, or introduce a new identifier shall record the reason, dependencies, alternatives, and authorizing decision before the change is treated as operative.}
\req{TC-007}{A work-item proposal shall identify purpose, scope and exclusions, conformance subjects, affected interests, foundation sources and tensions, existing alternatives, related publications, anticipated Specifications, reviewer competence, practical evidence, and maintenance ownership.}
\req{TC-010}{A proposed substantive scope expansion shall return to the responsible decision authority with its consequences for participation, sources, dependencies, review, and resources. Work outside the authorized scope shall remain exploratory pending a decision.}
\req{TC-032}{On acceptance of a work item, the convenor shall create a project record linking the authorized proposal, actual decision, bounded outputs, accepted assignments, resources, milestones, dependencies, and unresolved conditions. The record shall distinguish preliminary drafting already performed from work formally authorized.}
\req{TC-033}{Before scheduling a progression review, the convenor shall verify that the work remains within its mandate, that required responsibilities are staffed, and that necessary evidence is available. A scope or capacity defect shall receive correction, narrowing, or deferral rather than an unsupported stage label.}

\subsection{Programme coordination}
\req{TC-008}{The committee shall record work-item dependencies and allocate requirements to their responsible publication before producing a normative technical binding. A shared requirement shall have one authoritative location, with versioned references from dependent work.}
\req{TC-009}{Editors shall begin with the normative or procedural need and create a Specification proposal when reusable infrastructure is required to satisfy or assess it. A prototype shall not silently determine a theoretical definition or institutional obligation.}
\req{TC-034}{The committee shall maintain a dependency register identifying each shared concept, requirement, interface, profile, or artifact; its responsible publication and owner; its exact version; and the consequence of change. Competing normative homes shall be resolved before the affected requirement is stabilized.}
\req{TC-035}{For a coordination issue, the convenor shall identify affected editors and reviewers, record alternatives and semantic or institutional consequences, and seek an explicit decision within the appropriate authority. Technical convenience shall not resolve a normative, legal, or theoretical disagreement without that decision.}
\req{TC-036}{A split, merger, pause, or termination of a work item shall identify the fate of requirement identifiers, comments, evidence, responsibilities, dependencies, and publication expectations. The programme decision authority shall authorize the revised scope before dependent formal progression.}

\subsection{Specification and protocol initiation}
\req{TC-037}{A Specification proposal shall identify the candidate requirement it supports, the need for a reusable technical contract, implementation alternatives, version assumptions, and the evidence expected from a prototype. An institutional procedure shall identify its actual authority separately from protocol mechanics.}
\req{TC-038}{Before treating a protocol draft as ready for implementation, its editor shall define participants, inputs, preconditions, state transitions, observable outcomes, refusals, failures, recovery, authorization boundaries, and responsibility for interrupted or conflicting operations. Unresolved behavior shall be marked and excluded from unsupported completeness claims.}

\section{Organization and conduct of work}
\subsection{Assignments and competence}
\req{TC-011}{For each active deliverable, the committee shall record an accepted editor or lead, a maintenance contact, required review competence, available reviewers, and unfilled roles. Multiple roles held by one person shall be declared.}
\req{TC-012}{A new working group shall have a bounded scope, actual willing participants, an accountable lead, dependencies, intended outputs, and review or closure conditions. Its formation shall not imply autonomous adoption authority.}
\req{TC-013}{A reviewer shall identify the domain and scope of their assessment, relevant competence or situated knowledge, interests, material limitations, and whether they contributed to the material reviewed. A self-review shall not be labelled independent.}
\req{TC-014}{Where required expertise or affected perspectives are unavailable, the committee shall record the gap and its material effect on progression. It shall narrow claims, seek additional review, or defer the affected decision when the gap prevents a justified assessment.}
\req{TC-039}{An editor shall accept responsibility for the deliverable's current baseline, change record, source and requirement trace, pending comments, and progression evidence. A reviewer shall accept a bounded assessment assignment rather than an unspecified endorsement of the entire publication.}
\req{TC-040}{The convenor shall compare each work item's planned drafting, review, accessibility, protection, and maintenance tasks with actual participant capacity. A shortage affecting mandatory evidence or a safeguard shall change the schedule or scope and be reported to the programme decision authority.}

\subsection{Meetings and asynchronous work}
\req{TC-041}{For a substantive committee discussion, the convenor shall circulate the purpose, input versions, questions, expected output, access arrangements, and route for asynchronous contributions before the decision is taken. Participants unable to attend shall have the applicable opportunity to contribute under Directives Part 1.}
\req{TC-042}{The discussion record shall distinguish persons present from represented institutions, information reported from findings accepted, and recommendations from authorized decisions. Material reasons, alternatives, objections, actions, owners, and affected baseline versions shall be recorded.}
\req{TC-043}{Where new material changes the basis of a proposed decision during a meeting, the chair shall identify the change and apply the relevant further-review procedure. Attendance or an informal show of support shall not replace required notice, reply, or ballot.}

\subsection{Handover and group closure}
\req{TC-044}{An editor or working-group handover shall provide the current baseline, requirement and source registers, unresolved comments and findings, dependencies, permissions, build or assessment instructions, and pending deadlines. The convenor shall verify receipt and record any gap before further reliance on the work.}
\req{TC-045}{A working group that has no accepted lead or capacity to discharge its mandate shall be reported as inactive or pending reassignment. Its outputs shall retain their actual maturity, and unfinished responsibilities shall be reassigned or explicitly deferred.}

\section{Source interpretation and requirements}
\subsection{Foundation and external sources}
\req{TC-015}{Before introducing or materially changing a GR definition, notation, ontology class, or compatibility claim, the responsible editor shall examine the relevant foundation manuscripts and record exact source locations, terminology, scope, historical differences, and the proposed operational translation.}
\req{TC-016}{The committee shall preserve unresolved foundation differences rather than harmonizing them silently. A material operational narrowing or departure shall be recorded through an architectural decision with alternatives, rationale, affected publications, and remaining uncertainty.}
\req{TC-017}{A candidate record-class definition shall identify its definition, admissibility conditions, identity criterion, temporal behavior, permitted relations, required and optional metadata, examples, counterexamples, and unresolved edge cases, or explicitly identify which elements remain under development.}
\req{TC-018}{An external compatibility or reuse proposal shall identify the exact source and edition, verified relevant provisions, mapping assumptions, exclusions, and evidence needed to support the claimed relationship. Similar terminology shall not alone establish equivalence.}
\req{TC-046}{The source register shall distinguish source wording, editorial paraphrase, historical variant, operational proposal, and accepted programme decision. Each material interpretation shall identify the relevant passage and version, verification status, and unresolved difference.}
\req{TC-047}{When a source is revised, newly available, or found to have been misinterpreted, the editor shall identify affected definitions and requirements, assess the consequence, and submit necessary changes through the appropriate procedure. Earlier interpretations and the reason for revision shall remain traceable.}

\subsection{Requirement formation}
\req{TC-048}{For each candidate requirement, the editor shall identify the responsible actor or subject, applicability condition, required outcome, assessment method, evidence expectation, source or operational rationale, and related issue or decision. A requirement with unresolved scope or testability shall retain a visible development issue.}
\req{TC-049}{The editor shall separate independently assessable obligations where one result would conceal different applicability or failure. Recommendations, examples, and explanatory theory shall remain distinguishable from mandatory provisions.}
\req{TC-050}{Before stabilizing a requirement baseline, the review group shall examine examples, counterexamples, unknown or unavailable information, restricted records, and relevant conflicting interpretations. Required uncertainty declarations shall not be replaced by invented information to complete a record.}

\subsection{Assessment design and claims}
\req{TC-026}{A conformance proposal shall identify the assessed subject, edition, profile, applicable requirements, methods, evidence, unresolved findings, and limits of the resulting claim. Syntax checks, semantic interoperability, procedural compliance, usability, and substantive warrant shall receive distinguishable results.}
\req{TC-027}{The committee shall substantiate an interoperability claim with independent implementation or exchange evidence appropriate to the claim. A single internally consistent prototype shall be reported as such.}
\req{TC-051}{The requirement-to-assessment register shall identify which findings arise from automated checks, observed human tasks, documentary review, independent implementation, or substantive expert judgment. The reported result shall not claim evidence beyond what the chosen method establishes.}
\req{TC-052}{A proposed profile shall identify inherited requirements, additions, permitted restrictions, applicability, and assessment consequences. The editor shall identify any divergence that prevents unqualified parent conformance and propose an appropriately bounded claim.}
\req{TC-053}{An asserted external mapping shall identify direction, supported distinctions, unsupported cases, loss, and the exact tested source versions. An untested mapping shall be labelled proposed rather than interoperable.}

\section{Multidisciplinary review}
\subsection{Planning and scope}
\req{TC-019}{At work-item scoping, before a real pilot, and before publication progression, the committee shall maintain a review dossier covering applicable epistemic, ethical, jurisprudential, institutional, political-economic, archival, privacy/security, accessibility, and technical questions. Each domain shall have findings or an explicit justified statement of non-applicability or unassessed scope.}
\req{TC-020}{The review dossier shall identify evidence, reviewers and their capacities, affected perspectives, conflicts, alternative designs, findings, dispositions, owners of corrective action, and residual limitations. A metadata field or completed checklist shall not by itself count as domain assessment.}
\req{TC-054}{The review plan shall state the questions, needed competence and affected perspectives, evidence, review independence sought, access arrangements, and decision points for each applicable domain. It shall distinguish non-applicability from unavailable review capacity.}

\subsection{Findings and disagreement}
\req{TC-055}{Each domain reviewer shall classify a finding by the affected requirement or assumption, evidence and uncertainty, consequence for the proposed decision, and proposed disposition. A disagreement about values or policy shall be identified as such rather than converted into an unexplained technical defect.}
\req{TC-056}{The editor shall respond to each material finding with acceptance, modification, reasoned non-acceptance, or deferral, linking the resulting text or action. A deferred finding shall retain an owner, next action, and explanation of whether progression is prevented.}
\req{TC-057}{Where reviewers disagree across domains, the convenor shall preserve their respective questions, evidence, competence, and reasons and obtain a decision from the authority competent for the matter. A technical majority shall not substitute for an absent legal mandate or resolve a philosophical source conflict by assertion.}
\req{TC-058}{Before recommending progression, the convenor shall verify that material findings and replies have been considered and that mandatory authority, evidence, rights, and protection conditions are satisfied. A decision to narrow scope shall identify the excluded behavior and prevent it from appearing in the resulting conformance claim.}

\subsection{Jurisprudential and policy interfaces}
\req{TC-021}{A legal or institutional profile shall state its jurisdiction or forum, target action, relevant sources and status, claimed authority, temporal scope, unresolved conflicts, and reviewing competence. Generic technical conformance shall not replace assessment of actual applicability.}
\req{TC-022}{A policy proposal arising from committee work shall follow the separate policy-publication route and compare plausible institutional options, including existing infrastructure or deferral when relevant. The committee shall not present participation in technical review as assent to a policy recommendation.}
\req{TC-059}{A jurisdictional or institutional reviewer shall distinguish a party's assertion, an applicable source identified by the reviewer, an institution's operative decision, and an unresolved interpretation. The profile shall state the temporal and contextual limits of that review.}
\req{TC-060}{For an institutional or international-policy proposal, the committee shall identify which technical findings are evidence for the proposal and which recommendations concern mandate, funding, distribution, oversight, or adoption choices. Unaccepted institutional participation shall remain prospective.}

\section{Pilot preparation and authorization}
\subsection{Protocol and evidence plan}
\req{TC-023}{Before a pilot begins, its record shall identify the case and baseline, questions and success criteria, participants and actual authority, material and access permissions, resource burden, anticipated harms, challenge and correction routes, stop conditions, and responsible reviewers. Synthetic cases shall be labelled synthetic.}
\req{TC-024}{The initial coordinated demonstration shall exercise a claim and its grounds, a consequential relation or contribution, an alternative or challenge, a reasoned response, a historical revision, a protected or unavailable record, and a presentation that makes their distinct status understandable.}
\req{TC-061}{The pilot plan shall pin the requirement baseline, artifacts, scenario, input material, evaluation tasks, expected results, assessors, and comparison baseline before results are interpreted. A change during the exercise shall identify the reason and its effect on comparability.}
\req{TC-062}{For a real pilot, the responsible authority shall verify participant and institutional permissions, rights to material, protection arrangements, accessible challenge routes, available remedy, and stopping authority before the affected activity starts. A planned safeguard shall not be reported as operational without evidence of implementation.}
\req{TC-063}{A pilot relying on restricted evidence shall identify how authorized assessors can evaluate the relevant requirement and how a public result can communicate limitations safely. Lack of public access shall not be equated with absence of evidence or accepted as proof of adequacy.}

\subsection{Execution and interruption}
\req{TC-064}{The pilot operator shall retain observations of performed tasks, relevant inputs and outputs, failures, participant assistance, departures from the plan, and protection incidents, within the approved access and retention conditions. Simulated events shall remain distinguishable from observed real behavior.}
\req{TC-065}{When a stopping condition is met or an unanticipated material harm or authority defect is identified, the pilot operator shall pause the affected activity, notify the responsible authority, protect necessary evidence, and obtain a recorded disposition before restart. Unaffected activity may continue only within its existing authority and safeguards.}
\req{TC-066}{A pilot restart shall identify the corrected condition, evidence that the response is operational, changed baseline or scope, required further permissions or notice, and the decision maker. A technically successful repair shall not alone establish restored participant protection.}

\section{Pilot evaluation and implementation evidence}
\subsection{Results and learning}
\req{TC-025}{Pilot findings shall report failed tasks, missing or conflicting evidence, excluded participants, unmet assumptions, and burdens alongside successful behavior. The committee shall trace each material finding to a requirement change, retained decision with reasons, further inquiry, or scope limitation.}
\req{TC-067}{The evaluator shall report the actual participant population, excluded or unavailable perspectives, resource burden, supported tasks, failed cases, assistance required, uncertainty, and limits on transfer to other contexts. A small cooperative exercise shall not be presented as evidence of general institutional adoption.}
\req{TC-068}{The committee shall compare pilot results with the declared objectives and assessment criteria and trace each material finding to a requirement change, retained decision with reasons, further inquiry, or narrower claim. Unexpected benefits and harms shall be identified separately from the planned criteria.}
\req{TC-069}{Where an outcome depends on later institutional action or effects on future capacity, the report shall state whether that action or effect was observed, is pending, or was outside the study period. A corrected record shall not be treated as evidence that downstream consequences were repaired.}

\subsection{Reproducibility and independence}
\req{TC-070}{An implementation-evidence package shall identify source and artifact versions, configuration, test material, expected and actual outcomes, access or licence constraints, and instructions sufficient for an authorized reviewer to repeat the relevant assessment. Reproduction gaps shall be disclosed.}
\req{TC-071}{For an independence claim, the convenor shall record who authored the requirements, implemented the artifacts, selected the cases, performed the assessment, and funded or controlled the work. The claimed degree of independence shall be limited to the separations actually evidenced.}

\section{Progression, maintenance, and publication}
\subsection{Readiness dossier}
\req{TC-028}{For each requested progression decision, the convenor shall assemble the current draft, requirement and source trace, comment dispositions, unresolved objections, multidisciplinary findings, pilot or other assessment evidence, rights-clearance status, and maintenance proposal. The programme decision authority shall apply Directives Part 1.}
\req{TC-072}{The progression dossier shall identify the requested decision and provide a requirement-by-requirement evidence status, unresolved finding register, dependency review, public-comment status where applicable, source and rights clearance, and implementation or pilot limitations. Missing evidence shall remain visible rather than being treated as tacit approval.}
\req{TC-073}{Before forwarding a public-review or release package, an assigned reviewer shall check that the source, rendered document, associated artifacts, and declared status agree and that references, identifiers, and essential content remain usable. Material discrepancies shall be corrected through the applicable change procedure.}

\subsection{Change and maintenance}
\req{TC-029}{The committee shall retain a link from a corrected or superseded requirement to the prior text, reason, decision, affected dependencies, and migration consequence. Historical decisions shall remain distinguishable from the current authoritative edition.}
\req{TC-030}{The committee shall submit scope, participation, resource, and outstanding-risk information at its mandate review and when material conditions change. An inactive or unstaffed work item shall receive an explicit status and continuity disposition.}
\req{TC-074}{A substantive change shall identify affected requirements, interpretations, assessment fixtures, profiles, dependencies, existing users, and migration consequences. The maintenance owner shall distinguish a correction from an altered obligation and invoke the corresponding Directives route.}
\req{TC-075}{On evidence of a serious defect or loss of a necessary dependency, the convenor shall notify the authorized maintenance or release holder with the affected versions, evidence, consequences, and proposed interim response. The committee shall not issue a permanent normative change through an informal implementation update.}

\section{Committee assessment}
\subsection{Assessment method}
\req{TC-076}{An assessment of committee performance shall declare the activity, period, mandate and governance editions, assessor, interests, applicable TC requirements, evidence, and result for each requirement. Results shall distinguish fulfilled, not fulfilled, not assessed, and not applicable.}
\req{TC-077}{The assessment shall include a trace from a selected material problem through source interpretation, candidate requirement, alternatives, assessment method, pilot or review evidence, disposition, and authorized decision. Breaks in the trace shall be recorded with their effect on the claimed readiness.}

\subsection{Correction and oversight}
\req{TC-078}{The convenor shall assign owners and review dates for material performance findings and report unresolved scope, participation, capacity, or protection issues to the programme decision authority. Closing an action shall identify evidence of completion and any remaining limitation.}

\subsection{Work-item and baseline record}
\req{TC-079}{The committee shall maintain a work-item and baseline record containing the authorized problem and scope, decision and mandate, output classes, accepted roles, sources, dependencies, versions, requirements, assessment methods, review milestones, permissions, resources, and remaining conditions. Each substantive update shall identify the responsible actor and reason.}

\subsection{Source and requirement trace}
\req{TC-080}{The editor shall maintain a trace for each material requirement identifying its stable identifier, responsible publication, condition and subject, source passage or operational proposal, alternatives, decision rationale, assessment method, evidence, dependency versions, and unresolved question. Superseded interpretations shall remain linked to their replacements.}

\subsection{Domain finding record}
\req{TC-081}{A domain finding record shall identify the question, scope, reviewer and competence, relevant interests, evidence and access limitations, assessed alternatives, finding and reasons, progression effect, response, reply, action owner, and closure evidence. A safe public account shall be linked where the full record is restricted.}

\subsection{Pilot and assessment record}
\req{TC-082}{A pilot record shall identify its authorization, actual participants, baseline versions, scenario, data and permissions, evaluation tasks, expected and actual outcomes, failures, burdens, deviations, stopping or restart decisions, evidence limitations, and requirement dispositions. Synthetic and independent elements shall be explicitly identified.}

\subsection{Progression and handover index}
\req{TC-083}{The convenor shall provide an index linking the current baseline, scope decision, review and comment dispositions, pilot evidence, blocking findings, rights status, artifact manifest, maintenance assignment, and requested decision. The index shall identify unavailable or incomplete elements and their proposed treatment.}

\GSPannex{informative}{Worked review and implementation cases}
\subsection{Missing evidence mistaken for non-occurrence}
A synthetic institution records an uncalibrated instrument because no calibration field was present. A participant later supplies a restricted maintenance log. The exercise tests whether the representation distinguishes absent metadata from an occurrence claim, whether authorized review can consider the log, and whether the presentation exposes the changed basis without disclosing protected detail. Source and requirements review examine the inference; institutional review examines who can reconsider the consequential decision.

\subsection{Technically successful but incomplete exchange}
Two implementations exchange a current judgment successfully but omit its prior category and an unresolved objection. A syntax result can remain successful while trajectory and presentation requirements fail. The failure becomes evidence for a changed exchange requirement or an explicitly narrower profile, rather than being hidden by the successful parse.

\subsection{Conflicting review considerations}
An archival reviewer favors retaining an earlier attribution, while a protection reviewer identifies a risk in public release. Their findings address different questions. The committee examines preservation with restricted access, a safe public correction, and retention limits. A decision states the authority and evidence for each outcome without declaring either archival value or protection irrelevant.

\subsection{Deferred theory and bounded implementation}
A proposed mapping treats Property and the metamodel manuscript's Attribute as identical. The foundation register retains the difference and identifies the exact operational scope being proposed. An exploratory implementation can test one mapping with an experimental label. Stabilizing it requires the recorded interpretation and architectural decision; implementation convenience does not supply the missing conceptual argument.

\subsection{Unresolved matters}
\openissue{Committee appointments, accepted work items, domain-review capacity, actual independent implementers, and real institutional pilot mandates remain unassigned or unapproved. An exercise against this draft does not fill those roles.}
\openissue{Property/Attribute interpretation, manuscript version precedence, and the relation between fixed-carrier semantics and broader emergence remain source questions. Their resolution is separate from the constitution of the committee.}
