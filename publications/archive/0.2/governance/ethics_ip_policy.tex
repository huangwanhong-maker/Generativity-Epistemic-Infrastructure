\GSPpreface{Foreword}
This document specifies proposed programme requirements for conduct, conflict management, ethical impact assessment, contribution rights, and implementation-rights concerns. It is a Governance Working Draft. Version 0.2 preserves the EIP identifiers from version 0.1, clarifies the distinction between the mandatory acknowledgement period and extendible determination target in EIP-014, and adds operational handling, assessment, and evidence requirements.

Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/ethics_ip_policy.tex}. Intended readers are contributors, affected people, ethics and rights reviewers, project leads, custodians, and the programme decision authority.

Adoption requires the programme's actual authority and recorded procedure. This text is not a licence offer, copyright transfer, patent assurance, or legal determination. The specific organizational and jurisdictional context remains to be established for commitments that depend on it.

\GSPpreface{Introduction}
The infrastructure being developed can affect whose experience becomes visible, whose evidence receives consideration, and who can challenge an authoritative account. Programme participation can reproduce unequal access, while publication and preservation can expose people or depend on rights the programme does not hold. Ethical assessment therefore concerns both development conduct and the effects of proposed practices.

This policy separates four inquiries: whether programme conduct meets its rules; whether an interest impairs a particular role; whether a design or pilot has unacceptable or unresolved effects; and whether a proposed use of material has an established basis. Evidence of one does not resolve another. A well-attributed contribution can remain inaccurate; an ethically desirable publication can lack distribution permission; and a contribution from an interested person can still contain relevant evidence.

The policy supplies procedures and records rather than a universal score of ethical adequacy or generativity. It retains value disagreement, limitations of inquiry, and the distinction between internal programme remedies and external legal proceedings.

\section{Scope}
\subsection{Subjects and application}
This policy applies to participants acting in programme functions, programme decisions about conduct and conflicts, impact review of work items and pilots, and intake or distribution of contributed material. Its assessed subject is a specified activity, decision, contribution, or handling process under a declared edition.


\subsection{Authority boundary}
Internal corrective measures concern participation, assigned functions, programme access, and programme publications. Financial compensation, enforceable legal duties, jurisdiction, and findings of legal liability require separately established authority. Contribution rights and legal applicability remain subject to their actual context.

\section{Terms and definitions}
\subsection{Material interest}
A financial, institutional, personal, authorship, funding, or infrastructure-control interest relevant to impartial performance of a specified programme function.

\subsection{Conflict-management decision}
A reasoned determination of whether an interest affects a particular function and what disclosure, oversight, separation, recusal, replacement, or deferral is required.

\subsection{Report}
Information submitted concerning possible conduct, protection, conflict, or rights problems. Receipt does not establish the allegation.

\subsection{Reported person}
A person whose programme conduct is the subject of a report or inquiry. The term does not imply a concluded finding.

\subsection{Protective measure}
A temporary action intended to limit an identified immediate risk while fair consideration remains incomplete. It is distinct from a final conduct finding or sanction.

\subsection{Rights clearance}
A scoped programme determination that an identified use of particular material is supported by recorded permissions or another reviewed basis, subject to stated limitations.

\subsection{Contribution}
Material submitted for a stated programme purpose, including prose, comments, schemas, software, examples, data, or source materials. Submission alone does not establish ownership or a licence for further uses.

\subsection{Impact finding}
An assessed actual or anticipated consequence of a proposed practice, identifying affected interests, evidence, uncertainty, values, alternatives, and proposed response.

\subsection{Retaliation}
Adverse programme treatment because a person raised a concern, gave relevant evidence, participated in review, or exercised a permitted challenge route. A reasoned response to independently established misconduct is assessed separately.

\section{Responsibilities and readiness}
\subsection{Assignment and independence}

\req{EIP-001}{The programme shall identify the adopted edition, effective scope, responsible ethics contact and alternate, protected reporting route, decision authority, and neutral-review route before treating this policy as operational. Until then it shall retain Governance Working Draft status.}
\req{EIP-002}{The programme shall assess conduct, conflicts, substantive impacts, and contribution rights through distinguishable findings. A favorable finding in one area shall not be represented as clearance in another.}
\req{EIP-042}{The governance authority shall assign an ethics contact, an alternate, conflict-decision responsibility, competent rights review, record custody, and neutral procedural review before relying on those functions. Each holder shall accept a bounded mandate and declare interests; an unassigned function shall remain visibly unavailable.}
\req{EIP-043}{The ethics contact shall receive and triage reports, arrange appropriate consideration, communicate status, and protect access. The investigator or assessor shall examine the stated question; the authorized decision maker shall determine programme findings and remedies. Where functions are combined, the record shall identify resulting limits and independent review needed.}
\req{EIP-044}{Before opening a protected reporting route, the custodian shall verify restricted access, a safe alternate contact, retention arrangements, incident handling, and the information provided to reporters about confidentiality limits. If the route fails, the custodian shall publish or directly provide a safe alternative and prevent further avoidable disclosure.}

\subsection{Capacity and access to assistance}
\req{EIP-045}{The responsible authority shall provide information on feasible assistance with language, access, or submission format and shall record unmet needs and their effect on fair consideration. A person shall not be required to expose protected information publicly to obtain assistance.}
\req{EIP-046}{A material capacity gap in impartial handling, rights competence, or protection shall be reported to the governance authority with a proposed reassignment, narrower scope, or deferral. Continued drafting within existing permission shall remain distinct from approval of the affected use or finding.}

\section{Conduct and evidential integrity}
\subsection{Participation and attribution}
\req{EIP-003}{Participants acting in programme roles shall not engage in harassment, threats, retaliation, discriminatory exclusion, or coercive disclosure of protected information. Critical disagreement about sources, methods, policy, and requirements shall remain eligible for reasoned consideration.}
\req{EIP-004}{A participant presenting a factual or source-based claim shall distinguish observation, quotation, interpretation, inference, and proposal where that distinction affects review. Known material errors, fabricated evidence, or falsely attributed sources shall not be retained as support after discovery without correction or explicit disputed status.}
\req{EIP-005}{Editors shall preserve attribution of material contributions and competing positions within actual permissions and protection needs. Authorship, acknowledgement, evidential reliability, ownership, and decision authority shall remain distinct.}
\req{EIP-047}{A participant identifying a material error in their earlier contribution shall notify the responsible editor and supply the correction or known uncertainty. The editor shall assess affected arguments, decisions, and artifacts and retain an attributable correction record.}
\req{EIP-048}{A claim that a contribution was deliberately fabricated or knowingly misattributed shall be assessed through the conduct procedure. A disputed interpretation, incomplete evidence, ordinary error, or unsubstantiated report shall not by itself establish intentional misconduct.}

\subsection{AI and external processing}
\req{EIP-006}{A participant using AI assistance for a material draft, analysis, evidence synthesis, or disposition proposal shall identify the consequential assistance and the responsible human reviewer in the development record. The reviewer shall verify material sources and conclusions before relying on them.}
\req{EIP-007}{Participants shall not submit protected, confidential, or third-party material to a tool or service without an established basis for that disclosure. Uncertainty about such a basis shall be resolved before the disclosure.}
\req{EIP-049}{A consequential AI-use record shall identify the task, material source categories, transformations relied on, responsible human reviewer, verification performed, and known limits relevant to the resulting claim. Protected prompts or source contents shall not be exposed solely to demonstrate that assistance occurred.}
\req{EIP-050}{When external processing terms or data handling change materially, the responsible user shall reassess permitted disclosure and reliance before further affected use. A tool's technical availability shall not establish permission to disclose material or confidence in its output.}

\section{Interests and influence}
\subsection{Disclosure and assessment}
\req{EIP-008}{Before accepting a decision or review role and when relevant circumstances change, a participant shall disclose interests that could materially affect, or reasonably call into question, their impartial performance of that role. The disclosure shall include relevant financial, employment, funding, authorship, institutional, personal, and infrastructural-control interests.}
\req{EIP-009}{The responsible coordinator shall record a conflict assessment, its author, the affected decision, the disclosed interest, protection limits, and the selected management action. Disclosure alone shall not be treated as resolving a conflict.}
\req{EIP-010}{A person shall not determine the disposition of a complaint against themselves, act as neutral reviewer of their own decision, or independently assess a rights clearance in which they have an unresolved material interest. The alternate route shall be used.}
\req{EIP-011}{Conflict management shall identify whether disclosure with oversight, separation of roles, partial recusal, replacement, or deferral is required. A recusal shall identify the decision scope and substitute authority without suppressing relevant technical evidence from the recused contributor.}
\req{EIP-012}{The decision authority shall examine material concentration of funding, affiliation, available unpaid labor, infrastructure access, and agenda control when assessing fair consideration. If the concentration impairs review, it shall record corrective measures and reassess progression.}
\req{EIP-051}{An interest disclosure shall identify the affected role or decision, nature and duration of the interest, relevant relationships, and protection needs. The conflict assessor shall distinguish an actual conflict, a reasonably perceived conflict needing management, and an interest not material to the stated role, with reasons.}
\req{EIP-052}{Before the affected decision proceeds, the conflict assessor shall communicate the management decision to the role holder and responsible chair and record any objection. A challenged assessment involving the assessor's own interests shall be referred to the alternate or neutral route.}

\subsection{Recusal and control measures}
\req{EIP-053}{A recusal arrangement shall identify prohibited decision participation, permitted factual or technical contribution, access limitations where justified, substitute authority, duration, and reconsideration trigger. The chair shall verify that the recusal was applied and record its consequence for eligibility or quorum.}
\req{EIP-054}{If an undisclosed material interest is discovered after a decision, the governance authority shall assess the affected procedure and outcome, obtain independent consideration where needed, and record whether disclosure, renewed review, repeated decision, or another remedy is required. Retrospective disclosure shall not alone cure a defective process.}
\req{EIP-055}{Measures addressing concentrated influence shall identify the actual mechanism, such as agenda control, dependence on one host, repeated affiliations, or unequal time resources, and the proposed response. The assessment shall not treat head count alone as evidence of balanced influence or stigmatize a contributor solely for an affiliation.}

\section{Report intake and triage}
\subsection{Receipt and route}
\req{EIP-013}{The programme shall provide a reporting route to an ethics contact and an alternate independent of a reported person. Its notice shall explain receipt handling, who can access material, limits on confidentiality, possible outcomes, record retention, and the procedural-review route.}
\req{EIP-014}{A report shall receive acknowledgement, triage, status communication, and a reasoned disposition. The seven-calendar-day acknowledgement requirement in D1-035 and the 30-calendar-day initial-determination target, with reasoned extensions notified before expiry, in D1-036 shall apply to report handling without converting the report into a procedural appeal. An extension of the determination target shall not extend the acknowledgement requirement. Triage shall distinguish immediate protection needs, conduct issues, procedural appeals, substantive technical objections, and record-specific correction or access requests.}
\req{EIP-015}{A report shall not be rejected solely because its author requests protected handling or lacks a formal role. The reviewer shall record whether limited identity or unavailable evidence constrains investigation and what fair assessment remains possible.}
\req{EIP-056}{At intake, the ethics contact shall assign a stable identifier, record the reported concern and relevant programme connection, acknowledge the requested protection, identify immediate risk, and explain the next handling step. Requests for additional information shall be limited to what is needed for that step.}
\req{EIP-057}{Triage shall identify scope, required authority and competence, potential conflicts, available evidence, immediate protection needs, and whether another programme or external route is relevant. A referral shall preserve the original receipt history and explain what the programme will and will not determine.}
\req{EIP-058}{When a report contains both a technical objection and a conduct allegation, the handler shall provide linked but distinct handling records. The technical concern shall not lose consideration because the conduct allegation is unsubstantiated, and a conduct concern shall not be dismissed solely because the technical proposal is accepted.}

\subsection{Protected, anonymous, and repeated submissions}
\req{EIP-059}{The handler shall assess an anonymous or partly identified report to the extent supported by available information and record limits on verification, reply, and remedy. Identity disclosure shall not be demanded without a stated handling need and consideration of a less intrusive alternative.}
\req{EIP-060}{A repeated submission shall be linked to earlier handling and assessed for new evidence, unaddressed grounds, or changed circumstances. Limits on repetitive or abusive contact shall state the conduct basis, safe route for materially new information, scope, review date, and challenge route. Mere persistence in disagreement shall not establish abuse.}

\section{Inquiry and fair response}
\subsection{Scope and evidence plan}
\req{EIP-016}{Before an adverse conduct finding, the responsible reviewer shall provide the affected person a sufficient account of the concern and an opportunity to respond, using redaction or a protected intermediary when necessary. Where protection prevents a fair determination, that limitation shall remain explicit.}
\req{EIP-061}{Before a substantive inquiry, the appointed assessor shall state the questions, applicable programme rules, scope, evidence sources, access arrangements, opportunities to respond, intended timetable, and decision authority. A material expansion shall be notified to affected participants with an adequate response opportunity.}
\req{EIP-062}{The assessor shall distinguish direct information, testimony, contemporaneous records, inference, hearsay, and disputed interpretation where that distinction affects the finding. Relevant contrary evidence and plausible alternative explanations shall be examined; source status alone shall not determine credibility.}
\req{EIP-063}{The assessor shall document evidential gaps and the reasons material may be unavailable, including protection, loss, or lack of access. An unavailable record or a person's inability to participate shall not automatically be treated as proof of the alleged conduct or its absence.}

\subsection{Opportunity to respond and protection}
\req{EIP-064}{A response invitation shall identify the material concern and grounds sufficiently for a meaningful reply, the response route and deadline, available assistance, and the proposed decision scope. If safe disclosure cannot support a fair finding, the limitation shall prevent an unsupported adverse determination.}
\req{EIP-065}{The assessor shall avoid unnecessary repeated disclosure of sensitive experiences and shall record how protected testimony can be tested fairly through safe summaries, questions, or an authorized intermediary. A summary materially interpreted by another person shall retain that mediation and any confirmation or dispute by its source.}
\req{EIP-066}{The handler shall obtain an appropriate rights and protection assessment before disclosing case material to an external institution. A claimed obligation to disclose shall identify the competent authority, context, basis, and unresolved questions; the policy itself shall not be treated as creating that obligation.}

\section{Protective measures and determinations}
\subsection{Interim action}
\req{EIP-017}{A temporary protective measure shall identify the immediate risk, authority, limited scope, review point, and route to challenge. It shall be distinguished from a concluded misconduct finding.}
\req{EIP-067}{The authority issuing a protective measure shall identify less restrictive feasible alternatives, the effect on participation and evidence access, the person responsible for review, and the conditions for ending or modifying the measure. The D1-050 controls shall apply to temporary protective measures under this policy, including participation restrictions: independent review within seven calendar days and expiry within 30 calendar days unless the governance authority records a justified extension, next review date, and remedy plan. Continued restriction shall retain renewed reasons and an available challenge route; temporary protection shall not become a permanent sanction through elapsed time alone. These controls shall not expand the programme's substantive authority.}
\req{EIP-068}{A person subject to a protective measure shall receive its scope, safe reasons, duration or review point, and challenge route to the extent consistent with immediate protection. Any delayed notice shall be justified and reviewed rather than omitted indefinitely.}

\subsection{Findings and evidential criterion}
\req{EIP-018}{A final conduct disposition shall identify the applicable rule, assessed evidence and limits, conflicts and recusals, finding, reasons, proportionate remedy, responsible implementer, and review route. Available programme remedies include correction, facilitated participation, role reassignment, limited access, suspension, or removal under the Charter.}
\req{EIP-020}{If a fair, sufficiently independent review cannot be provided, the programme shall record that limitation, preserve appropriate protections, and defer the affected consequential determination. It shall not represent inability to investigate as proof that a report is false or established.}
\req{EIP-069}{For an internal conduct finding, the decision maker shall distinguish substantiated, not substantiated on available evidence, outside mandate, and resolved by an agreed measure without a conduct finding. A substantiated finding shall explain why the alleged conduct is more likely than not on the assessed evidence, address material contrary information and plausible alternatives, and identify limits. A numerical probability shall not be required. Where the assessment does not favor the alleged conduct over its non-occurrence, including an unresolved balance, the allegation shall not be classified as substantiated. This criterion shall not override EIP-016, EIP-020, or EIP-064 concerning fair response and sufficiently independent consideration, and shall not be represented as an external legal finding or burden of proof.}
\req{EIP-070}{The decision maker shall distinguish the conduct established, any intention or effect separately supported, the rule engaged, and the resulting programme consequence. A serious remedy shall be supported by reasons addressing severity, impact, recurrence, context, and feasible alternatives within programme authority.}
\req{EIP-071}{A disposition based on an agreed measure shall record the actual agreement, its scope, responsible parties, review point, and treatment of unresolved allegations. Agreement shall not be presumed from silence or obtained by making access to the ordinary complaint route conditional on acceptance.}

\subsection{Remedies and review}
\req{EIP-019}{The programme shall monitor agreed remedies and potential retaliation and shall publish a non-sensitive account sufficient to inspect procedural handling. Confidential material shall not be published merely to demonstrate transparency.}
\req{EIP-072}{A remedy plan shall identify required actions, owners, practical resources, dates or triggers, completion evidence, and consequences of non-performance. Correcting a record, restoring participation, changing a role, and addressing downstream effects shall be distinguished.}
\req{EIP-073}{The disposition notice shall explain the finding and safe reasons, the operative procedure, available procedural review, and the basis for any restricted evidence. A request concerning substantive reconsideration shall be distinguished from an appeal alleging procedural failure.}
\req{EIP-074}{A material new fact or demonstrated handling error shall trigger an assessment of reopening, revised remedy, or referral. The record shall retain the original determination and reasons for changed status; reopening shall not silently erase prior reliance or its consequences.}
\req{EIP-075}{Monitoring for retaliation shall identify a safe contact and review trigger without imposing unnecessary surveillance on participants. A reported adverse response shall receive separate assessment of its connection to protected participation and any independently stated reason.}

\subsection{Scoped substantive reconsideration}
\req{EIP-099}{A reporter or reported person may request substantive reconsideration through the ethics contact or non-conflicted alternate on grounds of material new evidence, an identified material error in assessment or application of the conduct criterion, or a remedy materially unsupported by the stated findings. The request shall identify the disposition, grounds, available evidence, and requested change. Mere repetition without identified grounds may receive a reasoned refusal; disagreement shall not itself constitute misconduct. The disposition notice shall explain this scoped route separately from the procedural appeal route.}
\req{EIP-100}{The ordinary filing period for substantive reconsideration shall be 30 calendar days after accessible notice of the disposition. The non-conflicted reconsideration reviewer may extend that period with recorded reasons for ineffective notice, newly available material evidence, or a previously concealed assessment defect. The ethics contact shall acknowledge receipt within seven calendar days and arrange an initial admissibility or substantive determination targeted within 30 calendar days of receipt, with reasoned extensions and revised dates notified before expiry. Silence shall not constitute rejection.}
\req{EIP-101}{The governance authority shall assign substantive reconsideration to a competent person or function that did not determine the challenged finding and has no unresolved material conflict, with a mandate identifying the issues and available programme remedies. The reviewer shall give an admissibility decision with reasons and provide affected parties an opportunity to respond to material new grounds before a consequential outcome. An admitted request shall have a notified timetable for replies and reasoned disposition; a change shall have reasons and notice before the affected target expires. If sufficiently independent competence is unavailable, EIP-020 shall govern deferral; reassessment by the original decision maker shall not be presented as independent reconsideration.}
\req{EIP-102}{The reconsideration reviewer shall identify the record examined, grounds admitted, evidence and replies considered, criterion applied, reasons, outcome, and implementation responsibility. Within the recorded mandate, the reviewer may uphold, qualify, set aside, or remit a conduct finding or remedy for renewed consideration; a remedy outside that mandate shall be referred to its authorized decision maker. The record shall preserve the earlier finding and explain any changed effect, notice, or practical action. Substantive reconsideration shall not determine external legal liability or replace access to procedural appeal under Directives Part 1.}
\req{EIP-103}{On a substantive reconsideration request, the responsible authority shall record whether an existing remedy requires a stay, modification, or continued operation within programme powers, considering protection, participation, evidence, and consequences. The decision shall have reasons, a review point, and permitted notice. Neither an automatic stay nor automatic continuation shall be inferred solely from the request. Any temporary restriction retained shall remain subject to EIP-067, and implementation of a reconsideration remedy shall be monitored under EIP-072.}

\section{Ethical impact assessment}
\subsection{Question, affected interests, and alternatives}
\req{EIP-021}{Each work item shall assess whose experiences become visible, who can challenge classifications, who bears recording and review burdens, and who gains control or access through the proposed infrastructure. Assessment shall consider relevant alternatives, evidence, and residual uncertainty.}
\req{EIP-022}{An impact assessment shall distinguish observed effects, predicted effects, value commitments, and institutional choices. It shall identify material tradeoffs involving recognition, privacy, autonomy, unequal participation, maintenance labor, ecological conditions, and future capacities where relevant to the use case.}
\req{EIP-024}{A proposal involving contribution or generativity return shall distinguish descriptive attribution, normative evaluation, and any proposed allocation of benefits or responsibilities. It shall not infer a monetary amount, entitlement, or contractual obligation solely from a generativity record.}
\req{EIP-076}{The impact reviewer shall identify the proposed use, affected interests and absent perspectives, decision context, baseline, foreseeable benefits and harms, evidence and uncertainty, and alternatives including reduced collection, restricted access, different institutional arrangements, or deferral where relevant.}
\req{EIP-077}{Where values conflict, the assessment shall state the values and affected people, reasons for the proposed priority, alternatives rejected, expected distribution of benefits and burdens, and conditions requiring reconsideration. A single aggregate score shall not replace those reasons.}
\req{EIP-078}{A participation or recognition proposal shall assess whether people can refuse optional recording, challenge an attribution, or obtain an explanation without disproportionate burden. A claimed exception shall identify its actual institutional basis and consequences rather than present coercion as technical necessity.}

\subsection{Authorization, monitoring, and intervention}
\req{EIP-023}{Before a real pilot or consequential expansion, the responsible authority shall record its purpose, actual participants and mandate, permitted material, accessible notice and challenge routes, protection measures, monitoring, stop conditions, and capacity to implement correction or repair.}
\req{EIP-025}{A material unresolved ethical or participation finding shall receive a recorded disposition before the affected publication or pilot decision. Completion of a form shall not alone establish that an impact is acceptable or a remedy effective.}
\req{EIP-079}{Before relying on mitigation to authorize a pilot or use, the responsible authority shall verify its owner, resources, operational status, and assessment method. Unimplemented mitigation shall remain a condition or limitation and shall not be counted as an achieved safeguard.}
\req{EIP-080}{Monitoring shall identify observations capable of challenging the intervention rationale, emerging burdens or exclusions, and stopping or revision triggers. Findings shall distinguish absence of observed harm from evidence of absence of harm within a sufficiently examined scope.}
\req{EIP-081}{After a material protection or ethical failure, the responsible authority shall assess immediate containment, affected people and uses, correction or repair, continued-use conditions, and lessons for requirements. Restart shall depend on recorded evidence addressing the failure, not solely on a revised description.}

\section{Contribution intake and permission}
\subsection{Artifact identity and intended uses}
\req{EIP-026}{For contributed material proposed for incorporation or distribution, the rights register shall identify the artifact and version, contributor and stated capacity, claimed rights holder, source, intended uses, asserted permission or licence, restrictions, third-party components, evidence of authority, and unresolved questions.}
\req{EIP-027}{A contribution record shall distinguish permission to examine material from permission to incorporate, edit, redistribute, sublicense, or publish it. Repository access, submission of comments, acknowledgement, or the absence of a licence notice shall not alone be treated as permission for those further uses.}
\req{EIP-028}{The programme shall keep terms for prose, schemas, software, examples, datasets, and foundation manuscripts separately identifiable. A licence applying to one artifact class shall not be presumed to cover the others.}
\req{EIP-029}{Foundation manuscripts and third-party sources shall retain their recorded source terms. Incorporating a source in the foundation manifest, citing it, extracting concepts from it, or placing it in the repository shall not be represented as relicensing the source.}
\req{EIP-082}{The contribution handler shall identify the submitted version, source and included components, contributor's stated capacity, intended examination or incorporation, and the scope of any assertion about rights. A claim of ownership or permission shall retain its source and verification status.}
\req{EIP-083}{Before incorporating material, the editor shall determine whether its intended modification, combination, publication, implementation, or retention is within the reviewed basis. New uses or components shall receive renewed clearance rather than inherit approval from an earlier narrower purpose.}

\subsection{Clearance and unresolved rights}
\req{EIP-030}{Before public distribution of an artifact, the designated rights reviewer shall record that the intended distribution and included material are covered by identified permissions or another reviewed basis. Unclear rights shall block the affected distribution, while drafting and review within established permissions may continue.}
\req{EIP-031}{Where an intended use exceeds available permission, the editor shall obtain suitable permission, replace or remove the material, narrow the use, or retain the affected artifact as restricted. The disposition shall preserve the source and reason without exposing restricted content.}
\req{EIP-032}{A change in contribution or publication terms shall identify the artifacts and versions affected, authority for the change, contributor acceptance needed, and transition treatment. A new licence shall not be represented as retrospectively covering existing contributions without an established basis.}
\req{EIP-084}{A clearance decision shall identify the artifact and exact intended use, claimed rights holder and authority evidence, applicable terms and version, third-party components, review competence, restrictions, unresolved issues, and expiry or reconsideration triggers. Clearance shall be limited to that recorded use.}
\req{EIP-085}{If a rights claim or permission dispute arises after incorporation, the rights contact shall record affected material and uses, preserve appropriate evidence, seek competent assessment, and notify the authorized release holder of any needed restriction or correction. The programme shall not declare the claim valid or invalid solely from its receipt.}
\req{EIP-086}{A contributor's request to withdraw or change permission shall be assessed against the actual terms, prior uses, downstream distributions, protection concerns, and relevant authority. The response shall distinguish feasible programme action from uses or rights outside programme control.}

\section{Terms, implementation rights, and publication}
\subsection{Selection and acceptance of terms}
\req{EIP-033}{Before offering contribution terms or choosing publication licences, the programme shall record its organizational and jurisdictional context, intended rights and obligations, artifact classes, alternatives, implementation consequences, and review by a person competent for the relevant rights questions.}
\req{EIP-034}{The programme shall make operative contribution terms available before requesting assent to them and shall retain the accepted version and scope. A record of assent shall not assert that the contributor holds rights that remain unverified.}
\req{EIP-087}{The terms-selection record shall compare rights needed for the intended artifact use, contributor interests, implementation and redistribution conditions, attribution, modified versions, translation, dependency compatibility, and maintenance. Prose, code, schemas, datasets, examples, and manuscripts shall receive distinct treatment where their requirements differ.}
\req{EIP-088}{An acceptance record shall identify who assented, their stated authority, the exact terms and covered contribution, date, and evidence of assent. A form submission or repository action shall count as assent only where the announced process clearly established that meaning and actual acceptance is evidenced.}

\subsection{Implementation-rights concerns}
\req{EIP-035}{A participant aware of a potentially material rights obstacle to implementing a proposed requirement shall report the known concern and its basis to the designated rights contact. The report shall distinguish actual knowledge, an asserted claim, and unresolved inference; it shall not be presented as a legal determination.}
\req{EIP-036}{The programme shall record a material implementation-rights concern, affected requirements, available public information, review competence needed, feasible alternatives, and its disposition before approving an implementation claim that depends on the concern.}
\req{EIP-037}{No programme participant shall make a licensing promise, patent assurance, rights waiver, or financial commitment on behalf of another person or institution without documented authority. A declaration under another organization's process shall not be represented as a programme agreement without review of its actual scope.}
\req{EIP-038}{Where implementation rights remain materially unresolved, the responsible decision authority shall narrow the claim, select a reviewed alternative, or defer the affected normative adoption. An empty concerns register shall not be described as proof that implementation is free of third-party rights.}
\req{EIP-089}{The handling of a potential essential-rights concern shall distinguish notification, factual clarification, specialist assessment, exploration of alternatives, and an authorized decision. A request for information shall not be phrased as a programme licensing commitment or as a determination of validity or essentiality.}
\req{EIP-090}{Where a technical alternative is proposed to address a rights concern, the committee shall assess whether it satisfies the affected requirement and whether it introduces other material constraints. Selecting an alternative shall not be reported as resolving all implementation rights without the corresponding evidence.}

\section{Records, oversight, and assessment}
\subsection{Custody and publication of case information}
\req{EIP-039}{Ethics, conflict, and rights records shall identify an accountable custodian, access rules, retention or review basis, correction route, and treatment of protected information. Public decision evidence shall avoid unnecessary disclosure while making authority, procedure, and unresolved limits inspectable.}
\req{EIP-091}{The custodian shall separate public disposition information from protected evidence, restrict access to accepted roles with a handling need, and retain access and disclosure decisions. Changes in custodianship shall verify transfer of restrictions and pending response or retention duties.}
\req{EIP-092}{Before releasing a case summary, the responsible handler shall assess whether combinations of facts disclose protected identity, existence, or content. The summary shall state material limits on public inspection without creating a false impression that an allegation or rights assertion was conclusively established.}

\subsection{Review and performance}
\req{EIP-040}{The programme shall review this policy with the governance review cycle and after a material complaint, rights dispute, change of legal or organizational context, or change in publication arrangements. A revision shall preserve the prior edition and unresolved cases' transition treatment.}
\req{EIP-093}{An assessment against this policy shall identify the subject, period, edition, assessor and interests, applicable EIP requirements, evidence, and results as fulfilled, not fulfilled, not assessed, or not applicable. Unperformed handling steps and blank templates shall not demonstrate fulfillment.}
\req{EIP-094}{The governance authority shall review repeated complaints, unresolved conflicts, rights-clearance delays, protection failures, unequal burdens, and remedy effectiveness using protected or aggregated information as appropriate. \GSPannex{informative}{Worked applications and unresolved choices}
\subsection{Interested expertise and neutral decision}
An implementer funds an editor who proposes an exchange feature. The editor's evidence remains relevant, while the funding and implementation interest require assessment against the decision role. Oversight or separation can preserve the contribution while preventing the editor from determining a complaint about suppression of alternatives. The record shows which capacities were retained and which decisions were reassigned.

\subsection{Protected testimony and limited finding}
A participant reports exclusion but cannot safely publish identifying details. Intake records the protection need and available evidence. A neutral assessor considers whether a safe summary permits a meaningful reply. If the material cannot be tested sufficiently for an adverse finding, the result remains limited; it is not converted into a conclusion that the account is false. Feasible access improvements can still be considered without pretending that an allegation was proved.

\subsection{Rights to read and rights to publish}
A manuscript is available to project members for background reading. An editor proposes attaching it to a public package. The contribution record distinguishes examination from redistribution. The proposed attachment awaits a reviewed basis; source interpretation and new drafting continue within actual permission. A licence selected for the new programme prose does not automatically cover the manuscript.

\subsection{Record correction and consequential repair}
A synthetic pilot corrects an inaccurate attribution after a challenge. The correction record establishes that the current account changed. A separate remedy record asks whether excluded participation, lost access, or other practical consequences were addressed. Where those effects were not examined, the report states the limitation rather than claiming that the corrected text restored the affected person's position.

\subsection{Local choices requiring review}
The internal evidential criterion for conduct findings is a programme proposal that makes decisions reviewable; it does not purport to prescribe a court's procedure. Remedies remain bounded by actual programme authority. Different organizational contexts could justify revised procedures through the governance amendment process.

The proposed criterion requires a reasoned comparison of the assessed grounds, not a compulsory probability calculation. Alternatives include a more demanding internal threshold for particular findings or leaving the criterion to case-specific choice. The present draft selects an explicit common criterion, alongside fair-response and independence conditions, so that participants can examine its application and request scoped reconsideration. The choice, its error and participation risks, and the local reconsideration periods require review before governance adoption; see \path{decisions/ADR-0005-expanded-drafts-and-source-models.md}. No finding becomes valid merely because its author asserts that the threshold was met.

\openissue{Organizational and jurisdictional context, operative contribution agreements, publication licences, patent policy, named contacts, protected infrastructure, and specialist rights-review capacity remain unresolved. The policy supplies conditions for reviewing those choices without making the commitments itself.}
\openissue{Procedural rehearsal needs to test handling burden, neutral-review availability, language and disability access, protected evidence, recusal, rights disputes, and remedy follow-up. Synthetic success does not establish capacity for a live consequential case.}
