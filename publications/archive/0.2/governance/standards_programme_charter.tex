\GSPpreface{Foreword}
This document establishes proposed governance requirements for the Generativity Standards Program. It is a Governance Working Draft prepared for review, not an adopted Charter or technical Standard. Its authority depends on the founding procedure specified herein. The foundation manuscripts supply conceptual source authority; they do not constitute a governance adoption instrument.

Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/standards_programme_charter.tex}. Intended readers are founding participants, administrators, reviewers, contributors, and affected people. Version 0.2 develops authority, participation, resources, oversight, transition, and assessment while retaining the requirement identifiers and obligations introduced in version 0.1.

This document is part of a coordinated draft governance package. Numerical development periods and ballot rules are specified in Directives Part 1. This Charter establishes the powers and accountability within which those rules operate.

\GSPpreface{Introduction}
The programme responds to instability in the connections among representations, their referents, institutional classifications, and practical trust. Its purpose is to support warranted recognition, reasoned contestation, revision of judgment and action, and preservation of consequential generative trajectories. This includes the conditions under which people can produce evidence, inspect grounds, and influence practices that affect their future possibilities.

The programme's own decisions can shape which accounts are recognized and which infrastructures become authoritative. Governance therefore forms part of the programme's substantive task. Open documents alone do not demonstrate accessible participation; a recorded vote does not establish truth; and a maintained history does not by itself repair the consequences of a mistaken decision. The requirements below assign functions, make their exercise inspectable, and provide routes to correction within the programme's actual authority.

The initial model uses individual contributions and explicitly delegated functions. It permits a small organization without presuming that named committees, independent reviewers, funding, or institutional mandates already exist. A transition to another participation model remains subject to recorded amendment.

\section{Scope}
\subsection{Subject and application}
This Charter specifies requirements for the programme's founding, mission, publication authority, participation, appointments, decision accountability, resources, records, external relations, and institutional continuity. The assessed subject is the programme's governance arrangement and its exercise during a declared review period. A work item or publication can supply evidence of that exercise without itself constituting governance adoption.


\subsection{Boundaries}
The portfolio includes recording, preservation, exchange, presentation, assessment, contestation, revision, and accountable institutional use of generativity-related records. The Charter does not settle a complete metaphysics or create authority over external legal decisions. Its requirements concern decisions and resources within the recorded programme mandate.

\section{Terms and definitions}
\subsection{Programme authority}
A person or constituted body with an expressly recorded mandate to decide specified programme matters. The term identifies the scope of decision power; it does not imply governmental authority or legal personality.

\subsection{Founding instrument}
The record of actual signatories, capacities, express commitments, accepted governance editions, effective conditions, appointments, and limits by which the programme adopts its initial governance.

\subsection{Delegation}
A bounded assignment of a function by an authority entitled to assign it, together with responsibility, limits, duration or review point, and reporting obligations.

\subsection{Affected interest}
A person, group, practice, or condition materially affected by a proposed programme activity. An identified affected interest does not imply that a particular participant represents it.

\subsection{Decision eligibility}
Recorded authority to participate in a particular formal decision under announced criteria. It is distinct from the opportunity to contribute or comment.

\subsection{Material governance change}
A change affecting authority, decision eligibility, participation rights, appeal independence, publication powers, essential resources, or the ability to maintain operative obligations.

\subsection{Reserved decision}
A decision retained by the programme decision authority rather than assigned to an editor or administrator. Its classification concerns programme accountability and does not establish a legal classification.

\subsection{Governance evidence}
Records or observed actions supporting an assessment of a governance requirement, identified by version, custodian, access conditions, and relevant limitations.

\section{Mission and programme commitments}
\subsection{Mission application}

\req{CH-006}{Programme publications shall distinguish metamodel, record ontology, epistemic representation, normative assessment, jurisprudential status, institutional governance, political economy, and technical implementation wherever a requirement or claim depends on more than one of these layers.}
\req{CH-007}{The programme shall preserve the distinction between theoretical GR concepts and scoped operational requirements. Intentional narrowing, operationalization, or departure shall identify its foundation sources, alternatives, rationale, and recorded architectural decision.}
\req{CH-008}{Programme publications and public claims shall not present technical conformance, authentic provenance, a programme decision, or a recorded institutional finding as sufficient proof of factual truth, ethical legitimacy, legal validity, or international endorsement.}
\req{CH-009}{The programme shall identify jurisdictional and institutional authority separately whenever a proposal concerns legal duties, rights, admissibility, liability, or public power. Unresolved applicability shall remain explicit.}
\req{CH-003}{Each proposed programme work item shall identify the recognition or practical problem it addresses, its intended beneficiaries and affected interests, its anticipated contribution to the mission, and the evidence by which that contribution will be evaluated.}
\req{CH-004}{Programme evaluations shall distinguish demonstrated improvements in recording, interpretation, contestation, or practical response from broader claims about restored trust, justice, or future generative capacity. A broader claim shall identify its evidence and limitations.}
\req{CH-005}{The programme shall examine its own classification, recording, decision, and publication practices as potential sources of exclusion, dependency, and concentrated authority. Findings and corrective decisions shall enter the programme's maintained issue record.}
\req{CH-043}{The programme decision authority shall maintain a current statement of objectives that links active work to the mission, identifies the practical capacities being developed, and distinguishes intended benefits from effects supported by evaluation. Each annual review shall examine whether the active portfolio still addresses those objectives.}

\subsection{Selection and review of work}
\req{CH-044}{When prioritizing work, the programme decision authority shall record the relative need, affected interests, alternatives to new standardization, resource demands, dependencies, and consequences of delay. A priority based on available funding shall identify the resulting coverage gaps and influence conditions.}
\req{CH-045}{Where evidence shows that a programme activity materially undermines participation, inspectability, contestation, or future access, the responsible authority shall examine revision, narrowing, suspension, or closure and record the chosen response and follow-up evidence. Publication volume shall not alone justify continuation.}

\section{Publication authority}
\subsection{Classes and approval boundaries}
\req{CH-010}{Each publication shall declare its class, maturity, version, responsible editor, and decision authority. Specifications, adopted Standards, policy proposals, governance documents, Technical Reports, and manuals shall retain their distinct authority.}
\req{CH-011}{The programme shall reserve the designation adopted Standard for an edition approved under its operative development procedure. A draft, schema, prototype, protocol, or technically normative Specification shall not acquire that designation through technical completion alone.}
\req{CH-012}{Standards-track work shall begin with a stated normative problem, scope, terminology, intended requirements, conformance subjects, and relevant constraints. A dependent Specification work item shall identify the reusable technical need that motivates it. Draft requirements and Specifications may co-evolve through pilots and review.}
\req{CH-013}{A policy proposal shall identify its intended institution or class of institutions, mandate assumptions, institutional alternatives, expected effects, safeguards, and evaluation approach. Its recommendations shall not amend technical conformance requirements outside the standards-development procedure.}
\req{CH-014}{Formal work-item approval shall depend on the governance launch conditions in this Charter and Directives Part 1. Preliminary source research, draft preparation, synthetic examples, and technical exploration shall retain their preliminary status while those conditions remain unmet.}

\subsection{Reserved and delegated powers}
\req{CH-046}{The programme decision authority shall reserve approval of governance amendments, creation or material alteration of committee mandates, formal work-item initiation, Standard adoption, substantive publication maintenance, and agreements creating programme commitments. A delegation of an operational step shall not transfer a reserved decision unless the operative founding or governance instrument expressly permits it.}
\req{CH-047}{An editor or repository administrator shall exercise only recorded editorial or custodial powers. Technical ability to merge, publish, assign identifiers, change access, or remove material shall not substitute for authorization of a substantive decision.}
\req{CH-048}{Before an authorized release, the release owner shall verify the applicable adoption or publication decision and the exact artifact set. If the intended public statement exceeds the recorded decision, the statement shall be corrected or referred for a new decision before release.}

\section{Participation and consideration}
\subsection{Entry and access}
\req{CH-015}{The programme shall provide a documented route for interested individuals to contribute proposals and comments and to receive reasoned dispositions. Employment, institutional sponsorship, a proprietary tool account, payment, or travel shall not be a prerequisite for submitting a relevant comment.}
\req{CH-016}{The participant register shall distinguish participation in an individual capacity from authorized institutional representation. It shall record relevant affiliations, interests, assigned roles, and any limits on a representation mandate, subject to justified protection of sensitive information.}
\req{CH-017}{For each work item, the coordinator shall identify affected interests, reviewer competence needed, known participation gaps, outreach undertaken, and feasible measures for language, disability, time-zone, connectivity, and resource barriers. Unfilled gaps shall remain visible in progression decisions.}
\req{CH-018}{The programme shall provide an asynchronous contribution route and a human-readable account of proposed substantive changes. A contribution requiring assistance with format shall be assessed for its substance after proportionate assistance or an accessible alternative has been offered.}
\req{CH-049}{The coordinator shall publish the purpose and actual availability of each contribution route, the information needed to handle a submission, access and attribution choices, and the process for obtaining assistance. Failed contact, inaccessible material, or unmet accommodation shall be recorded and referred to the responsible review chair.}

\subsection{Consideration and protection}
\req{CH-019}{Funding, institutional affiliation, authorship volume, technical access, or control of repository credentials shall not by itself confer decision rights or priority in comment disposition. The decision authority shall assess whether concentrated interests or unpaid-work burdens materially limit fair consideration.}
\req{CH-020}{Any restriction on participation shall state the conduct or process reason, its scope, duration or review trigger, responsible decision maker, and available challenge route. Restrictions shall not be imposed solely because a participant disputes a proposal or authoritative interpretation.}
\req{CH-050}{Before closing a review, the review chair shall examine recorded access failures and participation gaps, determine their materiality to the decision, and document extension, targeted outreach, alternative access, narrowed claims, or deferral as appropriate. The absence of a complaint shall not alone establish adequate access.}
\req{CH-051}{When an affected person cannot participate through the published format, the coordinator shall offer a feasible alternative or explain the resource limitation and a route to review it. An assisted submission shall preserve the contributor's intended meaning and identify substantive interpretation by the assisting person.}
\req{CH-052}{The programme shall distinguish endorsement, consultation, contribution, representation, and assent in public accounts. A participant's name, affiliation, or contribution shall not be used as evidence of support for a decision without an established basis for that attribution.}

\subsection{Decision eligibility}
\req{CH-039}{Before constituting a decision roster, the programme decision authority shall publish the proposed decision scope, nominations route, closing date, competence or affected-perspective criteria, expected review commitment, and conflict rules. Eligible individuals shall have an accepted bounded role, disclosed relevant interests, agreed applicable conduct terms, and a recorded capacity for the particular decision. Institutional representation shall require an identified mandate.}
\req{CH-040}{Selection to or exclusion from a decision roster shall have recorded reasons against the announced criteria and an available procedural challenge. The authority shall assess concentrated interests and missing perspectives before fixing the roster. A roster shall not change during a ballot except through the correction and restart rules of Directives Part 1.}
\req{CH-053}{A change in an eligible person's affiliation, capacity, or material interest shall be assessed before their next affected decision. The coordinator shall record whether the accepted role remains valid, requires a new mandate, or requires recusal or replacement under the operative procedure.}
Eligibility establishes a bounded role. It does not give priority to a participant's factual assertion or make institutional status a substitute for reasons and evidence.

\section{Organization and accountable appointments}
\subsection{Required functions}
The minimum functional arrangement comprises founding authority, programme decision authority, coordinator and records custodian, committee convenor, editors or work-item leads, independent procedural review, ethics contact and alternate, rights review, release ownership, and maintenance ownership. All actual appointments are \texttt{UNASSIGNED} unless separately recorded.

The programme decision authority corresponds to the governance authority in Directives Part 1. The coordinator and records custodian performs the Secretariat function. The neutral procedural reviewer performs the independent appeal-review function. A review chair is assigned to a particular review. Functions can be combined where competence, capacity, and conflict controls permit.

\req{CH-021}{An appointment shall identify the person, appointing authority, accepted mandate, decision limits, relevant interests, start date, term or review date, alternate or vacancy arrangement, and removal or replacement procedure. An office shall not be treated as staffed until acceptance is recorded.}
\req{CH-022}{The programme decision authority shall publish reasons for appointments, renewals, and removals at a level compatible with necessary protection. A challenged appointment or removal shall have access to neutral procedural review under Directives Part 1.}
\req{CH-023}{Where one person holds multiple functions, each decision record shall identify the capacity exercised and any resulting conflict. A person shall not conduct the neutral review of their own challenged decision.}
\req{CH-024}{The programme shall not delegate a decision to an automated system. AI-assisted drafting, summarization, analysis, or validation shall retain an accountable human role responsible for checking the resulting material and recording consequential use.}
\req{CH-025}{The programme shall not exercise a delegated function while its required responsible role is vacant or recused without an authorized substitute. Unaffected preliminary work may continue with its status declared.}

\subsection{Acceptance, performance, and replacement}
\req{CH-041}{An appointment renewal or proposed removal shall receive a recorded grounds statement and an opportunity for the affected person and relevant participants to respond before a final decision. A non-conflicted authority shall decide the matter within its mandate and provide access to neutral procedural review. Urgent temporary protective measures shall follow the ethics policy and emergency procedure, with a stated review point.}
\req{CH-054}{Before granting operational access for an appointed role, the appointing authority shall confirm the holder's accepted mandate, relevant competence or induction needs, conflict assessment, and knowledge of escalation and protection duties. Credentials shall be limited to the tasks actually assigned.}
\req{CH-055}{Each role holder shall report inability to perform a material assigned duty, loss of required access, or a conflict preventing performance to the appointing authority. The authority shall assign an authorized substitute, narrow the activity, or defer dependent decisions and record the response.}
\req{CH-056}{A role handover shall identify pending decisions, deadlines, records and access responsibilities, conflicts, accepted commitments, and unresolved risks. The outgoing and incoming custodians shall verify transferred access and the disposition of credentials; absence of an outgoing holder shall be recorded with recovery actions.}
\req{CH-057}{Role performance review shall consider completion and quality of assigned duties, handling of objections, protection of records, influence concerns, and resource limits. An adverse assessment shall identify evidence and permit a response before a final appointment action, subject to time-limited protective measures.}

\section{Founding and decision accountability}
\subsection{Founding review and express adoption}

\req{CH-001}{The programme shall identify this document as a Governance Working Draft until a founding adoption record identifies the adopting persons, their actual authority over programme resources and publications, the edition adopted, its effective date, and the scope of that authority.}
\req{CH-002}{An adoption record shall distinguish the programme's own publication authority from any claim to represent a profession, institution, community, jurisdiction, or international constituency. Each representation claim shall identify its actual mandate and limits.}
\req{CH-036}{Before founding adoption, the prospective founding signatories shall identify their actual capacities and proposed commitments, publish the exact governance package within cleared rights, identify proposed appointments and decision-eligibility criteria, and announce a founding review that applies the public-review, comment-disposition, reply, and material-change periods in draft Directives Part 1 as explicit conditions of that founding process.}
\req{CH-037}{The founding signatories shall execute an adoption instrument only after review dispositions, a governance rehearsal, accepted minimum appointments, and independent assessment of the founding process are recorded. The instrument shall identify each signatory's express agreement, scope of commitment, dissent, outstanding limits, and the exact texts adopted. A person or institution shall not be bound or represented by a signature that lacks actual authority.}
\req{CH-038}{The founding instrument shall identify the first programme decision authority, its delegated limits, appointment and succession route, and review date. Founding adoption shall not itself approve GR/NWIP-001 or any technical Standard. Subsequent formal adoption and maintenance decisions shall use the applicable ballot and review rules of operative Directives Part 1, distinguishing substantive maintenance from editorial correction and confirmation.}
\req{CH-058}{The founding review notice shall distinguish the proposed founding signatories from reviewers and commenters, identify the actual resources or functions they can commit, and explain how additional participation or an objection to authority will be considered. A claimed mandate disputed during review shall receive a reasoned disposition before reliance on that mandate.}
\req{CH-059}{Execution of the founding instrument shall identify the exact agreement accepted by each signatory, any reservation and its agreed effect, verification of assent, and satisfaction of effective conditions. An unsigned field, synthetic signature, or recorded invitation shall not be treated as assent.}

\subsection{Readiness and decision evidence}
\req{CH-026}{Each consequential progression, adoption, mandate, or appointment decision shall identify the operative procedure, authority, evidence considered, interests and recusals, alternatives, material objections, reasons, consequences, and review route.}
\req{CH-027}{A claim of consensus shall include evidence that relevant concerns were considered and that remaining substantial objections received reasoned treatment. Silence, elapsed time, a vote total, or lack of a funded alternative shall not alone establish consensus.}
\req{CH-028}{Before formal work-item initiation, the governance launch record shall identify the adopted Charter and Directives, committee mandate, ethics and contribution arrangements, public-review route, maintenance and records arrangements, accepted appointments, and an available neutral procedural review route. It shall include the results and dispositions of a governance rehearsal.}
\req{CH-029}{If neutral review is unavailable for a consequential contested decision, the programme shall defer that disputed progression or adoption decision and record the capacity gap. The programme shall continue research and drafting that do not depend on the contested authority.}
\req{CH-060}{The governance launch assessment shall classify each required capacity as available and evidenced, limited with a stated consequence, or unavailable. A rehearsal result shall be recorded separately from actual staffing, public review, permissions, and independent-review availability. Unmet mandatory launch conditions shall prevent formal work-item initiation.}
\req{CH-061}{For a decision involving multiple functions, the decision record shall identify who prepared the evidence, who reviewed it, who decided, and who implements the result. Combined capacities shall be explicit, and the record shall identify which findings depend on independent consideration.}

\subsection{Amendment and interpretation}
\req{CH-030}{An amendment to governance shall follow the change and review procedure in Directives Part 1, state its effective date and transition arrangements, and preserve the prior edition. A procedure shall not be changed retrospectively solely to validate a disputed decision.}
\req{CH-062}{A request to interpret an operative governance provision shall identify the text, affected decision, competing readings, and practical consequences. The governance authority shall publish a reasoned interpretation within its mandate; an interpretation changing authority, eligibility, obligations, or minimum safeguards shall use the amendment procedure rather than operate as an editorial clarification.}
\req{CH-063}{A governance amendment shall assess pending proceedings and identify the edition governing each transition. A pending participant's opportunity for reply or procedural review shall not be removed by an undisclosed change of rule.}

\section{Resources and infrastructure}
\subsection{Funding and expenditure authority}
\req{CH-031}{The programme shall maintain an inspectable record of material funding, in-kind support, decision-related conditions, and control of essential infrastructure. Restricted details shall have a stated reason for restriction and an accountable reviewer.}
\req{CH-032}{A funding or hosting arrangement shall not authorize a sponsor to suppress relevant criticism, require adoption of its preferred technical solution, or bypass review. Material changes in support shall trigger a capacity and continuity assessment.}
\req{CH-064}{Before accepting material support or expenditure commitments, the authorized holder shall record the purpose, amount or nature of support, actual committing authority, conditions, expected benefit, duration, and effect on independence and continuity. A contribution shall not create an undisclosed priority or publication veto.}
\req{CH-065}{The programme decision authority shall compare planned obligations with available financial, human, technical, and accessibility resources before authorizing an activity. An unfunded essential safeguard or maintenance duty shall be resolved by resourcing, scope reduction, or deferral rather than recorded as an assumed future capacity.}

\subsection{Custody and continuity}
\req{CH-066}{The coordinator shall maintain an inventory of essential repositories, identifiers, contact routes, publication locations, build resources, and protected record stores, with accountable custodians, actual access, recovery arrangements, and dependencies on external providers. Sensitive recovery information shall be protected.}
\req{CH-067}{The responsible custodian shall periodically exercise recovery of essential publication and decision records and record the recovered version, access limitations, failures, and corrective action. The review interval and extent shall reflect identified continuity risks and shall be included in the governance records schedule.}
\req{CH-068}{Before a change of hosting or custody, the governance authority shall record preservation of identifiers, versions, access restrictions, permissions, and continuity of comment and review routes. Unrecoverable losses shall be disclosed with their effect on pending decisions and publication claims.}

\section{Records and external relations}
\subsection{Inspectable institutional history}
\req{CH-033}{The records custodian shall maintain access to publication status, version history, work scopes, decision reasons, comment dispositions, and correction notices. Protection or deletion of underlying sensitive records shall retain a non-sensitive account of the action and its authority where permitted.}
\req{CH-069}{The records custodian shall distinguish operative decisions from proposals, superseded decisions, implementation reports, and unresolved questions. Corrections shall link the changed account to the reason and authority, preserving prior material subject to the applicable protection and retention decision.}
\req{CH-070}{Where a public account omits protected material, the custodian shall record the basis and scope of restriction and the availability of authorized review. The public account shall disclose its limitation without revealing protected existence, identity, or content where that disclosure would defeat protection.}

\subsection{Liaison, public statements, and agreements}
\req{CH-034}{An external liaison, pilot, endorsement, submission, or agreement shall identify who is authorized to act, its purpose and limits, and any external institution's actual assent. Participation in the programme shall not be represented as institutional endorsement.}
\req{CH-071}{An external agreement proposal shall identify the counterparty's actual mandate, authorized programme signatory, commitments and costs, control of resulting material, publication rights, protection duties, review and termination arrangements, and effects on programme independence. Unresolved authority or rights material to the agreement shall prevent execution of the affected commitment.}
\req{CH-072}{An authorized spokesperson shall distinguish the programme's adopted position from personal analysis, a committee draft, or a policy option. An inaccurate public claim of adoption, affiliation, or representation shall receive a recorded correction through the same relevant communication route where feasible.}

\section{Oversight, transition, and closure}
\subsection{Programme review}
\req{CH-035}{The programme decision authority shall review mission performance, participation gaps, concentrated influence, conflicts, unresolved appeals, review capacity, and maintenance resources at least annually with the programme account required by Directives Part 1 and after a material institutional change. The review shall record remedial actions, owners, and follow-up.}
\req{CH-073}{The annual governance review shall identify open corrective actions, repeated procedural failures, unresolved capacity gaps, resource concentration, overdue maintenance, and the practical effects of earlier remedies. The authority shall assign owners, review points, and escalation conditions for actions it retains.}
\req{CH-074}{A material governance failure shall receive an assessment of affected decisions and publications. The governance authority shall record whether renewed participation, independent review, correction, repetition of a stage, or suspension is required; correcting the administrative record shall not alone validate the original decision.}

\subsection{Transition and orderly closure}
\req{CH-075}{A transition to institutional or hybrid participation shall identify representation mandates, decision eligibility, related-entity influence, existing participant treatment, resource needs, and appeal independence, and shall undergo Charter amendment before the new model governs decisions.}
\req{CH-076}{A proposal to suspend or close programme operations shall identify the reason, actual deciding authority, current commitments, pending reviews and complaints, custodial destination of publications and protected records, rights limits, and feasible maintenance or withdrawal arrangements. The transition shall be reviewed under Directives Part 1 before permanent disposition.}
\req{CH-077}{A closure or suspension notice shall state which activities stop, which publication statuses remain operative or change, the custodian and available contact route, and unresolved matters. It shall not represent loss of operational capacity as erasure of historical decisions or as retroactive cancellation of external uses.}

\section{Assessment}
\subsection{Subject, evidence, and findings}
\req{CH-078}{A governance assessment shall identify the operative or experimentally assessed edition, review period, scope, assessor, interests, and evidence for each applicable Charter requirement. Results shall distinguish fulfilled, not fulfilled, not assessed, and not applicable, with reasons and corrective actions.}
\req{CH-079}{An assessor shall examine actual records and demonstrated routes relevant to the claim, including appointments, decision authority, access, recusal, independent review, resources, and continuity. A blank template, announced intention, or unperformed rehearsal shall not be accepted as evidence of completed performance.}

\subsection{Response and follow-up}
\req{CH-080}{The programme decision authority shall respond to material assessment findings with an action, owner, due or review date, and consequence for dependent decisions. A finding shall be closed only with recorded evidence of the action or a reasoned change of scope, while preserving any unresolved historical effect.}

\GSPannex{normative}{Minimum governance evidence records}
\subsection{Common record elements}
\req{CH-081}{Each governance evidence record shall include a stable identifier, record type, responsible actor, relevant date or interval, applicable governance edition, subject and version, evidence location or access limitation, status, related records, and correction history. Unknown information shall be explicitly identified rather than supplied by inference without attribution.}

\subsection{Authority and appointment record}
The authority and appointment record contains: actual person or body; source and scope of power; accepted capacity; reserved and delegated decisions; start and end or review conditions; interests and management; substitute or vacancy route; contact; and evidence of assent. Where public and protected versions differ, their relationship is identified through the access record.

\req{CH-082}{The appointing authority shall maintain the authority and appointment elements in this annex for each operative role and shall update the record when its holder, scope, interests, acceptance, or effective status changes.}

\subsection{Decision and review record}
The decision record contains: question; authority; input editions; evidence and alternatives; participating capacities; conflicts and recusals; material objections and replies; procedure and any ballot reference; reasons; result; conditions; implementation owner; notice; and review route. The review record adds findings, limitations, corrective actions, follow-up dates, and verified completion.

\req{CH-083}{The responsible decision maker shall ensure that a consequential decision has the decision and review elements in this annex before it is relied on for progression. Where evidence is restricted, authorized review access and a safe public account shall be identified.}

\subsection{Continuity and transition record}
The continuity record identifies essential resources, their custodians, recovery and access arrangements, test results, dependencies, and unresolved failures. The transition record identifies outgoing and incoming responsibility, transferred obligations, untransferred material, permission limits, notices, and acceptance of custody.

\req{CH-084}{The governance authority shall use the continuity and transition elements in this annex when authorizing a transfer, prolonged suspension, or closure and shall record material gaps and their disposition.}

\GSPannex{informative}{Application examples and design rationale}
\subsection{Funding concentration}
A prospective host offers free infrastructure subject to exclusive control over publication access. The funding record exposes the condition, while the authority record shows that the host has no adoption power. Review examines an alternative host, portable copies, protected records, and continuity costs. Acceptance depends on resolving the condition consistently with programme independence; the price alone does not determine the result.

\subsection{Founding capacity shortfall}
Prospective signatories complete draft charters and a synthetic appeal exercise but have no available independent reviewer. The governance assessment records completed drafting and rehearsal separately from missing operational capacity. Source inquiry and document development continue. Formal work-item initiation remains pending until accepted appointments and the actual review route meet the launch requirements.

\subsection{Changed representation}
A committee participant changes employment during a ballot. The coordinator examines whether the accepted capacity was individual or institutionally mandated and whether the new interest affects recusal or the roster. The applicable Directives determine whether correction requires a restarted ballot. The participant's earlier contribution remains attributed even if their decision capacity changes.

\subsection{Basis for the selected model}
Open individual contribution lowers initial organizational barriers but can concentrate influence among people able to contribute unpaid time. Bounded appointments, explicit affiliations, assistance routes, and review of missing perspectives address that risk without claiming universal representation. Institutional membership and hybrid chambers remain alternatives requiring demonstrated mandates and administrative capacity.

\subsection{Unresolved adoption decisions}
\openissue{Founding signatories, organizational form, actual appointments, neutral-review capacity, operating resources, protected contact routes, and operative contribution terms remain unresolved. This draft supplies the requirements against which their proposed resolution can be reviewed.}
\openissue{The minimum accessibility target, longer-term participation model, and evidence needed to assess societal effects remain subjects for governance and work-item review. The Charter does not assume that successful programme procedure demonstrates restored social trust.}
