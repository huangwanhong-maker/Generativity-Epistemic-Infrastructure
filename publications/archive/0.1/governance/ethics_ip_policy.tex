\section{Purpose, status, and distinct responsibilities}
Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/ethics_ip_policy.tex}. Intended readers are contributors, affected people, ethics and rights reviewers, and programme administrators.

This governance draft addresses conduct in programme development, conflicts affecting decisions, impacts of the infrastructure being developed, and the rights needed to receive, revise, preserve, and publish contributions. These are connected responsibilities with different review questions. Ethical acceptability, evidential adequacy, and permission to reuse material are not interchangeable.

No copyright transfer, licence, patent undertaking, contract, or institutional legal mandate is created by this draft. Operative contribution and publication terms require a recorded choice, authority to offer them, and acceptance where required. Programme-internal conduct remedies do not determine legal liability or replace external remedies.

\req{EIP-001}{The programme shall identify the adopted edition, effective scope, responsible ethics contact and alternate, protected reporting route, decision authority, and neutral-review route before treating this policy as operational. Until then it shall retain Governance Working Draft status.}

\req{EIP-002}{The programme shall assess conduct, conflicts, substantive impacts, and contribution rights through distinguishable findings. A favorable finding in one area shall not be represented as clearance in another.}

\section{Respectful participation and evidential integrity}
\req{EIP-003}{Participants acting in programme roles shall not engage in harassment, threats, retaliation, discriminatory exclusion, or coercive disclosure of protected information. Critical disagreement about sources, methods, policy, and requirements shall remain eligible for reasoned consideration.}

\req{EIP-004}{A participant presenting a factual or source-based claim shall distinguish observation, quotation, interpretation, inference, and proposal where that distinction affects review. Known material errors, fabricated evidence, or falsely attributed sources shall not be retained as support after discovery without correction or explicit disputed status.}

\req{EIP-005}{Editors shall preserve attribution of material contributions and competing positions within actual permissions and protection needs. Authorship, acknowledgement, evidential reliability, ownership, and decision authority shall remain distinct.}

\req{EIP-006}{A participant using AI assistance for a material draft, analysis, evidence synthesis, or disposition proposal shall identify the consequential assistance and the responsible human reviewer in the development record. The reviewer shall verify material sources and conclusions before relying on them.}

\req{EIP-007}{Participants shall not submit protected, confidential, or third-party material to a tool or service without an established basis for that disclosure. Uncertainty about such a basis shall be resolved before the disclosure.}

\note{The AI-use record can be proportionate: task, material sources, consequential transformations, and human checking can be sufficient without preserving every prompt or exposing protected inputs. AI use alone does not establish either error or authorship entitlement.}

\section{Interests, influence, and recusal}
\req{EIP-008}{Before accepting a decision or review role and when relevant circumstances change, a participant shall disclose interests that could materially affect, or reasonably call into question, their impartial performance of that role. The disclosure shall include relevant financial, employment, funding, authorship, institutional, personal, and infrastructural-control interests.}

\req{EIP-009}{The responsible coordinator shall record a conflict assessment, its author, the affected decision, the disclosed interest, protection limits, and the selected management action. Disclosure alone shall not be treated as resolving a conflict.}

\req{EIP-010}{A person shall not determine the disposition of a complaint against themselves, act as neutral reviewer of their own decision, or independently assess a rights clearance in which they have an unresolved material interest. The alternate route shall be used.}

\req{EIP-011}{Conflict management shall identify whether disclosure with oversight, separation of roles, partial recusal, replacement, or deferral is required. A recusal shall identify the decision scope and substitute authority without suppressing relevant technical evidence from the recused contributor.}

\req{EIP-012}{The decision authority shall examine material concentration of funding, affiliation, available unpaid labor, infrastructure access, and agenda control when assessing fair consideration. If the concentration impairs review, it shall record corrective measures and reassess progression.}

\note{Expertise and experience often arise through involvement. The aim is bounded, inspectable authority and conflict-sensitive review, not automatic exclusion of knowledgeable contributors. A public summary can state the conflict category and management action while protected details remain with a neutral reviewer.}

\section{Protected reports and fair treatment}
\req{EIP-013}{The programme shall provide a reporting route to an ethics contact and an alternate independent of a reported person. Its notice shall explain receipt handling, who can access material, limits on confidentiality, possible outcomes, record retention, and the procedural-review route.}

\req{EIP-014}{A report shall receive acknowledgement, triage, status communication, and a reasoned disposition. The acknowledgement and initial-determination targets, including notified extensions, in D1-035 and D1-036 shall apply to report handling without converting the report into a procedural appeal. Triage shall distinguish immediate protection needs, conduct issues, procedural appeals, substantive technical objections, and record-specific correction or access requests.}

\req{EIP-015}{A report shall not be rejected solely because its author requests protected handling or lacks a formal role. The reviewer shall record whether limited identity or unavailable evidence constrains investigation and what fair assessment remains possible.}

\req{EIP-016}{Before an adverse conduct finding, the responsible reviewer shall provide the affected person a sufficient account of the concern and an opportunity to respond, using redaction or a protected intermediary when necessary. Where protection prevents a fair determination, that limitation shall remain explicit.}

\req{EIP-017}{A temporary protective measure shall identify the immediate risk, authority, limited scope, review point, and route to challenge. It shall be distinguished from a concluded misconduct finding.}

\req{EIP-018}{A final conduct disposition shall identify the applicable rule, assessed evidence and limits, conflicts and recusals, finding, reasons, proportionate remedy, responsible implementer, and review route. Available programme remedies include correction, facilitated participation, role reassignment, limited access, suspension, or removal under the Charter.}

\req{EIP-019}{The programme shall monitor agreed remedies and potential retaliation and shall publish a non-sensitive account sufficient to inspect procedural handling. Confidential material shall not be published merely to demonstrate transparency.}

\req{EIP-020}{If a fair, sufficiently independent review cannot be provided, the programme shall record that limitation, preserve appropriate protections, and defer the affected consequential determination. It shall not represent inability to investigate as proof that a report is false or established.}

\section{Impacts of proposed standards and pilots}
\req{EIP-021}{Each work item shall assess whose experiences become visible, who can challenge classifications, who bears recording and review burdens, and who gains control or access through the proposed infrastructure. Assessment shall consider relevant alternatives, evidence, and residual uncertainty.}

\req{EIP-022}{An impact assessment shall distinguish observed effects, predicted effects, value commitments, and institutional choices. It shall identify material tradeoffs involving recognition, privacy, autonomy, unequal participation, maintenance labor, ecological conditions, and future capacities where relevant to the use case.}

\req{EIP-023}{Before a real pilot or consequential expansion, the responsible authority shall record its purpose, actual participants and mandate, permitted material, accessible notice and challenge routes, protection measures, monitoring, stop conditions, and capacity to implement correction or repair.}

\req{EIP-024}{A proposal involving contribution or generativity return shall distinguish descriptive attribution, normative evaluation, and any proposed allocation of benefits or responsibilities. It shall not infer a monetary amount, entitlement, or contractual obligation solely from a generativity record.}

\req{EIP-025}{A material unresolved ethical or participation finding shall receive a recorded disposition before the affected publication or pilot decision. Completion of a form shall not alone establish that an impact is acceptable or a remedy effective.}

\section{Contribution intake and rights register}
Contribution intake means receiving material for a specified purpose. Publication, redistribution, modification, retention, software implementation, and use of personal information can require different authority. A rights register makes those distinctions visible; it does not establish that an asserted permission is legally effective.

\req{EIP-026}{For contributed material proposed for incorporation or distribution, the rights register shall identify the artifact and version, contributor and stated capacity, claimed rights holder, source, intended uses, asserted permission or licence, restrictions, third-party components, evidence of authority, and unresolved questions.}

\req{EIP-027}{A contribution record shall distinguish permission to examine material from permission to incorporate, edit, redistribute, sublicense, or publish it. Repository access, submission of comments, acknowledgement, or the absence of a licence notice shall not alone be treated as permission for those further uses.}

\req{EIP-028}{The programme shall keep terms for prose, schemas, software, examples, datasets, and foundation manuscripts separately identifiable. A licence applying to one artifact class shall not be presumed to cover the others.}

\req{EIP-029}{Foundation manuscripts and third-party sources shall retain their recorded source terms. Incorporating a source in the foundation manifest, citing it, extracting concepts from it, or placing it in the repository shall not be represented as relicensing the source.}

\req{EIP-030}{Before public distribution of an artifact, the designated rights reviewer shall record that the intended distribution and included material are covered by identified permissions or another reviewed basis. Unclear rights shall block the affected distribution, while drafting and review within established permissions may continue.}

\req{EIP-031}{Where an intended use exceeds available permission, the editor shall obtain suitable permission, replace or remove the material, narrow the use, or retain the affected artifact as restricted. The disposition shall preserve the source and reason without exposing restricted content.}

\req{EIP-032}{A change in contribution or publication terms shall identify the artifacts and versions affected, authority for the change, contributor acceptance needed, and transition treatment. A new licence shall not be represented as retrospectively covering existing contributions without an established basis.}

\section{Selecting terms without presumed commitments}
The following options identify distinct review questions. They are not an operative licence offer.

\begin{description}
\item[Document prose.] Compare access to adopted text, quotation, redistribution, translation, derivative versions, attribution, and protection against misleading modified editions.
\item[Schemas and code.] Compare implementation, modification, distribution, dependency compatibility, notices, patent-related provisions, and long-term maintenance under the actual selected terms.
\item[Examples and datasets.] Examine source rights separately from privacy, confidentiality, consent, representational authority, cultural restrictions, and permitted reuse. Synthetic labels do not excuse copying protected source material.
\item[Foundation corpus.] Preserve manuscript-specific authority, authorship, and version history; choose any future distribution arrangements explicitly with the relevant rights holder.
\end{description}

\req{EIP-033}{Before offering contribution terms or choosing publication licences, the programme shall record its organizational and jurisdictional context, intended rights and obligations, artifact classes, alternatives, implementation consequences, and review by a person competent for the relevant rights questions.}

\req{EIP-034}{The programme shall make operative contribution terms available before requesting assent to them and shall retain the accepted version and scope. A record of assent shall not assert that the contributor holds rights that remain unverified.}

\section{Patent and essential-rights concerns}
This draft selects disclosure and issue handling as interim governance controls. It adopts no royalty policy, mandatory patent licence, representation of patent validity, or external standards body's assurance mechanism.

\req{EIP-035}{A participant aware of a potentially material rights obstacle to implementing a proposed requirement shall report the known concern and its basis to the designated rights contact. The report shall distinguish actual knowledge, an asserted claim, and unresolved inference; it shall not be presented as a legal determination.}

\req{EIP-036}{The programme shall record a material implementation-rights concern, affected requirements, available public information, review competence needed, feasible alternatives, and its disposition before approving an implementation claim that depends on the concern.}

\req{EIP-037}{No programme participant shall make a licensing promise, patent assurance, rights waiver, or financial commitment on behalf of another person or institution without documented authority. A declaration under another organization's process shall not be represented as a programme agreement without review of its actual scope.}

\req{EIP-038}{Where implementation rights remain materially unresolved, the responsible decision authority shall narrow the claim, select a reviewed alternative, or defer the affected normative adoption. An empty concerns register shall not be described as proof that implementation is free of third-party rights.}

\section{Records, review, and evidence}
\req{EIP-039}{Ethics, conflict, and rights records shall identify an accountable custodian, access rules, retention or review basis, correction route, and treatment of protected information. Public decision evidence shall avoid unnecessary disclosure while making authority, procedure, and unresolved limits inspectable.}

\req{EIP-040}{The programme shall review this policy with the governance review cycle and after a material complaint, rights dispute, change of legal or organizational context, or change in publication arrangements. A revision shall preserve the prior edition and unresolved cases' transition treatment.}

The adoption dossier includes tested public and protected contact routes, accepted ethics/alternate/rights roles, conflict and contribution-register examples, a synthetic recusal and complaint exercise, a rights-clearance exercise, and a report of remaining gaps. A useful rehearsal includes a contributor who disputes a draft, an editor funded by an affected implementer, and a manuscript available for reading but lacking established redistribution permission. The exercise checks separate dispositions for the objection, the conflict, and the proposed source use.

The draft selects granular contribution and clearance records over a presumed repository-wide licence. The alternatives are adoption of specified terms by artifact class or individually negotiated permissions; a future choice can combine them. Granularity permits useful drafting now while exposing exactly which public distribution or implementation claims still need a decision.
