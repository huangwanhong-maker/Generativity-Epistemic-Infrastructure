\section{Status and committee mandate}
Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/technical_committee_charter.tex}. Intended readers are committee participants, editors, domain reviewers and the programme decision authority.

This draft defines the proposed remit, responsibilities, and evidence obligations of GR/TC 1. It is a governance document; it does not constitute the committee, approve a work item, or adopt a technical Standard. The programme Charter and Directives supply decision authority and procedure.

\req{TC-001}{The committee shall become active only through a recorded mandate under the adopted programme Charter. The mandate shall identify its convenor, responsible decision authority, participating capacities, accepted work items, review resources, and mandate review date.}

\req{TC-002}{The committee shall develop coordinated recording and practical procedures that support warranted, contestable, revisable recognition and preservation of consequential generative trajectories. Each accepted work item shall state its contribution to this purpose and the limits of its expected effects.}

\req{TC-003}{Committee work shall distinguish changes in the situation under inquiry, changes in evidence and judgment, and changes in categories or methods of recognition. A technical representation connecting these histories shall preserve their distinguishable meaning.}

\section{Scope and exclusions}
The proposed scope includes source-grounded vocabulary, recording requirements, epistemic representation, evidence and interpretation, contestation, revision, accountable institutional use, information models, exchange, presentation, preservation, access, and conformance assessment.

\req{TC-004}{The committee shall retain the distinctions between theoretical metamodel, operational record ontology, epistemic representation, normative assessment, jurisprudential status, institutional governance, political economy, and implementation when defining scopes or allocating work.}

\req{TC-005}{The committee shall not claim authority to certify substantive historical truth, resolve a complete metaphysics, determine legal rights across jurisdictions, endorse a political choice on technical grounds alone, or mandate unrestricted disclosure of recorded material.}

\req{TC-006}{The committee shall preserve existing publication identifiers when refining scopes. A proposal to change a publication's class, merge or divide its scope, or introduce a new identifier shall record the reason, dependencies, alternatives, and authorizing decision before the change is treated as operative.}

\note{Protocols describe actors, interactions, states, transitions, outcomes, and failures within a Specification or adopted Standard. Protocol is not a separate publication-authority class. The committee can investigate category-theoretic semantics in Technical Reports without making them a user-facing ontology.}

\section{Work-item authorization and coordination}
\req{TC-007}{A work-item proposal shall identify purpose, scope and exclusions, conformance subjects, affected interests, foundation sources and tensions, existing alternatives, related publications, anticipated Specifications, reviewer competence, practical evidence, and maintenance ownership.}

\req{TC-008}{The committee shall record work-item dependencies and allocate requirements to their responsible publication before producing a normative technical binding. A shared requirement shall have one authoritative location, with versioned references from dependent work.}

\req{TC-009}{Editors shall begin with the normative or procedural need and create a Specification proposal when reusable infrastructure is required to satisfy or assess it. A prototype shall not silently determine a theoretical definition or institutional obligation.}

\req{TC-010}{A proposed substantive scope expansion shall return to the responsible decision authority with its consequences for participation, sources, dependencies, review, and resources. Work outside the authorized scope shall remain exploratory pending a decision.}

\note{GR/NWIP-001, Generativity Recording and Presentation Infrastructure, is the candidate first coordinated work item. Its current draft does not itself authorize formal committee drafting. Preliminary drafts and synthetic exercises provide evidence for that decision.}

\section{Functions, work groups, and capacity}
The proposed committee convenor, editors, domain reviewers, affected-participant contacts, implementation reviewers, and maintenance owners are \textbf{UNASSIGNED}. A single coordinated group can initially perform several tasks, subject to declared competence and conflicts. The six working groups named in the historical outline remain possible allocations; their names do not establish active bodies.

\req{TC-011}{For each active deliverable, the committee shall record an accepted editor or lead, a maintenance contact, required review competence, available reviewers, and unfilled roles. Multiple roles held by one person shall be declared.}

\req{TC-012}{A new working group shall have a bounded scope, actual willing participants, an accountable lead, dependencies, intended outputs, and review or closure conditions. Its formation shall not imply autonomous adoption authority.}

\req{TC-013}{A reviewer shall identify the domain and scope of their assessment, relevant competence or situated knowledge, interests, material limitations, and whether they contributed to the material reviewed. A self-review shall not be labelled independent.}

\req{TC-014}{Where required expertise or affected perspectives are unavailable, the committee shall record the gap and its material effect on progression. It shall narrow claims, seek additional review, or defer the affected decision when the gap prevents a justified assessment.}

\section{Conceptual sources and operational decisions}
\req{TC-015}{Before introducing or materially changing a GR definition, notation, ontology class, or compatibility claim, the responsible editor shall examine the relevant foundation manuscripts and record exact source locations, terminology, scope, historical differences, and the proposed operational translation.}

\req{TC-016}{The committee shall preserve unresolved foundation differences rather than harmonizing them silently. A material operational narrowing or departure shall be recorded through an architectural decision with alternatives, rationale, affected publications, and remaining uncertainty.}

\req{TC-017}{A candidate record-class definition shall identify its definition, admissibility conditions, identity criterion, temporal behavior, permitted relations, required and optional metadata, examples, counterexamples, and unresolved edge cases, or explicitly identify which elements remain under development.}

\req{TC-018}{An external compatibility or reuse proposal shall identify the exact source and edition, verified relevant provisions, mapping assumptions, exclusions, and evidence needed to support the claimed relationship. Similar terminology shall not alone establish equivalence.}

\section{Multidisciplinary review}
\req{TC-019}{At work-item scoping, before a real pilot, and before publication progression, the committee shall maintain a review dossier covering applicable epistemic, ethical, jurisprudential, institutional, political-economic, archival, privacy/security, accessibility, and technical questions. Each domain shall have findings or an explicit justified statement of non-applicability or unassessed scope.}

\req{TC-020}{The review dossier shall identify evidence, reviewers and their capacities, affected perspectives, conflicts, alternative designs, findings, dispositions, owners of corrective action, and residual limitations. A metadata field or completed checklist shall not by itself count as domain assessment.}

The following review questions guide implementation of TC-019. They are informative prompts, not additional undeclared conformance requirements.

\begin{description}
\item[Epistemic.] Can a reader distinguish a trace, observation, claim, inference, counterevidence, institutional finding, and a changed method? Are uncertainty and absent or restricted evidence intelligible?
\item[Ethical.] Who is exposed or excluded, who bears recording and contestation work, which values conflict, and what refusal, correction, or repair is feasible?
\item[Jurisprudential.] Which forum, rule, authority, and procedural status are asserted? Is the text descriptive analysis, a model rule, a policy option, or an implemented institutional procedure?
\item[Institutional and policy.] Who can decide, give reasons, reconsider, implement a remedy, and monitor consequences? Which mandate and policy alternatives remain open?
\item[Political economy.] Who controls evidence, categories, infrastructure, access, and migration? How do costs, contributions, dependency, and benefits differ among participants?
\item[Archival and protection.] What is preserved, omitted, restricted, revised, transferred, or destroyed? Can a later user understand an authorized historical view without exposing protected material?
\item[Technical and human use.] Can independent users implement the required behavior and understand the presentation? Are failures, conflicts, unsupported features, accessibility, and feasible human alternatives addressed?
\end{description}

\req{TC-021}{A legal or institutional profile shall state its jurisdiction or forum, target action, relevant sources and status, claimed authority, temporal scope, unresolved conflicts, and reviewing competence. Generic technical conformance shall not replace assessment of actual applicability.}

\req{TC-022}{A policy proposal arising from committee work shall follow the separate policy-publication route and compare plausible institutional options, including existing infrastructure or deferral when relevant. The committee shall not present participation in technical review as assent to a policy recommendation.}

\section{Pilots, assessment, and failure evidence}
\req{TC-023}{Before a pilot begins, its record shall identify the case and baseline, questions and success criteria, participants and actual authority, material and access permissions, resource burden, anticipated harms, challenge and correction routes, stop conditions, and responsible reviewers. Synthetic cases shall be labelled synthetic.}

\req{TC-024}{The initial coordinated demonstration shall exercise a claim and its grounds, a consequential relation or contribution, an alternative or challenge, a reasoned response, a historical revision, a protected or unavailable record, and a presentation that makes their distinct status understandable.}

\req{TC-025}{Pilot findings shall report failed tasks, missing or conflicting evidence, excluded participants, unmet assumptions, and burdens alongside successful behavior. The committee shall trace each material finding to a requirement change, retained decision with reasons, further inquiry, or scope limitation.}

\req{TC-026}{A conformance proposal shall identify the assessed subject, edition, profile, applicable requirements, methods, evidence, unresolved findings, and limits of the resulting claim. Syntax checks, semantic interoperability, procedural compliance, usability, and substantive warrant shall receive distinguishable results.}

\req{TC-027}{The committee shall substantiate an interoperability claim with independent implementation or exchange evidence appropriate to the claim. A single internally consistent prototype shall be reported as such.}

\section{Progression, maintenance, and accountability}
\req{TC-028}{For each requested progression decision, the convenor shall assemble the current draft, requirement and source trace, comment dispositions, unresolved objections, multidisciplinary findings, pilot or other assessment evidence, rights-clearance status, and maintenance proposal. The programme decision authority shall apply Directives Part 1.}

\req{TC-029}{The committee shall retain a link from a corrected or superseded requirement to the prior text, reason, decision, affected dependencies, and migration consequence. Historical decisions shall remain distinguishable from the current authoritative edition.}

\req{TC-030}{The committee shall submit scope, participation, resource, and outstanding-risk information at its mandate review and when material conditions change. An inactive or unstaffed work item shall receive an explicit status and continuity disposition.}

\section{Review evidence and informative sources}
Committee readiness is assessed against accepted appointments, a scoped work item, a requirements/dependency register, a source trace, a review dossier, and a rehearsal record. Technical publication readiness remains a separate decision. A reviewer can select a material requirement and follow it from the problem and source through alternatives, draft, assessment, comments, and disposition.

The draft selects one coordinated committee with declared review functions. Alternatives include immediate formation of six standing groups or independent domain committees. The selected direction keeps shared dependencies visible and avoids claiming staffing or independence that has not been demonstrated. Additional groups remain available when actual work and capacity justify them.
