\section{Purpose, status, and application}
Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/standards_programme_charter.tex}. Intended readers are founding participants, programme administrators, reviewers and affected people.

This Charter proposes the mission, authority, participation model, and accountability of the Generativity Standards Program. It is a governance publication, not a technical Standard. Its requirements are proposed conditions for operating the programme after recorded adoption. No office, membership, delegation, institutional partnership, or publication authority is created by this draft.

\req{CH-001}{The programme shall identify this document as a Governance Working Draft until a founding adoption record identifies the adopting persons, their actual authority over programme resources and publications, the edition adopted, its effective date, and the scope of that authority.}

\req{CH-002}{An adoption record shall distinguish the programme's own publication authority from any claim to represent a profession, institution, community, jurisdiction, or international constituency. Each representation claim shall identify its actual mandate and limits.}

\note{The proposed launch model is open individual participation with appointed, accountable decision functions. It does not presume national delegation, institutional membership, international recognition, accreditation, or a legally incorporated body. The older Markdown charter remains a historical outline.}

\section{Mission and public purpose}
The programme develops epistemic and practical infrastructure for warranted recognition, assessment and revision of factual claims, and preservation of consequential generative trajectories. Its motivation is symbolic crisis: instability among representations, their referents, institutional classifications, and practical trust. The purpose includes the conditions under which people can examine grounds, challenge interpretations, revise action, and preserve or reopen future possibilities.

Generativity records can concern what happened, how it became knowable, and how categories and decisions affected later relations. Preserving an output alone can omit contributions, alternatives, constraints, losses, and changes in capacity. More records, stronger authentication, and institutional acceptance do not themselves establish truth, justice, or beneficial generativity.

\req{CH-003}{Each proposed programme work item shall identify the recognition or practical problem it addresses, its intended beneficiaries and affected interests, its anticipated contribution to the mission, and the evidence by which that contribution will be evaluated.}

\req{CH-004}{Programme evaluations shall distinguish demonstrated improvements in recording, interpretation, contestation, or practical response from broader claims about restored trust, justice, or future generative capacity. A broader claim shall identify its evidence and limitations.}

\req{CH-005}{The programme shall examine its own classification, recording, decision, and publication practices as potential sources of exclusion, dependency, and concentrated authority. Findings and corrective decisions shall enter the programme's maintained issue record.}

\section{Scope and boundaries}
The portfolio covers recording, preservation, exchange, presentation, assessment, contestation, revision, and accountable institutional use of generativity-related records. It includes conceptual work, practical procedures, technical implementation, and distinct policy inquiry.

\req{CH-006}{Programme publications shall distinguish metamodel, record ontology, epistemic representation, normative assessment, jurisprudential status, institutional governance, political economy, and technical implementation wherever a requirement or claim depends on more than one of these layers.}

\req{CH-007}{The programme shall preserve the distinction between theoretical GR concepts and scoped operational requirements. Intentional narrowing, operationalization, or departure shall identify its foundation sources, alternatives, rationale, and recorded architectural decision.}

\req{CH-008}{Programme publications and public claims shall not present technical conformance, authentic provenance, a programme decision, or a recorded institutional finding as sufficient proof of factual truth, ethical legitimacy, legal validity, or international endorsement.}

\req{CH-009}{The programme shall identify jurisdictional and institutional authority separately whenever a proposal concerns legal duties, rights, admissibility, liability, or public power. Unresolved applicability shall remain explicit.}

\note{The six candidate record classes are operational recording concepts. This Charter neither declares them exhaustive constituents of reality nor selects a compulsory mathematical semantics or software platform. Legal and policy research can proceed without pretending that programme publications enact law.}

\section{Publication authority and work sequence}
\req{CH-010}{Each publication shall declare its class, maturity, version, responsible editor, and decision authority. Specifications, adopted Standards, policy proposals, governance documents, Technical Reports, and manuals shall retain their distinct authority.}

\req{CH-011}{The programme shall reserve the designation adopted Standard for an edition approved under its operative development procedure. A draft, schema, prototype, protocol, or technically normative Specification shall not acquire that designation through technical completion alone.}

\req{CH-012}{Standards-track work shall begin with a stated normative problem, scope, terminology, intended requirements, conformance subjects, and relevant constraints. A dependent Specification work item shall identify the reusable technical need that motivates it. Draft requirements and Specifications may co-evolve through pilots and review.}

\req{CH-013}{A policy proposal shall identify its intended institution or class of institutions, mandate assumptions, institutional alternatives, expected effects, safeguards, and evaluation approach. Its recommendations shall not amend technical conformance requirements outside the standards-development procedure.}

\req{CH-014}{Formal work-item approval shall depend on the governance launch conditions in this Charter and Directives Part 1. Preliminary source research, draft preparation, synthetic examples, and technical exploration shall retain their preliminary status while those conditions remain unmet.}

\section{Participation and fair consideration}
\req{CH-015}{The programme shall provide a documented route for interested individuals to contribute proposals and comments and to receive reasoned dispositions. Employment, institutional sponsorship, a proprietary tool account, payment, or travel shall not be a prerequisite for submitting a relevant comment.}

\req{CH-016}{The participant register shall distinguish participation in an individual capacity from authorized institutional representation. It shall record relevant affiliations, interests, assigned roles, and any limits on a representation mandate, subject to justified protection of sensitive information.}

\req{CH-017}{For each work item, the coordinator shall identify affected interests, reviewer competence needed, known participation gaps, outreach undertaken, and feasible measures for language, disability, time-zone, connectivity, and resource barriers. Unfilled gaps shall remain visible in progression decisions.}

\req{CH-018}{The programme shall provide an asynchronous contribution route and a human-readable account of proposed substantive changes. A contribution requiring assistance with format shall be assessed for its substance after proportionate assistance or an accessible alternative has been offered.}

\req{CH-019}{Funding, institutional affiliation, authorship volume, technical access, or control of repository credentials shall not by itself confer decision rights or priority in comment disposition. The decision authority shall assess whether concentrated interests or unpaid-work burdens materially limit fair consideration.}

\req{CH-020}{Any restriction on participation shall state the conduct or process reason, its scope, duration or review trigger, responsible decision maker, and available challenge route. Restrictions shall not be imposed solely because a participant disputes a proposal or authoritative interpretation.}

\note{Participation does not imply agreement, authorship, ownership, or authority to speak for others. Consultation with one participant does not establish consent of an entire community. Funded support and declared expertise can improve participation; their influence remains inspectable.}

\section{Governance functions and accountable appointments}
The following functions constitute the proposed minimum organization. They are functions to staff, not assertions that bodies already exist.

\begin{description}
\item[Founding authority --- UNASSIGNED.] Records the basis on which the launch package is adopted and establishes bounded appointments.
\item[Programme decision authority --- UNASSIGNED.] Authorizes scoped work, assesses progression and adoption evidence, appoints accountable officers, and maintains the programme mandate.
\item[Coordinator and records custodian --- UNASSIGNED.] Maintains notices, role and work registers, decision evidence, protected access arrangements, and continuity information.
\item[Technical committee convenor --- UNASSIGNED.] Coordinates work and multidisciplinary review within the committee mandate.
\item[Editors and work-item leads --- UNASSIGNED.] Prepare drafts, trace requirements, and resolve comments under recorded decisions.
\item[Neutral procedural reviewer --- UNASSIGNED.] Reviews challenged procedure without participation in the disputed decision or a material conflict.
\item[Ethics contact and alternate --- UNASSIGNED.] Receive protected reports and arrange conflict-sensitive consideration under the ethics policy.
\item[Maintenance owner --- UNASSIGNED.] Coordinates review, corrections, revisions, withdrawal, and continuity for assigned publications.
\end{description}

For coordination with Directives Part 1, the programme decision authority is the governance authority; the coordinator and records custodian performs the Secretariat function; and the neutral procedural reviewer performs the independent appeal-review function. A review chair is an explicitly assigned function for a particular review, not an additional standing body. The appointment record identifies the responsible release owner and maintenance owner.

\req{CH-021}{An appointment shall identify the person, appointing authority, accepted mandate, decision limits, relevant interests, start date, term or review date, alternate or vacancy arrangement, and removal or replacement procedure. An office shall not be treated as staffed until acceptance is recorded.}

\req{CH-022}{The programme decision authority shall publish reasons for appointments, renewals, and removals at a level compatible with necessary protection. A challenged appointment or removal shall have access to neutral procedural review under Directives Part 1.}

\req{CH-023}{Where one person holds multiple functions, each decision record shall identify the capacity exercised and any resulting conflict. A person shall not conduct the neutral review of their own challenged decision.}

\req{CH-024}{The programme shall not delegate a decision to an automated system. AI-assisted drafting, summarization, analysis, or validation shall retain an accountable human role responsible for checking the resulting material and recording consequential use.}

\req{CH-025}{The programme shall not exercise a delegated function while its required responsible role is vacant or recused without an authorized substitute. Unaffected preliminary work may continue with its status declared.}

\section{Decision procedure and governance launch}
Directives Part 1 specifies stages, notice and review periods, comment handling, consensus assessment, decision evidence, appeals, and maintenance. Directives Part 2 specifies drafting and publication structure. This Charter defines their mandate and does not duplicate numerical rules.

\req{CH-026}{Each consequential progression, adoption, mandate, or appointment decision shall identify the operative procedure, authority, evidence considered, interests and recusals, alternatives, material objections, reasons, consequences, and review route.}

\req{CH-027}{A claim of consensus shall include evidence that relevant concerns were considered and that remaining substantial objections received reasoned treatment. Silence, elapsed time, a vote total, or lack of a funded alternative shall not alone establish consensus.}

\req{CH-028}{Before formal work-item initiation, the governance launch record shall identify the adopted Charter and Directives, committee mandate, ethics and contribution arrangements, public-review route, maintenance and records arrangements, accepted appointments, and an available neutral procedural review route. It shall include the results and dispositions of a governance rehearsal.}

\req{CH-029}{If neutral review is unavailable for a consequential contested decision, the programme shall defer that disputed progression or adoption decision and record the capacity gap. The programme shall continue research and drafting that do not depend on the contested authority.}

\req{CH-030}{An amendment to governance shall follow the change and review procedure in Directives Part 1, state its effective date and transition arrangements, and preserve the prior edition. A procedure shall not be changed retrospectively solely to validate a disputed decision.}

\section{Resources, records, and external relations}
\req{CH-031}{The programme shall maintain an inspectable record of material funding, in-kind support, decision-related conditions, and control of essential infrastructure. Restricted details shall have a stated reason for restriction and an accountable reviewer.}

\req{CH-032}{A funding or hosting arrangement shall not authorize a sponsor to suppress relevant criticism, require adoption of its preferred technical solution, or bypass review. Material changes in support shall trigger a capacity and continuity assessment.}

\req{CH-033}{The records custodian shall maintain access to publication status, version history, work scopes, decision reasons, comment dispositions, and correction notices. Protection or deletion of underlying sensitive records shall retain a non-sensitive account of the action and its authority where permitted.}

\req{CH-034}{An external liaison, pilot, endorsement, submission, or agreement shall identify who is authorized to act, its purpose and limits, and any external institution's actual assent. Participation in the programme shall not be represented as institutional endorsement.}

\req{CH-035}{The programme decision authority shall review mission performance, participation gaps, concentrated influence, conflicts, unresolved appeals, review capacity, and maintenance resources at least annually with the programme account required by Directives Part 1 and after a material institutional change. The review shall record remedial actions, owners, and follow-up.}

\section{Founding procedure and decision eligibility}
The founding instrument records an explicit commitment by persons with actual authority over identified programme resources or publication functions. It does not derive authority from a draft claiming to be already adopted. The following procedure is the proposed condition for declaring the programme launched.

\req{CH-036}{Before founding adoption, the prospective founding signatories shall identify their actual capacities and proposed commitments, publish the exact governance package within cleared rights, identify proposed appointments and decision-eligibility criteria, and announce a founding review that applies the public-review, comment-disposition, reply, and material-change periods in draft Directives Part 1 as explicit conditions of that founding process.}

\req{CH-037}{The founding signatories shall execute an adoption instrument only after review dispositions, a governance rehearsal, accepted minimum appointments, and independent assessment of the founding process are recorded. The instrument shall identify each signatory's express agreement, scope of commitment, dissent, outstanding limits, and the exact texts adopted. A person or institution shall not be bound or represented by a signature that lacks actual authority.}

\req{CH-038}{The founding instrument shall identify the first programme decision authority, its delegated limits, appointment and succession route, and review date. Founding adoption shall not itself approve GR/NWIP-001 or any technical Standard. Subsequent formal adoption and maintenance decisions shall use the applicable ballot and review rules of operative Directives Part 1, distinguishing substantive maintenance from editorial correction and confirmation.}

\req{CH-039}{Before constituting a decision roster, the programme decision authority shall publish the proposed decision scope, nominations route, closing date, competence or affected-perspective criteria, expected review commitment, and conflict rules. Eligible individuals shall have an accepted bounded role, disclosed relevant interests, agreed applicable conduct terms, and a recorded capacity for the particular decision. Institutional representation shall require an identified mandate.}

\req{CH-040}{Selection to or exclusion from a decision roster shall have recorded reasons against the announced criteria and an available procedural challenge. The authority shall assess concentrated interests and missing perspectives before fixing the roster. A roster shall not change during a ballot except through the correction and restart rules of Directives Part 1.}

\req{CH-041}{An appointment renewal or proposed removal shall receive a recorded grounds statement and an opportunity for the affected person and relevant participants to respond before a final decision. A non-conflicted authority shall decide the matter within its mandate and provide access to neutral procedural review. Urgent temporary protective measures shall follow the ethics policy and emergency procedure, with a stated review point.}

\note{The founding instrument uses express agreement by the actual signatories because it establishes their own programme commitments. Later technical adoption uses the separately proposed ballot rules. The requirement for review and available independent capacity prevents the initial signature from being represented as an already completed standards process.}

\section{Evidence for adoption and continuing review}
The adoption dossier provides a clause-to-evidence index. For CH-001--CH-002, CH-021--CH-030, and CH-036--CH-041, evidence includes the founding instrument, accepted role register, decision records, rehearsal record, and neutral-review arrangement. For CH-015--CH-020 and CH-031--CH-035, it includes tested contribution routes, participation and funding records, protection rules, maintenance arrangements, and documented gaps. Mission and scope clauses are checked against an actual proposed work item and its intended claims.

\note{A paper declaration of independence or accessibility is insufficient evidence that a reviewer is available or a route works. Rehearsal can expose these defects without asserting that an external institution has participated. Adoption establishes local governance authority only within the recorded mandate.}

The selected draft direction uses individual contribution and bounded appointments, with separation of authoring, decision authority, and neutral review. Alternatives include institutional membership or a hybrid representative model. The launch choice reduces administrative assumptions while leaving representation and concentrated influence visible. Transition to a different membership model needs a recorded Charter revision; it does not occur through informal growth.
