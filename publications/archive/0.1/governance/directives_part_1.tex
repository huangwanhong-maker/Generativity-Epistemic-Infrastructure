\section{Status, scope, and authority}
This document proposes an executable development procedure for the Generativity Standards Program. Its clauses govern the programme only after explicit governance adoption. It is a \textbf{PROVISIONAL Governance Working Draft}, not an adopted Standard, an approved project, or evidence that a committee exists. Its numbered requirements are proposed requirements. A rehearsal can test them without making them operative.

Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{governance/directives_part_1.tex}. Intended readers are founding participants, procedural administrators, editors, reviewers, affected people, and prospective implementers of the development procedure. The assessed subject is a particular project-development or maintenance procedure and its records, not the substantive truth of a resulting Standard.

The procedure supports inspectable, revisable recognition and practical action by preserving grounds, alternatives, objections, decisions, and subsequent changes in standards development itself. It does not determine the truth of recorded claims, settle GR theory by vote, or confer external legal authority.

\req{D1-001}{The programme shall apply the adopted Charter for decision authority and appointments, these Directives for development procedure, Directives Part 2 for drafting, and the adopted Ethics, Conflict-of-Interest, and Contribution Rights Policy for conduct, conflicts, and rights. A conflict between these instruments shall be referred to the governance authority and resolved before a dependent decision proceeds.}
\req{D1-002}{Every procedural decision shall identify its accountable decision maker, operative authority and procedure versions, input document versions, grounds, outcome, date, and review or appeal route. Drafting, a repository merge, an ADR, or successful compilation shall not constitute governance adoption or Standard approval.}
\req{D1-003}{An organizational function without an appointed holder and recorded mandate shall remain \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Formal project initiation and adoption shall remain pending while required authority or an independent appeal route is unavailable. Preliminary research, drafting, and synthetic rehearsals may continue with explicit status labels.}

\section{Terms used in this procedure}
\begin{description}
\item[Governance authority] The body or bounded transitional authority constituted by the adopted Charter; its mandate determines which decisions it can take.
\item[Technical committee] An actually constituted committee with a bounded project mandate; not a label that itself establishes authority.
\item[Substantive change] A change that could alter scope, meaning, duties, permissions, conformance outcomes, interoperability, protection, or practical consequences.
\item[Substantive objection] A reasoned concern about technical adequacy, evidence, scope, procedure, rights, participation, harm, or feasibility relevant to the proposed decision.
\item[Consensus assessment] A documented assessment of participation, consideration of concerns, remaining disagreement, and grounds for progression. It is distinct from a ballot tally.
\item[Eligible participant] A person assigned decision rights under the Charter and recorded for the specific decision; authorship, payment, or attendance alone does not confer those rights.
\item[Calendar day] A day counted using the closing time and time zone stated in the notice. A notice states an exact closing timestamp.
\end{description}

\section{Founding, responsibilities, and participation}
\req{D1-004}{Before formal standards-project approval, the founding adoption record shall identify the founding basis, adopted governance texts and versions, participants and capacities, decision method, dissent, transitional limits, review date, and replacement route. Governance adoption shall follow the founding procedure recorded under the Charter, rather than assume that these draft Directives have already authorized themselves.}
\req{D1-005}{The governance authority shall assign oversight, project progression, public-review administration, release, maintenance, conflict handling, and independent appeal functions. The assignment register shall identify holders, terms, delegated limits, substitutes, and contact routes. Combined roles shall be disclosed; an interested decision maker shall not determine their own procedural appeal.}
\req{D1-006}{The Secretariat shall maintain an accessible work programme and participation route. A project notice shall identify the problem, scope, current status, available documents, contribution terms, comment routes, decision roles, and intended review dates. Public comment shall not require voting membership or endorsement of the proposal.}
\req{D1-007}{The review chair shall document outreach to affected interests and relevant expertise, participation barriers, affiliations relevant to dominance, and remaining gaps. The chair shall provide an asynchronous contribution route and record requested accessibility or language accommodations and the response. Lack of response shall not be recorded as consent.}
\req{D1-008}{Before a formal decision, the chair shall confirm current conflict declarations and apply the adopted recusal procedure. The record shall identify recusals and their effect on eligibility and quorum without disclosing protected details unnecessarily. If conflicts prevent a valid decision or impartial review, progression shall be deferred.}
\note{Participant eligibility and appointment belong to the Charter; conduct and contribution permissions belong to the Ethics, Conflict-of-Interest, and Contribution Rights Policy. These Directives do not grant membership, impose a license, or establish a legal entity.}

\section{Project initiation and scope control}
\req{D1-009}{A New Work Item Proposal shall identify purpose, intended users and affected people, publication class, bounded deliverables, exclusions, proposed conformance subjects, alternatives to new standardization, source basis, dependencies, review expertise, pilot evidence, resource needs, and prospective maintenance responsibility.}
\req{D1-010}{The proposal shall explain how the work addresses warranted recognition or generative trajectory and identify epistemic assumptions, ethical impacts, legal and institutional boundaries, public-policy implications, accessibility, protection needs, and distribution of recording and implementation burdens. A claimed institutional mandate shall identify its source and limits.}
\req{D1-011}{The technical committee shall begin with the normative problem and assessment needs. A dependent Specification proposal shall identify the draft requirements requiring reusable technical infrastructure, the implementation alternatives, and intended tests. A protocol proposal shall additionally identify actors, interactions, states, transitions, outcomes, failure handling, and authority boundaries.}
\req{D1-012}{The authorized project decision maker shall record accept, revise, defer, or reject, with reasons, scope, assigned responsibilities, dependencies, and review milestones. A coordinated proposal shall retain a separate bounded scope and progression record for each deliverable. A technical dependency shall not acquire Standard status through inclusion in a proposal.}
\req{D1-013}{A proposed expansion beyond approved scope shall receive impact review and an explicit scope decision before dependent progression. A Specification intended for promotion to a Standard shall enter an explicit standards-track proposal and the applicable review stages; technical stability alone shall not authorize promotion.}

\req{D1-014}{Each progression decision shall state the current and proposed stage, evaluate the relevant evidence, identify remaining limitations, and record approval, return for revision, deferral, or closure. A stage label shall not substitute for the decision record. No Standard shall skip public review or final approval by claiming that a prototype or pilot already establishes acceptance.}
\req{D1-015}{The editor shall retain versioned drafts, a requirement-to-source and assessment map, an issue register, and substantive decision records identifying alternatives, selected direction, rationale, and affected artifacts. GR interpretation shall use relevant foundation manuscripts and preserve unresolved source differences; operational narrowing affecting architecture shall have an ADR.}
\req{D1-016}{At committee review, named reviewers shall evaluate epistemic adequacy, practical usability, ethical and distributional effects, accessibility, preservation and protection, legal/institutional boundaries, and technical feasibility within declared competence. Missing expertise or evidence shall be reported and resolved, or the scope narrowed, before unsupported requirements advance.}
\req{D1-017}{Pilot reports shall identify the tested versions, cases and negative cases, methods, participants' roles, failures, burdens, limitations, and resulting changes. Synthetic or author-operated tests shall be labelled accordingly. External institutional participation, access authority, independent validation, and legal validity shall not be inferred from a synthetic rehearsal.}

\section{Public review and comment disposition}
\req{D1-018}{Before opening public review, the chair shall freeze a uniquely identified draft and publish its scope, class, maturity, change summary, dependencies, known limitations, review questions, exact opening and closing times, submission routes, and intended treatment of commenter information. Required supporting material shall be accessible to reviewers under workable terms.}
\req{D1-019}{Public review shall remain open for at least 60 calendar days. The chair shall record outreach, access interruptions, and accommodation needs; a material access failure shall receive an extension sufficient to restore meaningful opportunity, with reasons and revised closing time published.}
\req{D1-020}{The Secretariat shall accept comments in an accessible free-text route as well as any structured template. It shall assign a stable identifier, record the affected version or topic, requested change if supplied, receipt date, and disclosure preference, and acknowledge receipt within seven calendar days. A comment shall not be rejected solely for lacking technical drafting expertise.}
\req{D1-021}{The Secretariat shall offer a protected route for justified confidential submissions and explain its access limits. The chair shall arrange competent consideration and publish a safe summary of the issue and disposition where possible. If essential grounds cannot be exposed sufficiently for fair review, the chair shall record the limitation and defer or narrow the affected decision.}
\req{D1-022}{The responsible review group shall give every in-scope comment a disposition of accepted, accepted with modification, not accepted, or deferred, with reasons and links to affected clauses or issues. Out-of-scope and duplicate comments shall retain identifiers and a reason or linked disposition. Deferral shall identify an owner, next action, and why the unresolved issue does not invalidate proposed progression.}
\req{D1-023}{The chair shall target initial dispositions within 30 calendar days after review closes. If that target cannot be met, the chair shall publish reasons, interim status, and a new target before it expires. A target extension shall not deem comments resolved. Commenters shall receive disposition notice and at least 30 calendar days to reply before final approval.}
\req{D1-024}{The chair shall assess each reply and record remaining disagreement. Late comments shall be logged with reasons for current-cycle consideration or later referral; a newly identified material safety, rights, or validity defect shall receive assessment before adoption regardless of receipt date.}
\req{D1-025}{A substantive post-review change shall undergo public re-review for at least 30 calendar days, showing the changed text, reasons, effects, and affected dependencies. The chair shall notify previous commenters and newly affected interests. A change that substantially alters the purpose, affected constituency, or conformance subject shall receive renewed scope approval and a full 60-day review.}
\req{D1-026}{Re-review shall permit comments on changed material and its consequences for unchanged clauses. Further substantive changes shall repeat the applicable review. Editorial changes shall be logged with a reason why meaning and conformance remain unchanged.}

\section{Consensus, ballots, and final approval}
\req{D1-027}{Before recommending progression, the chair shall produce a consensus assessment identifying participation and gaps, objections, attempts at resolution, evidence and alternatives considered, remaining dissent, and reasons for the recommendation. Numerical approval shall not establish substantive truth or by itself dispose of an objection.}
\req{D1-028}{A sustained substantive objection shall receive reasoned consideration and an opportunity for reply. It shall not automatically confer a veto. Unresolved defects in mandatory procedure, decision authority, rights to participate, or required evidence shall prevent progression until remedied. Other remaining disagreement shall be visible in the approval package and assessed by the authorized decision maker.}
\req{D1-029}{For adoption, substantive maintenance decisions, and other decisions assigned a formal ballot by the Charter, the Secretariat shall circulate a frozen package and proposed resolution for at least 30 calendar days. It shall state eligible participants, recusals, closing time, threshold calculation, ballot options, and challenge route. Ballots shall be approve, disapprove, or abstain; nonresponse shall be recorded separately.}
\req{D1-030}{The eligible non-recused roster shall be fixed before ballot opening. Each eligible person shall have one ballot in their declared capacity; proxies and duplicate capacities shall not count. Eligibility corrections or later recusals shall be recorded. A change materially affecting the electorate, quorum, or result shall require a new ballot; membership shall not be manipulated to obtain a desired outcome.}
\req{D1-031}{Let $N$ be the number of eligible non-recused participants, $R$ valid returned ballots including abstentions, $Y$ approvals, and $D$ disapprovals. A ballot shall meet quorum only if $R\geq\lceil2N/3\rceil$. Approval shall also require at least three substantive ballots, $Y+D\geq3$, and $Y\geq\lceil2(Y+D)/3\rceil$. Abstentions shall count for returned-ballot quorum but not the substantive-ballot minimum or approval denominator; nonresponses shall count for neither.}
\req{D1-032}{The result shall include the frozen roster count, recusals, returned ballots, approvals, disapprovals, abstentions, nonresponses, calculation, minority grounds, and decision. Failure of quorum or threshold shall return the matter for further work or deferral, without deemed approval. A substantive change during ballot shall return to the applicable review stage.}
\req{D1-033}{Final approval shall require a procedurally valid ballot, reasoned consensus assessment, completed dispositions and reply periods, resolved blocking findings, compatible normative dependencies, rights clearance, maintenance assignment, and an authorized adoption record. The Secretariat shall publish accessible decision notice, notify participants and commenters, and record when the appeal period starts. Release shall await that period and resolution of any appeal affecting adoption or publication.}
\openissue{All numerical values in this draft are local proposals requiring author and participant review before governance adoption. They are not ISO/IEEE rules. A valid three-person ballot does not itself demonstrate stakeholder breadth or independent review capacity; Annex A identifies these participation-risk choices for explicit review.}

\section{Procedural appeals and separate complaint routes}
\req{D1-034}{A participant, commenter, or person directly affected by a programme decision may lodge a procedural appeal identifying the decision, alleged procedural failure, available evidence, and requested remedy. Membership or payment shall not be a condition. The ordinary filing period shall be 30 calendar days after accessible notice of the decision; lack of effective notice or newly discovered concealed procedural failure shall permit a reasoned extension by the independent reviewer.}
\req{D1-035}{The Secretariat shall acknowledge an appeal within seven calendar days and refer it to an appointed reviewer independent of the disputed decision. The reviewer shall disclose relevant involvement and interests, consider conflict objections, and record independence. The decision's author, a materially interested participant, or a person deciding a related complaint about their own conduct shall not determine the appeal.}
\req{D1-036}{The reviewer shall state scope, admissibility reasons, record access arrangements, and timetable; provide the appellant and responsible decision maker a meaningful opportunity to reply; and issue a reasoned determination. An initial determination shall be targeted within 30 calendar days of receipt, with reasoned extensions and updated dates notified before expiry. No response or elapsed time shall constitute rejection.}
\req{D1-037}{An appeal concerning adoption, public-review exclusion, or authority shall pause the affected publication decision. Other appeals shall receive a recorded decision on whether a stay is necessary to prevent prejudice. If no independent reviewer is available, consequential contested progression shall remain pending while unaffected work continues.}
\req{D1-038}{The reviewer may uphold the process, require disclosure or correction of records, set aside a procedurally defective decision, or remit the matter for renewed participation, review, or ballot. The reviewer shall not substitute a ruling on factual truth, external legal entitlement, or technical merit for procedural review. The accountable body shall record implementation of the remedy before renewed progression.}
\req{D1-039}{Technical reconsideration, conduct complaints, record-specific access or correction requests, and external legal remedies shall have distinguishable routes. Misrouted submissions shall receive assisted referral rather than silent rejection. Protected conduct handling shall follow the adopted ethics policy; disagreement alone shall not be treated as misconduct. Internal review shall not be presented as replacing external rights or remedies.}

\section{Publication and release integrity}
\req{D1-040}{The release owner shall verify that the exact publication package implements the approved text and decision. The package shall identify edition, date, class, maturity, adoption authority, conformance scope, dependencies, licenses or permissions, known limitations, maintenance contact, next review date, and superseded publications where applicable.}
\req{D1-041}{The release manifest shall identify authoritative source files, rendered publications, schemas or fixtures where included, their versions and integrity checks, build instructions and tool versions, and the approval record. A reviewer other than the final editor shall compare the rendered output with the approved source for omitted, altered, or unreadable requirements and record the result.}
\req{D1-042}{The release owner shall resolve source/build discrepancies before release. Source corrections affecting meaning shall return to substantive review; a rebuild shall not silently change an adopted requirement. Public notices shall distinguish an adopted Standard, a Specification, an informative publication, and a policy recommendation and shall not claim ungranted endorsement or certification.}

\section{Maintenance, review, and withdrawal}
\req{D1-043}{Every release shall have an assigned maintenance owner, issue intake route, and next review date. The default review interval shall be two years, shortened by a recorded risk-based decision. Loss of ownership, serious defects, changed dependencies, inaccessible implementation rights, or evidence of material exclusion or harm shall trigger earlier review. An overdue review shall be publicly marked and escalated; silence shall not confirm continued suitability.}
\req{D1-044}{The maintenance owner shall log reported issues, classify their effect, identify affected versions and dependencies, and publish disposition or investigation status. A correction request shall not be rejected merely because the reported behavior matches the current text.}
\req{D1-045}{A corrigendum shall correct an error without introducing a new obligation, altering intended meaning, or changing a valid conformance outcome. It shall identify the affected text, corrected text, justification, reviewer, and authorization. If semantic effect is uncertain, the change shall use the amendment or revision route. Historical publications shall remain identifiable rather than silently overwritten.}
\req{D1-046}{An amendment shall make a bounded substantive change to a named edition; a revision shall issue a consolidated new edition. Both shall receive scope approval, assessment of dependent artifacts and users, appropriate pilots, committee and public review, and final approval under these Directives. An incompatible change shall include migration, coexistence, and deprecation information without claiming lossless conversion absent evidence.}
\req{D1-047}{Periodic review shall invite evidence from users and affected parties and record confirmation, correction, amendment, revision, supersession, or withdrawal with reasons. Confirmation shall require a maintenance report and an authorized decision; it shall not erase known limitations.}
\req{D1-048}{Proposed withdrawal or supersession shall receive a public notice of reasons, dependencies, transition consequences, and alternatives for at least 60 calendar days, followed by comment disposition, reply, and formal approval. Withdrawn editions shall remain identifiable with status and date; withdrawal shall not retroactively change what a past conformance claim asserted. Necessary protection of underlying records shall follow D1-052, D1-053, and EIP-039.}

\section{Emergency safeguards and continuity}
\req{D1-049}{An authorized governance or release holder may issue a time-limited warning or suspend programme distribution or programme recommendations for new use where a documented serious protection, rights, or integrity risk requires immediate action. The action shall state evidence, scope, authority, duration, contact, affected users, and safe public reasons. It shall remain within programme authority and shall not create or amend normative requirements or purport to invalidate external legal decisions.}
\req{D1-050}{Emergency action shall receive independent review within seven calendar days and expire within 30 calendar days unless the governance authority records a justified extension, next review date, and remedy plan. Unavailable independent capacity shall not authorize emergency adoption. Permanent amendment or withdrawal shall follow the ordinary procedure; protective handling of unlawfully or improperly disclosed material remains governed by the applicable policy and context.}
\req{D1-051}{A vacant role shall trigger recorded reassignment within the Charter's mandate or deferral of dependent decisions. A temporary substitute shall meet the same conflict and independence conditions. Emergency procedure shall not be used to avoid consultation because ordinary development is late or under-resourced.}

\section{Records, transparency, and external liaison}
\req{D1-052}{The Secretariat shall preserve linked proposal, draft, comment, disposition, decision, ballot-result, appeal-outcome, release, and maintenance records. Public readers shall be able to follow the grounds and changes of a decision without mandatory access to private correspondence. Record corrections shall retain the prior account and correction relationship where lawful and appropriate.}
\req{D1-053}{A records schedule shall state purpose, custodian, access category, retention period or trigger, restriction review, and authorized disposition for each record category. Public publication history and safe decision reasons shall be retained durably; sensitive submissions and personal data shall not be retained indefinitely by default. A restriction or deletion shall preserve a safe explanation when permitted, without revealing protected existence or identity.}
\req{D1-054}{The governance authority shall publish an annual account of active work, decisions, unresolved objections, participation gaps, funding influence, overdue maintenance, and procedural incidents at a level that protects affected people. The account shall distinguish claimed representation from evidenced participation.}
\req{D1-055}{External liaison shall have an explicit mandate identifying purpose, authorized speaker, commitments permitted, and reporting duties. A policy proposal shall identify its intended institution and review route and remain recommendatory. Liaison, use of external sources, and informal participation shall not imply affiliation, institutional endorsement, or adoption of another body's procedures.}

\subsection{Publication routes outside Standard adoption}
\req{D1-056}{Before external release of a Specification or informative publication, the responsible authority shall approve a work-item review plan identifying publication class, maturity criteria, release authority, reviewers and needed competence, public-comment arrangements and period with rationale, objection disposition, appeal route, maintenance responsibility and rights clearance. The release record shall identify fulfillment and remaining limitations. Drafting alone shall not constitute that approval.}
\req{D1-057}{A Specification release containing technical conformance requirements shall use the technical review, public review, disposition, ballot and appeal rules in this Part, while retaining Specification status and its declared maturity. Its release shall not use stage 60 or imply Standard adoption. Promotion shall require an explicit Standard work-item and adoption decision with review of changed scope, requirements and prior evidence.}
\req{D1-058}{A Policy or Institutional Proposal shall receive a separate mandate-specific release decision identifying its intended addressee, institutional and affected-interest review, public-comment opportunity, considered alternatives and unresolved objections. Its review plan shall preserve a reasoned disposition and the procedural appeal route in this Part. An informative report or manual shall likewise identify its review and release decision without claiming technical Standard conformance authority.}

\section{Governance rehearsal evidence (informative)}
A useful rehearsal records actors as fictional or unassigned, uses identified draft versions, and leaves a complete decision trail. It is not actual public review, proof of representativeness, or governance adoption.

Assessment uses an evidence register with a row for each applicable D1 identifier, the procedure or decision assessed, required record location, reviewer, result (fulfilled, not fulfilled, not assessed, or not applicable), and unresolved action. A not-applicable result identifies the absent trigger. Document inspection assesses notices, disclosures, reasons, and manifests; arithmetic recalculation assesses ballot results; observed exercises assess intake, recusal, reply, appeal, and restoration behavior. Independent procedural review assesses contested fairness and authority. Missing records or unperformed procedures are not counted as fulfillment.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Process a bounded NWIP through accept, revise, and defer alternatives. Identify an infrastructure need without presuming a Specification or Standard already exists.
\item Receive an accessible free-text comment, a protected submission, and a sustained minority objection. Show acknowledgement, reasoned disposition, reply, and safe publication.
\item Re-review a material change and demonstrate why a change of constituency restarts full review. Preserve unsuccessful alternatives and source uncertainty.
\item Recalculate a ballot after recusal: with five eligible people, four returns meet quorum; two approvals, one disapproval, and one abstention satisfy the approval threshold. Repeat with two returns and show deferral.
\item Test three returns consisting of one approval and two abstentions. Record failure of the three-substantive-vote minimum even though returned-ballot quorum can be met.
\item Appeal exclusion from review; document independent appointment, conflict challenge, stay, reply, remedy, and renewed decision. Repeat with no independent reviewer and show pending progression.
\item Compare approved source and rendered output, detect an omitted requirement, and prevent release. Distinguish typographic correction from a changed conformance result.
\item Simulate maintenance, incompatible revision, withdrawal, an urgent warning, and role vacancy. Demonstrate that an emergency cannot adopt a Standard.
\end{enumerate}
The rehearsal report records failures, required staffing, elapsed effort, participation and confidentiality barriers, revised clauses, and remaining questions before the founding participants assess readiness.
