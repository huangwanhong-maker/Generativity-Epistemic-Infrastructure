\section{Scope and standing}
Responsible editor: \texttt{UNASSIGNED}. Canonical source: \path{standards/GR-110-recording-requirements/GR-110.tex}. Intended readers are recording practitioners, record custodians, affected participants and assessors.

This document proposes requirements for a bounded generativity record package and for the practice that produces and maintains it. A package is an identified collection assembled for a stated inquiry or practical purpose, with an inventory, scope, responsible recording practice, attributed claims and a history relevant to that purpose.

The objective is to preserve enough information to examine what is represented, how it became known, how judgments changed and how earlier relations contributed to later capacities, constraints or possibilities. Completeness of reality, exhaustive personal surveillance and a uniquely recoverable history are outside scope.

This is preparatory authoring, not a formally initiated or adopted Standard. Proposed requirements are available for synthetic assessment while programme governance is developed. Full GR-120/130/140/150/160/400 documents and a common technical schema are not prerequisites for this draft's manual assessment.

\section{Draft dependencies and terminology}
For the bounded assessment in this edition, GR-100 Working Draft 0.1 supplies operational terms and GR-200 Working Draft 0.1 supplies the epistemic requirements invoked below. Both dependencies are drafts, not adopted Standards. A change to either dependency requires review of affected results.

A \emph{material claim} in this document is an assertion relied on to explain a consequential relation, generative effect, decision, classification, assessment or revision within the declared purpose. The package's selection rationale identifies such reliance. Importance is purpose-relative; the label does not establish truth.

A \emph{recording practice} comprises the responsible people or organization, methods and custody arrangements used to assemble and maintain a package. Technical capture can be one component. A \emph{generative account} is an attributed account of how conditions or relations enabled, maintained, transformed or constrained subsequent capacities, relations or possibilities. It remains subject to evidential assessment.

\section{Purpose, boundary and responsible practice}
\req{R110-001}{A package shall identify its purpose, intended users, subject matter, boundary, relevant time horizon, recording practice and package version.}
\req{R110-002}{The recording practice shall state the selection criteria for included material and identify known omissions material to the purpose, including unavailable perspectives, uncollected information and limits of reconstruction.}
\req{R110-003}{The package shall distinguish an inventory of retained records from an assertion that the underlying history is complete. The existence of a record shall not be used as proof of its represented content.}
\req{R110-004}{The recording practice shall assign responsibility for capture, assessment, custody, access decisions and correction, recording combined roles and relevant conflicts. An automated component shall not substitute for an unassigned responsibility.}

\section{Epistemic and identity requirements}
\req{R110-005}{The package shall identify its material claims and assessments as a bounded epistemic record package assessed against GR-200 Working Draft 0.1, including that draft's package-level obligations. It shall identify the assessed claim set, dependency versions, applicable requirements, evidence, exceptions and results; a claim-level check alone shall not establish fulfillment of the package dependency.}
\req{R110-006}{Each retained record and version shall be distinguishable within the package, and a reference shall identify its intended target sufficiently for the declared use. A record identifier shall not itself establish identity of the represented person, event or relation.}
\req{R110-007}{A continuity or identity judgment material to the trajectory shall identify its criterion, responsible source, supporting grounds and known dispute. Merger, succession and common labels shall not silently be treated as identity.}
\req{R110-008}{The package shall distinguish the represented occurrence or validity time from observation, recording and later assessment times when their difference is relevant. Uncertain intervals and unsupported ordering shall remain explicit.}
\req{R110-009}{Represented observations, actions, relations, states and properties shall retain the information needed to distinguish their operational roles. A relation with a material history, status or properties of its own shall be identifiable as a recordable subject.}
\note{These provisions do not mandate a database type hierarchy. A source, a claim about it and an assessment can be represented in different technical forms if the distinctions survive the declared uses. Property remains programme terminology pending the Attribute/Property decision.}

\section{Recording generative trajectories}
\subsection{Conditions, contributions and consequences}
\req{R110-010}{For each generative account, the package shall identify the target capacity, relation, constraint, distinction or possibility; the relevant actors or configuration; the comparison or earlier condition; and the temporal and contextual scope.}
\req{R110-011}{The account shall state how the proposed enabling, maintaining, transforming or constraining contribution connects earlier conditions to the target, with grounds, method and limitations. Temporal precedence or a diagram edge alone shall not be presented as establishing that connection.}
\req{R110-012}{An account shall distinguish the asserted occurrence or modality of an effect, such as realized, intended, possible, unrealized or disputed, from its epistemic basis, such as observed, reported, inferred or reconstructed. A realized past effect can be retrospectively inferred. The recording practice shall not manufacture an observed outcome where follow-up evidence is absent.}
\req{R110-013}{Material participants and contributions shall be attributed with their basis and uncertainty. The recording practice shall provide a means to record disputed, collective, indirect or unknown contribution without forcing attribution to one actor.}
\req{R110-014}{When an enabling encounter, decision or judgment is material to the account, the package shall identify its represented role, relevant conditions and the claim connecting it to subsequent developments.}

\subsection{Alternatives, changes in distinctions and loss}
\req{R110-015}{A recorded alternative shall state whether it was considered at the time, inferred retrospectively, modeled or merely proposed, and identify the grounds for any claim of feasibility. Recorded alternatives shall not be presented as exhaustive or equally feasible without supporting grounds.}
\req{R110-016}{A material change in categories, available distinctions or methods shall identify the earlier and later formulation, the reason for change and its effect on interpretation. A new description shall not automatically be represented as a newly existing underlying object.}
\req{R110-017}{Known maintenance, depletion, restriction, exclusion or loss material to the declared generative account shall be recorded alongside creation or expansion, or have an explicit collection or protection limitation under R110-002 and R110-026. The account shall distinguish descriptive effects from their evaluation for particular people or values.}
\req{R110-018}{Where generativity return is assessed or proposed, the package shall identify the asserted concept, intended recipients, scope, grounds and evaluative basis. Where it is not assessed, that limitation shall be stated without inventing a quantity or entitlement.}
\note{A stable laboratory collaboration can maintain capabilities despite little immediate output. A successful output can coexist with the loss of a contributor's access. Neither observation alone establishes whether the arrangement was just.}

\section{Decisions, practical uptake and revision}
\req{R110-019}{When a represented claim or classification informs a decision within scope, the package shall identify the claim version relied on, the decision, the asserted decision authority, its stated grounds and known subsequent response.}
\req{R110-020}{A revision shall identify its actor or responsible source, time, target, reason and affected records, distinguishing a change in the represented situation, evidence or interpretation, and descriptive regime. More than one reason can apply.}
\req{R110-021}{The recording practice shall preserve the intelligible relationship between earlier and later accounts, subject to stated retention and access rules. It shall not retrospectively present earlier actors as possessing evidence or categories first available later.}
\req{R110-022}{A correction shall identify dependent records or presentations known to the practice, actions taken to correct them and any limits on propagation. The practice shall not assert that uncontrolled external copies were corrected or deleted without evidence.}
\req{R110-023}{The practice shall provide a declared route for questions, contested attribution and correction requests, identify the responsible recipient and maintain permitted request/disposition records. Availability of the route shall not imply a legal entitlement or a promise of agreement.}
\note{GR-210 Working Draft 0.1 supplies a complementary procedure for receiving, examining and disposing of such cases. It is not made a blanket dependency of every record package: a claim about a GR-210 procedure needs its own assessment.}

\section{Protection, custody and absence}
\req{R110-024}{The recording practice shall declare the purpose and authority basis for collecting, retaining, accessing and disclosing material within the package, including the applicable policy or institutional context and unresolved constraints. It shall limit collected detail to that justified by the declared purpose.}
\req{R110-025}{Access and retention decisions shall distinguish content, metadata, derived views and audit traces. Preservation of history shall not automatically require public disclosure or indefinite retention of sensitive material.}
\req{R110-026}{Where material is unavailable, the package shall distinguish known non-collection, loss, destruction, restriction and an unknown reason to the extent that doing so is supported and permitted. It shall not disclose a protected fact merely to label an absence.}
\req{R110-027}{A transfer, migration or custody change performed by the practice shall identify the source and destination versions or custodians, its authority, relevant transformation and known information loss. Unsupported finer distinctions shall not be invented during conversion.}
\req{R110-028}{An authorized correction, restriction, retention or disposal action shall have a scoped disposition and an accountable executor. The practice shall record permitted evidence of its result and known limitations without retaining prohibited content solely to prove deletion.}

\section{Presentation and reviewability}
\req{R110-029}{A presentation supplied as part of the assessed practice shall identify its source package/version and disclose material selection, reconstruction, uncertainty, disagreement and restriction within the permitted audience. It shall distinguish an attributed claim, an assessment and an institutional determination.}
\req{R110-030}{A public or reduced view shall not imply that it contains the entire retained package. A validation or provenance result shall state what was checked and shall not be presented as certification of substantive truth or normative legitimacy.}
\req{R110-031}{The practice shall provide enough accessible explanation for intended users to locate the grounds of a material claim and the relevant review route. A practical assessment shall record observed comprehension barriers and the alternatives or limitations offered.}

\section{Conformance and experimental assessment}
\subsection{Separate subjects}
Two subjects are distinguished: the \emph{package} and the \emph{recording practice}. Package assessment examines the retained representation. Practice assessment additionally examines responsibility, selection, protection, action and user-facing behavior. A practice claim identifies at least one package or scenario through which behavior was examined.

This edition defines no reduced package profile. A package-only assessment reports a documentary subset and its limits; it is a partial assessment, not overall fulfillment of GR-110. An obligation to perform an action remains not assessed when only its documentation was examined. Not applicable is reserved for an absent stated trigger, not a change of assessment subject. Practice assessment covers every applicable requirement, with recording and behavioral components distinguished where a clause contains both.

\req{R110-032}{A report shall identify the assessed subject, versions of this draft and its dependencies, purpose/profile, applicable requirements, assessor, method, evidence, results and unresolved limitations. Package-only results shall not be presented as assessment of unobserved practice.}
\req{R110-033}{The report shall distinguish fulfilled, not fulfilled, not assessed and not applicable results for each requirement, with reasons for exclusions. A failed or unassessed applicable requirement shall prevent an unqualified claim that the declared scope satisfies this draft.}
\req{R110-034}{The report shall distinguish structural or documentary checks from tests of understanding, evidential warrant and institutional performance. It shall not infer truth, complete historical recovery, beneficial generativity or legal validity from structural fulfillment.}

\section{Informative example and source rationale}
The accompanying synthetic funding-decision case is in \path{examples/p1-contested-decision.md}. It includes an inference from missing calibration metadata, contrary testimony, a restricted log, a change in evidential assessment, a separate change in classification, and uncertain downstream effects. It is a design fixture, not an empirical pilot.

\source{FM-001, GRE Chapter 1, section 1.14, PDF pp.\ 78--83, distinguishes authenticity, provenance, representational adequacy and practical trust. FM-005, GRE Chapter 5, sections 5.4 and 5.14, pp.\ 28--36 and 113--122, distinguishes trace from claim-relative evidence and relational from generative trajectories. Sections 5.18--5.20, pp.\ 150--176, inform situated evidence, contestation and revision.}
\source{FM-003, GRE Chapter 3, section 3.12, pp.\ 129--141, motivates the relation among diagnosis, action, feedback and repair; FM-002 preserves a separately supplied Chapter 3 variant whose chronological relationship remains unresolved. FM-007, Metamodel Part II, sections 7--12 and 16--19, informs distinctions among identity, occurrence, process history, retained description and institutional uptake. These are conceptual sources, not normative dependencies.}

The proposed operationalization is recorded in ADR-0004. It requires attributable and scoped generative accounts rather than a universal metric. Separate histories are required where they affect interpretation; no fixed ontology of claim objects, universal clock or exhaustive branch tree is selected.
