# ADR-0001 — Separate Governance, Standards, Manuals, and Technical Reports

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The project combines procedural governance, normative standardization, practical guidance, and theoretical research.

## Decision

Maintain four primary publication layers:

1. governance documents;
2. normative standards;
3. non-normative manuals/guides;
4. technical reports.

## Rationale

This reduces category confusion and allows philosophical or mathematical work to mature without prematurely becoming normative.

## Consequences

Every substantive contribution should be placed in the appropriate publication class.
