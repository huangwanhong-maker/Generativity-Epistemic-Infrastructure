# GR-TR102 — Category-Theoretic Semantics

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL

Candidate topics:

- categories of states and transformations;
- event generators and process composition;
- spans/cospans for first-class relations;
- profunctors and relational semantics;
- double categories for relations and transformations;
- fibrations/indexing for persistence across contexts;
- functorial observables for properties;
- evolving discrimination/equivalence structures.

The core standards should not depend on any one of these constructions until the semantics are stable.
