## 1. Boundary, authorship and evidence status

<a id="declaration"></a>

Package `P1/v2` records an invented community water-monitoring funding case, using synthetic days −2 through 40. Its purpose is to examine whether a narrative package preserves the distinction among a missing attachment, a claim of non-calibration, an exclusion decision, challenge, evidential revision and practical consequences. The recording role is the **fixture author, a Codex assistant acting for this repository task**. The assessment role is the same assistant; this is author review, not independent review. The date of this actual documentary review is 2026-09-22. Synthetic day labels express scenario chronology, not elapsed real activity.

The collection rule includes claims C1–C7, their identified versions, material invoked for them, rule versions K1/K2, decisions D1/D2, challenge CH1, and the downstream dependencies named below. Shared context `CTX-P1/v2` consists of this section and the declared method in section 4. All those records inherit it unless a row states otherwise. The unit of inquiry is one fictional application and a claimed maintenance relation; no population-wide inference is attempted. The judgment horizon ends at day 40; the pre-submission maintenance target is day −2 or otherwise explicitly unknown. Claims about later durable environmental or social benefit fall outside the supplied evidence.

The **actual supplied evidence** is the text of this file, the historical P1/v1 text, the archived draft clauses and the linked assessment register. Every in-world actor, submission, notice, receipt, review, authority and action is fictional. The dossier supplies authored representations of them, not evidence that such procedures occurred. Text inspection can establish that a specified field or distinction is present; it cannot establish actual receipt, fair participation, competent review, enforcement, deletion or repair. Such performance remains **not assessed**.

This edition adds explicit scenario declarations, issue tracks and component assessments. These additions are authoring choices, not newly discovered historical facts. It does not fill the absent original application, original maintenance log, calibration method, instrument identity, contact details, delivery receipts or funding outcome. No real restricted material is held. The claimed protected original exists only as a scenario premise; the current file is a fully public authored demonstration of differentiated views.

The package is a **partial documentary assessment subject**. It does not claim full GR-110, GR-200 or GR-210 conformance. Its assessment register contains selected components and explicitly identified unassessed whole-requirement results. GR-200's package obligations remain applicable; nothing is removed because this is a small example. No reduced conformance profile is assumed. Neither this fixture nor its checks establish truth, legal rights, institutional legitimacy or beneficial generativity.

## 2. Scenario declaration and service schedule

<a id="procedure"></a>

`P1-PROC/v2` is a **proposed synthetic procedure**, effective for this fixture only. The fictional Fund is the operator. Secretariat records and routes submissions; Panel applies the fictional internal eligibility rule and alone can authorize funding reassessment; Reviewer B assesses the scoped evidential questions; Review Contact handles procedural reconsideration; Custodian controls the described original log. No role is an actual appointment or an available external service. The fixture supplies no jurisdiction or external legal mandate.

Covered users are the team, technician and Panel for this case. No fee is proposed. Text and assisted oral submissions, protected contact and representative submission are designed routes, not deployed channels. An issue description and target sufficient to identify a concern are the proposed minimum intake content; technical clause numbers and public identification are unnecessary. English is the authored fixture language. Translation, disability support and safety arrangements require participant needs assessment in a rehearsal; availability is not presumed. Known barriers are unequal documentation resources, possible exposure of a technician's contact details and inability to inspect a protected original. Actual experience of those barriers and adequacy of assistance have not been measured.

The following illustrative periods are local design choices. Their rationale is a small case needing prompt status communication while allowing a protected evidence request and a reply. No resource study validates their adequacy. They do not replace any external deadline. All clocks use elapsed scenario days; day of receipt is day 0 of each local clock. The proposed extension rule is one reasoned extension, authorized by Review Contact before the current due time and communicated with a new date; missed targets stay in the record. Any further extension requires a capacity review and reasoned alternative. A hold preserves pending status and receives a review point; it does not decide the merits.

| Service event | Proposed owner; start → period | Notice, pause or escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt/acknowledgement | Secretariat; receipt → 2 days | Identify case, issues and next step; failed contact recorded and protected alternative attempted if available. |
| Clarification/scoping | Secretariat; missing-information request → 3 days for reply, then review | Explain why information matters and available assistance; no automatic factual rejection on silence. |
| Urgent triage | Review Contact; identified urgent risk → 1 day | Limited temporary protection only within fictional internal authority; review after 2 days. |

| Disposition notice | Secretariat; disposition → 1 day | Identify exact notice version, recipient and known delivery status. |
| Further-review request | Review Contact; confirmed receipt of disposition → 10 days to request | Where receipt is unknown, no expiry is inferred; acknowledge within 2 days and decide scope within 5 days. |
| Further review | Review Contact; accepted further-review scope → 10 days | Conflict screening and stay consideration; unavailable independence disclosed. |

No hearing, appointment, capacity test, acknowledgement, notice transmission or clock performance was executed. The schedule's completeness is a documentary question; its feasibility and actual fulfillment remain unassessed. It deliberately exposes a resource question: an institution with only the original decision maker cannot honestly advertise independent further review.

Alternative register `ALT/v2`: **retained** explanations include calibration outside the intake channel, adjustment short of adequate calibration, and identity mismatch.
