# Specifications

**Document class:** Informative publication-track overview  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL

Specifications are technically precise publications that may be implemented or tested without necessarily having the formal status of a Standard.

See the [publication taxonomy](../docs/framework/publication_taxonomy.md).

Initial candidate specifications include:

- GR-SPEC-120 — Generativity Information Model
- GR-SPEC-130 — Generativity Interchange
- GR-SPEC-200 — Epistemic Recording

GR-SPEC-120 and GR-SPEC-130 now have experimental implementable texts: the [record/module/transaction model](GR-SPEC-120-information-model/GR-SPEC-120.md) and [Git/HTTP binding](GR-SPEC-130-interchange/GR-SPEC-130.md). Their reference package and generalized application exercise selected behaviors; neither is an adopted Standard or a claim of full GR conformance. GR-SPEC-200 remains a preliminary work item. The parallel GR-120/130/200 standards-track projects express semantic, interoperability and epistemic requirements; corresponding Specifications provide implementable detail when those requirements justify it. Existing identifiers and paths are retained.

Protocols describe interaction behavior within a Specification, including roles, transitions, outcomes and failures. They are not a separate publication authority class. Candidate capability allocations and development triggers are in the [protocol and specification plan](../docs/planning/protocol_specification_plan.md); the [comprehensive plan](../docs/planning/comprehensive_development_plan.md) coordinates requirements, pilots and review.
