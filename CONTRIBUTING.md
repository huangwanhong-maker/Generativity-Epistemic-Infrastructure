# Contributing

This repository is in early standards-design mode.

Before proposing normative text:

1. identify the target publication;
2. check whether the issue is conceptual, procedural, normative, or implementation-specific;
3. add unresolved matters to `docs/planning/open_questions.md`;
4. create an ADR for architecture-level decisions;
5. preserve dissenting or superseded reasoning rather than rewriting history.

Public contribution and consensus procedures will be formalized in Directives Part 1.

## Contribution terms and attribution

This repository's project-authored material is offered under the [MIT licence](LICENSE), within the scope described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). By intentionally submitting original work for incorporation, you offer that contribution under those terms while retaining your copyright. State your authority to contribute material on behalf of another rights holder, and identify third-party material and its licence separately. Submission for discussion alone is not an assignment of ownership or permission to redistribute material outside the terms you state.

Include your preferred name or organization and a brief contribution description in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) when your contribution is incorporated. Existing notices and contributors' attribution are preserved. AI tools can be acknowledged as tools; they are not added as copyright holders.

Application changes belong in the corresponding application repository; shared packages and launch tools belong in `applicative_infrastructure`. Each repository carries its own licence, contributor list and contribution guidance. The programme's draft ethics and IP policy remains a separate governance work item; the MIT grant does not imply its formal adoption.
