# International Organizations

This folder is reserved for policy proposals aimed at or relevant to international organizations.

Potential themes include:

- international standards for generativity records;
- research-integrity and AI provenance;
- cultural and scientific archives;
- institutional memory;
- transnational interoperability;
- AI-assisted decision records;
- future-generations access and preservation;
- capacity-building for under-resourced institutions;
- governance of contested records;
- model procedures for annotation, correction, and restricted access.

Proposals should be adapted to the mandate and legal authority of the intended organization rather than assuming one universal institutional model.
