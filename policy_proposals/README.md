# Policy Proposals Programme

This directory contains policy-oriented publications derived from the Generativity Standards Program.

## Purpose

The standards programme may generate proposals for international organizations, public institutions, universities, archives, research funders, and other governance actors.

These documents are separate from normative technical standards.

## Possible publication types

- Policy Brief
- Institutional Proposal
- Model Governance Framework
- Pilot Programme Proposal
- Capacity-Building Proposal
- Model Clause / Model Procedure
- Consultation Paper
- Discussion Paper

## Relationship to standards

A policy proposal may recommend:

- piloting a GR standard;
- establishing generativity-record requirements in a programme;
- creating interoperable archival infrastructure;
- adopting provenance or contestation procedures;
- testing rights of correction or annotation;
- creating international research or cultural-preservation initiatives.

A policy proposal should identify which parts are:

- descriptive background;
- normative proposal;
- legal analysis;
- implementation option;
- unresolved question.

## Neutrality and plurality

Policy documents should distinguish factual description, analysis, value judgments, and proposed institutional choices.

Where reasonable alternatives exist, they should be presented clearly enough for decision-makers to evaluate them.


## Publication classes

For international-facing work, use one of the following where appropriate:

- **GR-IPP** — International Policy Proposal
- **GR-IIP** — International Institutional Proposal

These are distinct from Specifications and Standards. They are recommendatory and deliberative rather than conformance documents.
