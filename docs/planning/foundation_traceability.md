# Foundation Traceability for Development Planning

**Document class:** Informative planning and source-analysis document  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL; proposed operational translations and prospective evidence  
**Date:** 2026-09-22

## 1. Purpose and limits

This map supports the [comprehensive development plan](comprehensive_development_plan.md). It connects the foundation corpus to questions for standards, protocols, specifications, and pilots. Its organizing problem is the reliability of relations among reality, manifestations, claims, institutional recognition, and action under symbolic abundance and representational instability.

The [manuscript manifest](../../foundation_manuscripts/MANIFEST.md) assigns stable FM identifiers to all seven supplied PDFs, including both Chapter 3 variants. Source passages supply conceptual grounds; the candidate requirements below are programme proposals. None is an adopted clause, a claim of completed validation, or a replacement definition of a GR concept.

Existing publication codes route potential work without establishing project approval or maturity. Protocol names describe candidate procedures whose publication placement remains subject to the plan. A protocol is a subject of a Specification or Standard, not a separate authority class. Requirements begin in standards-track work; reusable technical needs can then produce a Specification and a pilot. GR-300/310 routing identifies questions for contextual legal and procedural review, not a legal effect derived from the foundation manuscripts.

## 2. Reading the trace

Each FT identifier follows this chain:

`manuscript passage → distinction at stake → candidate requirement or question → discriminating evidence → drafting decision`

The evidence column describes a future test or pilot artifact. It does not assert that evidence has been collected. Useful results include failure: an example the proposed record cannot reconstruct, a distinction it silently merges, unmanageable recording burden, or a safeguard a participant cannot actually exercise.

Page numbers are one-based PDF pages. Chapter and section numbers identify the argument more durably than page numbers alone. Source links point to the exact file entry in the manifest. Candidate requirements are expressed as drafting tasks, not normative clauses.

## 7. Evidence package for the next drafting cycle

The plan's first demonstrator can use programme decisions plus a synthetic institutional case, supplemented by counterexamples from FM-007 §19. A later bounded institutional or research pilot provides different evidence. Synthetic cases test a design property; they are not evidence of successful institutional adoption.

For each selected FT row, the drafting record can contain:

1. Manuscript identifier, exact section/page, and the distinction retained.
2. Candidate requirement, intended publication, scope, and conformance target.
3. A normal example, a failure case, and a counterexample to an overbroad interpretation.
4. Evidence collected, including recording burden and unresolved access restrictions.
5. Test result and limits, including differences between independent implementations or reviewers.
6. Disposition: retain, revise, split into a profile, move to a technical report, or defer; with rationale and affected documents.

External mappings require separate verification of the exact external edition and claimed relationship. This map establishes no compatibility, legal effect, factual guarantee, or automatic equivalence between a foundation formulation and a programme requirement.
