## 1. Scope and method

This review examines all **49 GR-200 requirements and 105 GR-210 requirements** by bounded topical groups. Every requirement ID occurs in exactly one review group. Group status `reviewed` means its text was compared with the declared conceptual basis and related clauses; it does not mean the requirement was fulfilled by a practice or validated across domains. `finding` identifies a concrete drafting or fixture issue, and `open` identifies a question needing further evidence or a decision.

The work comprised direct reading of both drafts, their normative annexes, the original P1 example, GR-100/110 scope and dated-dependency clauses, and relevant actual foundation PDF passages. P1/v2 was authored to make the existing case's distinctions inspectable, then compared manually with selected components of the requirements. Whole-package and whole-procedure fulfillment was not assessed. There was no actual institutional reviewer, participant, exchange, hearing, signature test, access-control test, timing exercise or outcome observation. No public-policy or legal compatibility determination was made.

The supplied foundation passages were read from [FM-005](../../../foundation_manuscripts/GRE_Chapter_05_Complete_First_Draft.pdf), one-based PDF pp. 28–29, 145, 150–151, 161–162, 169–170 and 172–173. This is focused source review of the relevant distinctions, not a new complete manuscript collation or verification of the manuscript's external citations. The [foundation manifest](../../../foundation_manuscripts/MANIFEST.md) preserves version uncertainty and source differences.

## 3. Findings and dispositions proposed for the next edition

<a id="ep-01"></a>

### EP-01 — Ambiguous GR-100 incorporation in GR-210

**Type:** Draft clarification; normative dependency scope. **Priority:** Resolve before publishing the coordinated next edition.

GR-210 Clause 2 says GR-100 supplies operational terms, then says the referenced drafts' relevant requirements remain applicable to the bounded claim package. That sentence can be read to import a separate GR-100 vocabulary-application assessment. GR-200 and GR-110 instead precisely incorporate Clause 3 terms, Clause 5 record-role meanings when used, and result-category meanings in 8.3, while explicitly excluding a separate GR-100 conformity claim. C210-030 invokes the complete applicable GR-200 package obligations, not an independently specified V100 result set.

**Alternatives:** (a) import every applicable V100 requirement and specify the additional subject/results; (b) adopt the precise terminology incorporation already used by GR-200/110. **Proposed direction:** (b), preserving all GR-200 package-level obligations. It avoids a hidden conformance expansion without weakening the epistemic package. **Affected material:** GR-210 Clause 2 and C210-030's interpretation; dependency declarations in updated fixtures. No new requirement ID is needed solely to clarify this reference.

### EP-02 — P1/v1 lacks documentary and performance evidence needed by the expanded procedure

**Type:** Fixture gap, not a newly discovered event or universal draft defect. **Priority:** Address documentary structure now; rehearse performance later.

P1/v1 has a useful narrative but only two response targets, incomplete issue/action separation and no performed grounds/reply, notice or practical-response evidence. Against C210-035/052/069/071/073/102/104, an overall successful-case label would obscure those differences. P1/v2 adds an explicitly authored service schedule, I1–I6, AC1–AC6, a grounds-notice specimen and component results. All actual performance remains not assessed. The current values are local hypotheses whose feasibility needs resource and participant evidence.

**Proposed disposition:** retain the v0.1 fixture historically; use the new one as an experimental rehearsal input. Do not manufacture receipts, assignments or completed funding outcomes. Full C210 procedure conformance remains unclaimed.

### EP-03 — Protected-source and retention claims remain bounded

**Type:** Evidential limitation and OPEN contextual design question; corrected author assessment. **Priority:** Resolve contextual policy before a real pilot.

P1's protected original is not supplied, and B's scenario authorization concerns the excerpt only. A statement that B inspected or authenticated the original would contradict E200-023/042 and C210-059/068/095. P1/v2 distinguishes that unavailable source from the actual invented excerpt and preserves the examination limit. Its retention text supplies a proposed arrangement and future Custodian review, not an identified operative retention or disposal decision. C210-094's decision trigger is therefore unresolved for this design, and its result is **not assessed**. Contextual authority, timing and verification remain questions for the proposed arrangement; no fixed retention period is imposed by this review. Actual retention/disposal/enforcement is also **not assessed**.

**Review correction QA-01:** The initial author assessment marked a C210-094 component and its requirement not fulfilled because contextual authority and a specified retention period were absent. That classification inferred a decision trigger not supplied by P1 and treated a fixed period as necessary when a stated event can define timing. The finding was withdrawn after critique. The register preserves this rationale and now records not assessed with applicability unresolved; no baseline decision or artificial failure case was added.

**Proposed disposition:** keep the limitation visible and prepare a contextual retention arrangement before collection of real data. No blanket retention period or legal basis is invented. Restriction does not turn an unexamined obligation into fulfillment or make an actual activity inapplicable.

### EP-04 — Conformance claim inflation through component results

**Type:** Assessment design hazard; existing normative safeguards sufficient for this case. **Priority:** Enforce in result tooling and summaries.

E200-049 explicitly requires materially different component results to remain visible. C210-097 similarly distinguishes documentary adequacy from performance. A row showing that C1/v2 states an uncertainty is insufficient to pass E200-014 for every included claim, and a notice specimen cannot pass C210-022's actual notification duty. The P1 register labels each subject, method and coverage; whole-requirement rows remain unassessed unless their whole scope was examined. No new permission to downgrade applicable practice obligations is proposed.

**Proposed disposition:** retain these clauses; make their aggregation rule explicit in assessment artifacts and later GR-150 work. A fulfilled component never overrides a failed or unassessed applicable component. Even a row with no known failure is not an overall pass when coverage is incomplete.

### EP-05 — Proportionality and actual access remain open pilot questions

**Type:** OPEN empirical/profile question. **Priority:** Measure before fixing reduced profiles or routine service commitments.

The expanded drafts can express a careful case, but this review does not establish a practicable workload for a small project. The number of requirements is not a burden measure: one shared record can support several checks, while a single participation duty can demand significant resources. Independent capacity, user comprehension, protected participation, actual notice recovery and follow-up are still untested. A future proportionate profile needs an explicit decision about its claim and retained protections; describing a small operator does not silently remove current duties.

**Proposed disposition:** collect preparation time, per-case time, handoffs, support requests, inaccessible material, disputed judgments, comprehension failures and unresolved practical outcomes in a bounded rehearsal. Retain shared-context references where useful, without treating their mere existence as actual performance. No normative burden threshold is selected here.

## 4. P1 reassessment design and results interpretation

The machine-readable review groups cover every E200/C210 requirement exactly once. The component result register is a different artifact: it records whole-requirement coverage limits and selected inspected components against the fixed 0.2 baseline. Whole-requirement rows are deliberately not promoted from a selected component pass. Conditional absence can produce `not applicable` only with an actual missing trigger; this edition uses that result narrowly for E200-041's absent reproducibility/independent-reviewability claim and E200-035's absent comparison or transfer of confidence grades. Unperformed notification, competent review or user comprehension remains `not assessed`.

Actual review evidence consists of the cited fixture portions and the assessment reasoning recorded in the register. In-world representations are identified as authored synthetic material. Documentary checks locate content/version distinctions, source derivation, reasons, qualifications, action status and view limitations. The failure branches are manually compared variants: no live operator was observed making the expected response.

P1 now exposes the complete intended path from capture through practical response without declaring every step performed: A1→M1→S1→C1→D1→CH1→AS1→C1/v2→K2→D2→pending action. C7 and AC5 preserve unresolved effects rather than ending the inquiry at record correction. A proposed acknowledgment or policy change remains different from replenished monitoring capacity, funding or trust.

## 5. Next evidence and publication handling

The immediate drafting correction is EP-01. The principal remaining validation work is a participant rehearsal of the documented procedure, followed by independent assessment of the recording and procedural evidence. It needs an ordinary case and the conflict, unavailable-source, missed-target, failed-notice, restricted-view, rule-challenge, propagation and reopening conditions in GR-210 Annex C. The current authored variants prepare those cases but do not discharge their performance tests.

A later edition can reuse an unchanged documentary result only after recording why changed text, dependencies, scope or evidence do not invalidate it. This report remains fixed to archived Working Draft 0.2. Any next-edition fixes belong in a separate disposition record; they do not retroactively change the reviewed baseline or transform an unperformed scenario into empirical pilot evidence.
