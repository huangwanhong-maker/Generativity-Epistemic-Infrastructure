# Conceptual-fidelity review of GR-100 and GR-110, Working Draft 0.2

**Document class:** Informative review record  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL; editorial source comparison, not programme adoption or a conformance assessment  
**Review date:** 2026-09-22  
**Reviewer:** AI-assisted documentary review; no independent manuscript-author confirmation obtained  
**Baseline:** [Archived GR-100 0.2](../../../publications/archive/0.2/standards/GR-100-foundations-vocabulary/GR-100.tex) and [archived GR-110 0.2](../../../publications/archive/0.2/standards/GR-110-recording-requirements/GR-110.tex)

## 1. Review question and result

This review asks whether the operational proposals preserve the distinctions they draw from the foundation manuscripts, where they deliberately narrow the theory, and where their drafting or assessment instructions can lose those distinctions. It examined all **26 GR-100 requirement identifiers and 111 GR-110 identifiers**, their surrounding definitions, and their normative assessment schedules. The [machine-readable group register](conceptual_review_groups.json) assigns every identifier to exactly one review group. A group's `reviewed` status means that its text was examined; it does not mean that an implementation fulfilled the requirement, that a historical claim is true, or that the author endorses the interpretation.

The central architecture is consistent with the inspected passages: record existence is distinct from factual warrant; relational reality can precede recognition; representations can change later relations; contribution is more demanding than association; historical continuity differs from a chain of contributions; and descriptive generation has no automatic favorable normative sign. The text preserves these distinctions substantially better than a provenance-only or output-only account would.

Three local improvements are proposed. **CF-01** repairs an assessment instruction that can exempt a general safeguard. **CF-02** separates an ambiguous modality example from a claim's assessment disposition. **CF-03** makes the conditional interpretation of a recirculation claim explicit in the return requirements. CF-03 is a precision improvement rather than evidence that version 0.2 contradicts the theory: its general linkage requirements already supply part of the needed discipline. No finding licenses a universal measure of generativity, a compulsory causal method, or a complete ontology.

Source fidelity remains only one part of validation. The number of required declarations, actual recording effort, review competence, comprehension, protected access, and operational correction still need case evidence. Their presence in a well-written clause does not demonstrate a working institution.

## 4. Findings and proposed dispositions

### CF-01 — Assessment instruction can waive part of V100-011

**Location:** GR-100 V100-011; normative Annex A, Table A.1, V100-011 row.  
**Type:** Assessment applicability ambiguity; priority: fix before relying on a complete vocabulary assessment.

The body first requires the application to distinguish operational record roles from metamodel concepts. Its second sentence conditions particular identifications on an explicit scoped mapping and argument. The assessment row says that absence of a mapping makes the conditional check not applicable. A reader can interpret that as making the whole requirement inapplicable, even when an application describes record roles and implicitly confuses them with formal primitives.

**Proposed disposition:** Retain V100-011. Rewrite the assessment row to check the general separation safeguard in the declared application, then assess the mapping-and-argument component only where a mapping or identification is made. A declared absence of mappings can support non-applicability of that component, but cannot exempt an implicit identification found elsewhere. This follows the component-result rule V100-026.

**Alternative:** Make V100-011 itself entirely conditional. That would weaken the general architectural safeguard and is not recommended.

**Discriminating check:** An application labels a semigroup mechanism as Process without a mapping document. The assessor finds an implicit identification requiring an argument; absence of the document is not an absent trigger. An application that makes no formal identification still receives review of the general separation component, with only the mapping-specific component excluded.

### CF-02 — R110-012 mixes a dispute label into modality examples

**Location:** GR-110 R110-012; compare GR-100 V100-016 and R110-051.  
**Type:** Terminological ambiguity; priority: local clarification.

R110-012 lists `disputed` alongside realized, intended, possible and unrealized effects. A dispute can concern whether an effect occurred, who contributed, or whether it was beneficial. Its location in the modality list can encourage one status value to carry actuality, knowledge mode and assessment disposition. GR-100 V100-016 expressly separates these three dimensions. FM-005 §5.17.3 p. 145 additionally distinguishes warrant for the relation, target change and connecting attribution.

**Proposed disposition:** Retain the identifier and its existing purpose. Name actuality/modality, epistemic mode, and assessment disposition separately. Use `unresolved actuality` where the occurrence question itself is unresolved, or qualify the dispute's target. Keep the example that an actual past effect can be retrospectively inferred. Do not make a disputed attribution change a supported occurrence to hypothetical.

**Alternative:** Retain `disputed` but add an explanatory note that identifies exactly which claim is contested. This is weaker because the normative clause would continue to place an assessment expression among modality examples.

**Discriminating check:** A maintenance activity is documented; whether it materially enabled a later discovery is contested. The record preserves an actual activity, a retrospectively inferred contribution, and an unresolved attribution assessment without forcing them into one status.

### CF-03 — Make recirculation links explicit only when that return claim is made

**Location:** GR-100 provisional Generativity return definition and source note; GR-110 R110-018/058/073, semantic and assessment schedules.  
**Type:** Conditional recording precision; priority: develop in the next draft without settling the broader return concept.

R110-018/073 correctly avoids inventing a quantity or entitlement. It does not explicitly tell the reader how to distinguish a transfer from its contribution to regenerated conditions. The broader linkage clauses can cover that problem, but their application to a return account is indirect. FM-005 §5.17.3 p. 145 identifies the separate uncertainty in generation, conversion/transfer, return and subsequent effects. An improvement should expose this structure while preserving the open relationship between `generativity return` and `recirculation`.

**Proposed disposition:** Add a conditional requirement or extend R110-073: where a return account claims recirculation or alteration of future generative conditions, identify those asserted links, relevant subjects/recipients, boundary, horizon, grounds, and link-specific uncertainty, or state that a link is proposed, unobserved, unavailable or unassessed. Cross-reference R110-058. State that completed transfer alone does not establish restored or expanded generative capacity. Add the same discriminating check to the return assessment row and semantic schedule.

**Alternatives:** Keep only the general linkage clauses and clarify their application informatively; or impose a universal return formula. The first remains possible if pilots show the direct clause unnecessary. The second is unsupported by the reviewed sources and would close an unresolved theoretical and normative question.

**Discriminating check:** An institution proposes training support following a disputed decision. This is a proposal, not an observed transfer, a legally established entitlement, or evidence of changed future capacity. A later receipt can establish only the transfer claim unless further evidence supports the remaining links.

## 5. Tensions deliberately preserved

1. **Property and Attribute — OPEN.** FM-007 §10 treats Attribute through a declared domain, interpretation, scale and definedness. GR-100 retains Property as a provisional operational role and expressly denies an established equivalence. Its minimum metadata, numerical-scale rule and missingness rule preserve many relevant distinctions; they do not settle whether the concepts coincide. A future ADR needs comparative examples for a qualitative classification, a partial value, disputed assignment, and a value whose subject changes across descriptive levels.

2. **Fixed carrier, declared regimes, and emergence — OPEN.** FM-006 pp. 10–11 acknowledges that its fixed carrier and congruence do not generate new states or distinctions. FM-007 §17.1–2, pp. 78–80, models transitions within declared regimes and expressly leaves genuinely unrepresented vocabulary development to further work. R110-066 can record changing distinctions without claiming that these formalisms exhaust emergence. Category counts, graph size, and formal collapse are consequently not universal measures of generativity.

3. **Preservation and normativity — OPEN.** FM-006 pp. 10–11 places normative weight on the chosen congruence while acknowledging that not every collapse is wrong. FM-007 §16.7, pp. 77–78, separates formal preservation from reasons to value a distinction. The standards retain explicit evaluations, access limits and justified loss; they do not silently rewrite the earlier formulation or claim that mathematical preservation proves justice.

4. **The two Chapter 3 files — OPEN.** The manifest records a verified eight/nine problem-family difference and unresolved chronology. This review uses FM-003's specified praxis passages. It does not declare FM-003 final, replace FM-002, or claim that the two files have been fully collated.

5. **Return terminology and distribution — OPEN.** The recording topic can hold proposals and disputed accounts. Its relationship to manuscript recirculation, any assessment of beneficial return, recipients' claims, and legally effective allocation remain separate decisions. CF-03 does not resolve those matters.

6. **Proportionality and practical value — OPEN.** Source support for a distinction does not prove that every proposed declaration is necessary at every scale. R110-035–037 allows bounded purposes and acknowledges unmet work; it does not yet establish a lighter conformance profile. Measure actual authoring/review effort and losses before selecting GR-150 profiles.

## 6. Implications for the P0/P1 exercises

These are review tasks, not reports that institutional actions occurred. Their actual execution and component results belong in the separate pilot assessment records.

The [P1 version 2 fixture](../../../examples/validation_0.2/p1-contested-decision.md) now supplies stable documentary locators: [claims](../../../examples/validation_0.2/p1-contested-decision.md#claims), [claim-relative evidence](../../../examples/validation_0.2/p1-contested-decision.md#evidence-roles), and [revision/action records](../../../examples/validation_0.2/p1-contested-decision.md#revision-actions). `GEN/v2` distinguishes practical access and the retrospectively imagined alternative; `AC6` and the following return statement preserve the absence of an established return chain. These passages were inspected as authored case representations, not as independently established events.

| Task | Requirements principally tested | Observable discriminator | Limit to retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read the original and revised claim with its evidence | V100-002–004/016/017; R110-005/012/046/047/051 | Reader distinguishes a recorded statement, an occurrence claim, a contribution claim, and their different grounds/statuses. | Reading authored fiction cannot authenticate a missing original log or establish a real occurrence. |
| Reconstruct a historical alternative | R110-015/063–066 | Actor-period evidence distinguishes a contemporaneously available option from a later analyst's imagined option; practical access remains separate. | No evidence of an option is not proof that every alternative was absent. |
| Follow correction into consequences | R110-019/074/077–083 | Receipt, reliance, revised judgment, implemented action, and repair have distinct locators or explicit unassessed status. | A pending funding outcome and unverified external spreadsheet correction remain pending/unverified. |
| Examine a return proposal | R110-018/058/073; proposed CF-03 | Proposed support, completed transfer and altered future capacity remain distinct; each asserted link has grounds or its own uncertainty. | The current P1 has no verified return pathway. Recording that limitation does not assess substantive return. |
| Apply a role and assess its criteria | V100-009–011/025/026; R110-009 | Stable structural references remain supplied, uncertain contextual values remain typed, and unmet criteria do not receive fulfillment. | A usable role description is not proof of subject identity; review of the V100-011 general safeguard is not waived. |
| Re-read after classification change | V100-007/015/018; R110-016/050/067/068/105 | Old categories and grounds remain inspectable; correction, new knowledge and rule change are separately located. | A revised account does not give earlier actors later knowledge or establish that practical harm was repaired. |

The immediate revision can address CF-01–03 without resolving the open theoretical questions. Subsequent pilots need cases beyond P1: maintenance with delayed effects; conflicting identity criteria after organizational succession; and historical reconstruction with severe preservation gaps. Those cases test whether the present distinctions survive use and whether the required recording effort is proportionate.
