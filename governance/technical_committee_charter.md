The Markdown outline below is retained as historical planning context; its candidate choices are not adopted. Current requirements and unresolved decisions are in the TeX draft.
## Generativity Records and Infrastructure

**Status:** Draft

### Scope

Standardization of conceptual models, recording requirements, data models, interchange formats, presentation requirements, preservation/access rules, and conformance methods for records of generativity.

### Exclusions

The committee does not, by default:

- define a complete metaphysics of generativity;
- certify substantive historical interpretations as true;
- require one software platform;
- require public disclosure of all recorded material.

### Initial working groups

- WG 1 — Foundations and Ontology
- WG 2 — Recording Model
- WG 3 — Data and Interoperability
- WG 4 — Presentation and Reconstruction
- WG 5 — Ethics, Privacy, and Preservation
- WG 6 — Conformance and Implementation
