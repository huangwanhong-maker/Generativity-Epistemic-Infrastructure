# Standards Programme Charter

> **Developed draft available:** [Working Draft 0.3 source](standards_programme_charter.tex) and [compiled PDF](../build/publications/GSP-Charter.pdf). The Markdown outline below is retained as historical planning context; its candidate choices are not adopted. Current requirements and unresolved decisions are in the TeX draft.

**Status:** Working outline

## 1. Mission

Develop and maintain open, rigorous, interoperable standards concerning the recording, preservation, presentation, and exchange of generativity-related information.

## 2. Principles

The programme should pursue:

- openness;
- transparency;
- procedural fairness;
- traceability;
- stakeholder participation;
- technical coherence;
- implementability;
- revisability;
- respect for privacy and access constraints.

## 3. Publications

The programme may issue:

- Standards;
- Technical Specifications;
- Technical Reports;
- Manuals and Guides;
- Schemas and validation artifacts;
- Corrigenda and Amendments.

## 4. Governance bodies

- Standards Council
- Technical Committees
- Working Groups
- Editors
- Project Leads
- Reviewers and Observers

## 5. Decision principle

Consensus is the preferred basis for technical adoption. Voting procedures, objection handling, appeals, and escalation shall be defined in Directives Part 1.

## 6. Maintenance

Published standards remain subject to review, confirmation, amendment, revision, supersession, or withdrawal.
