# GR-120 — Generativity information model

**Publication class:** Standards-track Working Draft; not an adopted Standard  
**Maturity:** Preparatory WD 0.1, 2026-09-22; proposed requirements for review  
**Scope of this edition:** Record identity, overlapping roles, relational incidence and modular descriptions

## 1. Scope

This document proposes requirements for an implementation-neutral model of retained representations and their relationships. It distinguishes a represented target, an identifiable record about that target, revisions of that record, and views derived from those revisions. It covers records acting in several operational roles and relations whose participants can themselves be relational records.

Detailed transport, canonical serialization, authentication, evidential assessment, institutional review and legal admissibility are outside this edition. Candidate technical realization is supplied separately by GR-SPEC-120.

## 3. Terms

**record identity:** identity of a retained representation within a declared identification scope; it does not establish the identity of the represented target.

**record role:** an operational classification as Entity, State, Event, Process, Relation or Property. Several roles can apply to one record.

**incidence:** one participation of a referenced record or represented target in a stated relational account, distinguished by its own identifier and role.

**module:** a versioned, identified part of a record's information envelope. A module's technical boundary does not establish a separate source of epistemic, legal or institutional authority.

## 4. Proposed information requirements

**M120-001.** A model shall distinguish record identity from claims about represented-target identity and shall declare the scope in which a record identifier is resolved.

**M120-002.** A model shall permit more than one applicable operational role and shall distinguish any primary display role from an exclusive classification claim.

**M120-003.** A model shall preserve a Relation as an identifiable record when its own description, provenance, properties or history is recorded. Reduction to an anonymous pair of endpoints shall not be its sole representation.

**M120-004.** A structured relational account shall identify its stated relation, participating references, participant roles, and whether each reference concerns the record or its represented target.

**M120-005.** Distinct incidences shall remain distinguishable when they reference the same participant. A model shall declare any profile restrictions on participant cardinality, self-reference, cycles, parallel relations and relations participating in other relations.

**M120-006.** A structured relational account shall distinguish a stated complete participant scope from a partial scope. A partial account shall retain its known limitations without requiring invented participant records.

**M120-007.** A model shall distinguish an untyped navigation reference from a stated semantic relation. Migration shall not assign substantive relation meaning solely because an earlier record contained a link.

**M120-008.** A module envelope shall identify its module kind, version, interpretation requirement and content. Consumers shall preserve uninterpreted optional content when changing unrelated supported content, or refuse the change with an explicit limitation.

**M120-009.** A consumer that cannot interpret a required module shall not represent a mutation as validated against that module's requirements. Its applicable mutation boundary shall be declared.

**M120-010.** A model shall distinguish occurrence descriptions and modality from recording time, revision order, epistemic basis and disputed status. Changing a stored representation shall not automatically imply a change in its represented target.

**M120-011.** Absent descriptions of temporal scope, identity criteria or context shall not be interpreted as proof of timelessness, resolved identity or unrestricted applicability.

**M120-012.** A profile shall declare unsupported representation needs, including cross-scope references, mixed epistemic grounds and unresolved participant identity, without asserting that the underlying GR theory excludes them.

## 5. Assessment and infrastructure need

An assessment identifies the model version, claimed clauses and profile. Inspectable cases include a record with two roles; a three-participant relation; repeated participation; a relation participating in another relation; a partial account; a migrated neutral link; and an unknown optional and required module. Rejection of a case is assessed against the declared profile restriction, not mistaken for a philosophical impossibility.

GR-SPEC-120 supplies UUID references, versioned JSON module envelopes, one-to-32 incidence entries, supported mutation operations and a validation package. These choices operationalize this draft for a pilot; JSON and these numerical bounds are not requirements of M120-001–012.

## 6. Open matters

Q-05–09 and Q-36 remain relevant. Cross-project reference disclosure, participant identity revision, richer epistemic structures and loss-aware imported modules need further development. A fully articulated model of State, Event and Process identity is not completed by this graph profile.
