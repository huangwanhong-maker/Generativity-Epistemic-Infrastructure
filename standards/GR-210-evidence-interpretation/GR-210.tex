\GSPpreface{Introduction}
An account can be authentic, widely repeated, or institutionally accepted and still lack adequate grounds for its substantive conclusion. A person affected by that account also needs a usable route to question it. This document connects the examination of evidence with the practical organization of receipt, response, review, revision, and action. It keeps those functions distinct so that a successful correction of text is not mistaken for a completed remedy and an inclusive procedure is not mistaken for proof of truth.

The foundation manuscripts distinguish trace from evidence, evidential adequacy from epistemic justice, and current knowledge of the past from the past and its historical representations. They also identify unequal capacities to produce, preserve, interpret, and contest evidence. The procedures proposed here operationalize a bounded part of those concerns. They do not claim to exhaust the philosophical concepts or prescribe a single institutional model.

The procedure can be operated with documents and accountable people before a machine protocol is needed. Technical infrastructure becomes useful when case correlation, controlled exchange, version tracking, notification, or concurrent action requires interoperable behavior. Such infrastructure remains subordinate to declared authority and protection. A complete state log cannot establish that a reviewer actually considered the grounds or that an affected person could use the route.

\section{Scope}
\subsection{General}

This document proposes requirements for a bounded procedure through which people can question an attributed account, introduce evidence or an alternative interpretation, obtain a reasoned disposition and seek further review. Its subjects are the procedure operated by a named organization or recording practice and the associated case record.

An evidential assessment, a procedural disposition, an institutional action and an externally competent legal finding remain distinct. This draft provides no universal tribunal, evidentiary ranking or legal right to review. A conforming procedure can conclude that the available evidence does not resolve the dispute.

The scope includes a procedure declaration, accessible receipt, case scoping, reviewer assignment, evidential and interpretive examination, participation, reasoned disposition, notification, further review, correction propagation, practical response, closure, and protected retention. It excludes a universal evidentiary ranking, mandatory probability scale, cross-jurisdiction legal remedy, and a claim that procedural conformity proves the underlying facts. Institutional profiles determine the contextual service periods and authority within these boundaries.

\section{Terms and definitions}
\subsection{Operational procedure terms}
The following terms narrow the procedural use in this document without redefining the broader GR concepts supplied by GR-100.
\begin{description}
\item[Operator] The accountable organization, person, or arrangement that declares and provides the procedure within its stated authority.
\item[Case] A distinguishable collection of issues, submissions, review actions, dispositions, and follow-up concerning identified records or decisions.
\item[Case record] The retained representation of a case, including its boundaries, versions, access scope, and known missing material. Its existence does not prove that every represented action occurred.
\item[Issue] A question capable of receiving a scoped response, such as whether a date is wrong, evidence supports a claim, a category is adequate, or the declared procedure was followed.
\item[Reviewer] The assigned person or accountable review function that examines specified issues within declared competence and authority.
\item[Disposition] The reasoned procedural outcome for an issue or case. It can maintain a judgment, support revision, record unresolved evidence, refer the matter, or close a procedure without settling the substantive question.
\item[Practical response] An authorized or proposed action following review, distinct from recording the disposition and from evidence that the action was completed.
\item[Further review] Reconsideration, a procedural appeal, or another declared examination of a previous handling or disposition, with the route's authority and independence stated.
\item[Material grounds] Evidence, assumptions, criteria, or reasons capable of changing a reasonable assessment of the issue, its disposition, or its consequential use within the declared scope.
\item[Case state] A procedural status indicating completed and pending handling. It is not a truth value, a probability, or an institutional judgment on the underlying claim.
\end{description}

\section{Procedure declaration}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-001}{The operator shall publish or otherwise provide an accessible procedure declaration identifying its purpose, eligible users, covered records or decisions, responsible roles, asserted authority, submission routes and exclusions.}
\req{C210-002}{The declaration shall distinguish technical correction, evidential disagreement, procedural complaint, ethical or conduct complaint and an asserted legal claim, identifying their available handling routes and limits of authority.}
\req{C210-003}{The operator shall declare response and review periods, their start events, permitted extensions, notice arrangements and the route available when a period is exceeded. The chosen periods shall be justified for the institutional context and affected users.}
\req{C210-004}{The operator shall declare the information needed to submit a case, the support or alternatives available to users and how restricted evidence or protected identities can be handled. A demand for unnecessary public disclosure shall not be a condition of receipt.}
\note{No universal period is selected here. The programme's own standards-development comments and appeals are governed by Directives Part 1 if adopted; an institution using records has its own declared procedure and legal context.}

\subsection{Declared authority and service conditions}
\req{C210-033}{The declaration shall have an identifier, version, effective interval, accountable owner, and accessible change history. It shall identify the normative dependencies and any institutional profile applied, the intended users and languages, and the procedure functions actually available. A named but unstaffed function shall be identified as unavailable rather than presented as an operative review route.}
\req{C210-034}{For each kind of issue, the declaration shall identify who can receive it, examine evidence, determine a disposition, authorize action, and conduct further review. It shall distinguish an operator's internal policy from a claimed external legal mandate and identify the source, scope, and uncertainty of the latter. Receipt by one function shall not transfer authority belonging to another.}
\req{C210-035}{The operator shall maintain the service schedule in Annex A, including receipt, acknowledgement, clarification, review, reply, disposition, notification, further-review requests, action follow-up, and closure. The schedule shall identify clock starts, responsible roles, expected periods or deadlines, permitted pauses and extensions, and escalation when service cannot be provided.}
\req{C210-036}{The operator shall make submission and further-review routes available without requiring a person to accept the challenged account, withdraw an unrelated complaint, or expose unnecessary protected information. Any eligibility limitation, fee, identity requirement, representative arrangement, or technological prerequisite shall have a declared purpose, authority, proportionality rationale, and available alternative or challenge route.}
\req{C210-037}{Before changing the procedure, the operator shall assess effects on open cases, participation, protection, and remedies. The change notice shall state the new version, effective date, reasons, transition treatment, and how affected users can question the change. A later rule shall not be applied silently to make an earlier handling appear compliant.}
\req{C210-038}{A profile shall identify its institutional setting, covered actions, authority limits, service periods, available resources, review competence, protection basis, and assessment scope. It shall identify any requirement not met rather than claim full GR-210 fulfillment through a profile that silently removes an applicable obligation.}
\req{C210-039}{The operator shall assess whether its declared channels and staffing can support the expected cases and urgency. Material capacity gaps shall produce a documented mitigation, narrower service declaration, referral arrangement, or postponement with notice. A backlog shall not be concealed by classifying unexamined submissions as resolved.}

\note{An institution can distinguish a clerical correction desk from a merits-review panel and an external legal forum. A single contact can assist routing, but that convenience does not make it competent to decide each type of question. A profile's named institution is part of the authority claim to verify, not evidence of authority by itself.}

\section{Receipt and scoping}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-005}{On receiving a submission, the operator shall create a distinguishable case reference and record receipt time, the target record or decision and version if available, the asserted issue and the submitter's attribution or protected-contact arrangement. Receipt shall not be reported as agreement with the submission.}
\req{C210-006}{The operator shall acknowledge receipt through the declared route or record why acknowledgment is not possible. The response shall explain the next step, expected timing and any missing information needed for further action.}
\req{C210-007}{Where a case is out of scope, incomplete, duplicated or directed to the wrong recipient, the operator shall record that procedural reason and any available correction or referral route. That outcome shall not be represented as factual refutation.}
\req{C210-008}{Any restriction on repetitive or abusive submissions shall use declared criteria, record the reason and preserve a route to challenge the restriction. The operator shall not classify a submission as abusive solely because it criticizes an authoritative account or repeats an unresolved material concern.}

\subsection{Intake, triage, and issue separation}
\req{C210-040}{The operator shall accept enough information to identify a concern without requiring the submitter to supply a complete evidentiary case or technically correct amendment at receipt. If the target or issue is unclear, the operator shall seek clarification and offer the declared assistance before closing for incompleteness, unless contact is unavailable or a stated protection constraint prevents it.}
\req{C210-041}{Triage shall identify immediate protection or irreversible-consequence risks, the covered question, required routing, known deadlines, and whether temporary preservation or action review is available within the operator's authority. Triage shall not be presented as a concluded evidential finding. Any urgent measure shall have a responsible actor, scope, reason, review point, and notice appropriate to protection needs.}
\req{C210-042}{A submission containing several kinds of issue shall be divided into distinguishable questions with linked handling routes and a coordinating contact. A referral shall identify what is sent, on what authority, to whom, and whether receipt or acceptance has been confirmed. The operator shall not describe sending a referral as evidence that the recipient has assumed the case.}
\req{C210-043}{The intake record shall preserve the submitted meaning and relevant original formulation within protection limits. Translation, transcription, summarization, or assisted reformulation shall identify its responsible role, method, version relationship, and material uncertainty. The submitter shall have a practicable opportunity to correct a material misunderstanding through the permitted contact arrangement.}
\req{C210-044}{A duplicate-case decision shall identify the related case and explain which issues are shared and which remain distinct. Consolidation shall retain separate attribution, protection preferences, and individual practical consequences where material. A previous disposition shall not automatically dispose of new evidence or a newly identified defect in the previous handling.}
\req{C210-045}{A request for further information shall specify the missing information, why it matters, who is expected to supply it, available assistance or alternatives, the response period, and the effect of nonresponse. The operator shall not shift responsibility for evidence within its own declared control to a person who lacks access without explaining and addressing that access problem.}
\req{C210-046}{A case placed on hold shall identify the reason, affected work, responsible owner, next review date, and clock treatment. A hold shall not remove the case from pending-work reporting or imply acceptance, rejection, or abandonment. The operator shall reassess the hold at the stated review point and notify permitted recipients of a material change.}
\req{C210-047}{When a submitter withdraws a request, the operator shall record its scope, distinguish withdrawal of participation from withdrawal of an evidential assertion, and explain any continuing handling under a separate declared responsibility. Withdrawal shall not be described as proof that the original claim was false or that the person agreed with the challenged decision.}
\req{C210-048}{Case transitions shall conform to the semantic controls in Annex B. The operator shall record the previous and new state, trigger, responsible actor, time, reason, and next action. Local status labels may differ if their mapping preserves the distinctions between receipt, review, disposition, action pending, and closure.}

\section{Review responsibilities and participation}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-009}{Before substantive review, the operator shall identify the reviewer or review function, relevant competence, relation to the contested account and conflicts of interest. Material conflicts shall be handled through recusal, reassignment or a disclosed limitation with an appropriate further-review route.}
\req{C210-010}{The reviewer shall identify the questions within the case, the relevant claim versions, evidence available, known limitations and decision criteria. Factual, normative, procedural and legal questions shall remain distinguishable.}
\req{C210-011}{Affected parties shall be given an opportunity to respond to material grounds used in the disposition through a procedure consistent with protection and applicable institutional constraints. Where direct disclosure is restricted, the operator shall explain the permitted alternative and its limits.}
\req{C210-012}{The operator shall record participation barriers known to affect the case, assistance or adjustments offered and material perspectives that could not be obtained. Absence of a response shall not automatically be represented as consent or admission.}
\note{A reviewer cannot promise that every perspective will be available. The proposed obligation is to expose the evidential and procedural consequence of a material gap.}

\subsection{Assignment, competence, and meaningful response}
\req{C210-049}{The reviewer assignment shall state the questions delegated, relevant domain competence or situated knowledge, decision limits, availability, and accepted responsibility. Where several reviewers supply different expertise, the record shall identify who integrates findings and who is accountable for the disposition. Unavailable competence shall narrow the finding or trigger additional review, referral, or deferral.}
\req{C210-050}{The operator shall provide a route to raise a conflict concern and assign its assessment to a person not deciding a complaint about their own interest. The conflict disposition shall identify the concern, relevant evidence, management action, and effect on the case. Disclosure alone shall not be represented as independent handling.}
\req{C210-051}{An author of the challenged account may explain its sources or participate in a disclosed initial reconsideration, but shall not be described as an independent reviewer of that account. A material unresolved conflict shall not support an unqualified independent-review claim; the available further-review arrangement and its limits shall remain explicit.}
\req{C210-052}{Before a consequential disposition, the operator shall give affected parties notice of the questions, material grounds proposed for reliance, applicable criteria, response route, and response period to the extent permitted by protection. A later-added material ground shall receive a further response opportunity unless a declared urgent measure requires temporary action with prompt subsequent review.}
\req{C210-053}{An opportunity to respond shall be assessed in relation to actual access, language, disability, resources, time, and the form of the material. The operator shall record requested adjustments, its response, and unresolved barriers. Merely making a complex dossier available shall not establish that an affected person could meaningfully address the proposed grounds.}
\req{C210-054}{Where direct participation is unsafe or impracticable, the operator shall assess the declared alternatives, including protected contact, assisted submission, representative participation, or controlled independent examination. The record shall identify the chosen arrangement, its authority and limitations, and any material perspective it cannot provide. Representation shall not be inferred without a basis.}
\req{C210-055}{A reviewer shall assess testimony in relation to the source's access, claim scope, method, and material limitations rather than institutional status alone. Relevant experience and technical expertise shall remain distinguishable. Giving a person an opportunity to speak shall not be reported as showing that their grounds influenced the decision unless the disposition records how they were considered.}
\req{C210-056}{If the reviewer and decision authority are different, the authority shall identify the review findings received, any departure from them, the grounds and competence for that departure, and the additional response opportunity required by new material grounds. A managerial instruction shall not silently replace an evidential assessment while retaining the reviewer's attribution.}
\req{C210-057}{The operator shall provide a protected route to report retaliation or interference linked to participation and shall record available protective responses within its authority. Such a report shall remain distinct from the factual merits of the original case. A person shall not have to publish the protected allegation to obtain triage or an explanation of the available route.}

\section{Evidence and interpretation}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-013}{An assessment shall identify the claim under examination, the evidence relied on, how that material bears on the claim, relevant method and assumptions, and known counterevidence or materially plausible alternatives.}
\req{C210-014}{The reviewer shall distinguish the authenticity or integrity of a source from the warrant it provides for a particular claim. A signed assertion shall not receive an unqualified truth status merely because its signature validates.}
\req{C210-015}{A conclusion based on missing evidence shall identify the expected trace-production, observation or preservation conditions supporting that inference. Where those conditions are not established, absence of the record shall remain distinguishable from non-occurrence.}
\req{C210-016}{The assessment shall state uncertainty and its scope without inventing a common probability scale for heterogeneous judgments. Disagreement among assessors shall be attributed and retained where material and permitted.}
\req{C210-017}{Where a category, method or institutional rule is challenged, the reviewer shall distinguish whether it was applied correctly from whether its adequacy or legitimacy is itself under review. A correct application shall not by itself dispose of a challenge to the rule.}
\req{C210-018}{A material computational or AI-assisted contribution to the review shall be identified with the inputs or sources available for inspection, its role, relevant limitations and responsible human or institutional uptake. An inaccessible internal process shall not be replaced by an invented explanation.}

\subsection{Evidence plan and material examination}
\req{C210-058}{The reviewer shall maintain an issue-to-evidence plan identifying the question, claim versions, relevant criteria, material available, material sought, expected access, alternatives to examine, and known gaps. A change in the plan that materially affects the scope or burden of review shall be recorded and communicated to permitted affected participants.}
\req{C210-059}{An evidence item used in a disposition shall have a locator or controlled reference, source attribution, acquisition context, relevant version, and any known transformation or custody limitation material to the inference. An unavailable original shall be distinguished from an examined copy, excerpt, testimony about the item, or generated reconstruction.}
\req{C210-060}{For each material item, the assessment shall state its role relative to the specified claim: support, challenge, qualification, non-discrimination, or another explained relationship. The reviewer shall not give the item a universal reliability status that silently transfers across different claims, times, or contexts. A role assigned by a party shall remain distinguishable from the reviewer's assessment.}
\req{C210-061}{Where several items are treated as corroboration, the reviewer shall examine known derivation and dependence material to that claim. Copies, summaries, translations, model outputs, and repeated citations of one source shall not count as independent observations solely because they are separately stored or differently worded. Any asserted independence shall have a stated basis and limits.}
\req{C210-062}{The reviewer shall identify material alternatives that fit the accessible evidence and state why an alternative is retained, weakened, rejected, or outside the assessed scope. An alternative need not receive equal weight merely because a party proposes it. Failure to examine alternatives shall remain a limitation rather than be reported as evidence that none exists.}
\req{C210-063}{When an identity, temporal order, measurement, classification, or causal link is contested, the review shall identify the relevant criterion or method and assumptions. Agreement on a record identifier, timestamp, endpoint, or category label shall not by itself resolve the corresponding underlying identity, chronology, trajectory, or causal question.}
\req{C210-064}{An assessment of a generative contribution shall state the capacity, relation, distinction, constraint, or possibility at issue, the comparison condition, temporal horizon, proposed connection, and evidential limits. A change following an intervention shall be examined for whether it reveals an earlier condition, creates a new condition, or combines both; later observation shall not silently become direct evidence of the earlier configuration.}
\req{C210-065}{If a rule or category is challenged, the operator shall separate the case-specific application question from the rule-review question and identify the competent recipient for each. A referral of the rule question shall leave its status visible. Rejection under the existing rule shall not be represented as a finding that the rule is adequate, fair, or legally valid.}
\req{C210-066}{A change in evaluative criteria during review shall identify the earlier and revised criteria, authority, reasons, affected issues, and transition effects. The operator shall provide a response opportunity where the change materially affects a party's case. Historical acts shall retain the criteria actually relied on at the time, even when the present assessment uses a later framework.}
\req{C210-067}{A numerical score, confidence estimate, or model recommendation used materially in review shall identify what it measures, relevant inputs, method and version where available, assumptions, uncertainty, and role in the decision. A threshold chosen for operational action shall remain distinguishable from an estimate of factual warrant. Unavailable technical details shall qualify the assessment rather than be fabricated.}
\req{C210-068}{The reviewer shall document the planned examination actually performed, material steps not performed, reasons, and effects on the conclusion. An evidence request sent, an access permission obtained, or a tool available shall not be reported as an examination completed. A finding shall remain bounded by the material and methods actually used.}

\section{Disposition and practical response}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-019}{The disposition shall state the questions decided, findings and their scope, evidence and reasons, responsible decision function, decision time, remaining disagreement and matters outside its authority.}
\req{C210-020}{The disposition shall distinguish correction of a record, changed evidential assessment, maintained judgment, changed institutional action, inability to decide and procedural closure. Procedural closure shall not imply epistemic agreement.}
\req{C210-021}{Where the procedure supports a remedy or practical response, the disposition shall identify the proposed or authorized action, its responsible executor, scope, timing and evidence needed to establish completion. Recording a proposed action shall not establish that it occurred.}
\req{C210-022}{The operator shall notify the submitter and other relevant recipients through the declared, permitted routes, explain any further-review route and record delivery or known failure. Public notices shall respect the case's disclosure limits.}
\req{C210-023}{A material correction shall be linked to the earlier account and known dependent views or decisions. The operator shall record which corrections or notices were performed and which downstream effects remain unresolved.}
\note{A corrected classification can leave lost opportunities, costs or harm unremedied. The procedure records the distinction rather than declaring repair complete because a field changed.}

\subsection{Reasons, notification, and action tracking}
\req{C210-069}{Each disposition shall address every scoped material issue or state why it remains deferred, referred, withdrawn, or unresolved. The record shall distinguish the parties' positions, the reviewer's assessment, the authorized determination, and any minority or competing assessment retained within protection limits. A single closed-case label shall not conceal different outcomes for different issues.}
\req{C210-070}{A maintained judgment shall explain why the challenge does not warrant the requested change, including treatment of material contrary grounds. An inability-to-decide disposition shall identify the limiting evidence, authority, or method and any conditions for further review. Neither outcome shall rely solely on institutional acceptance of the original account.}
\req{C210-071}{An action plan shall distinguish proposed, authorized, assigned, initiated, completed, verified, failed, and no-longer-pursued actions where those differences are material. It shall identify the executor, authority, affected records or persons, due or review date, completion evidence, and route when execution fails. A reviewer lacking implementation authority shall identify the required decision recipient rather than promise completion.}
\req{C210-072}{When immediate action is taken before full review, the operator shall record the urgency, authority, limited purpose, expected consequences, duration or review point, notice limits, and later examination required. The temporary action shall not be presented as a final evidential finding or remove the remaining opportunity to challenge material grounds.}
\req{C210-073}{The notification record shall identify the permitted recipient, route, exact disposition or summary version, dispatch time, known delivery status, and any protected information withheld. The operator shall distinguish dispatch, delivery, receipt acknowledged, and substantive response. A delivery failure material to a review opportunity shall trigger the declared alternative or timing correction.}
\req{C210-074}{A notice of disposition shall give an accessible account of the outcome, material reasons and limitations, action status, further-review route, relevant deadline and starting event, and assistance available. If the full record cannot be shared, the notice shall explain the permitted way to examine or challenge the grounds without overstating the completeness of the summary.}
\req{C210-075}{Correction planning shall identify known dependent claims, assessments, records, presentations, and decisions within the operator's control or declared notification relationships. For each, the operator shall record correction performed, review pending, no change with reason, notification sent, unreachable recipient, or another explained status. It shall not assert universal propagation beyond the evidenced boundary.}
\req{C210-076}{A correction shall preserve a distinguishable relationship between the prior and revised account, its reason and authority, and the time of each formulation, subject to protection and retention. The corrected view shall not present earlier decision makers as having used evidence or categories acquired later. A removed sensitive detail shall not be retained in the public history solely to illustrate the correction.}
\req{C210-077}{A consequential action or remedy shall be evaluated separately from record correction. The follow-up shall identify the intended practical change, evidence of what occurred, unresolved costs or losses, affected perspectives obtained, and limits on causal attribution. An apology, amended field, or scheduled meeting shall not be recorded as restoration of an opportunity without supporting evidence.}
\req{C210-078}{A case closure shall identify which issues are disposed, which actions remain pending, who owns continuing follow-up, and the conditions for reopening. Where the declared procedure requires action verification before closure, unavailable verification shall prevent that closure claim. Administrative closure shall not erase outstanding obligations, remaining disagreement, or unavailable evidence.}
\req{C210-079}{If a disposition is itself corrected, the operator shall identify the earlier disposition and notice versions, the error, authority for correction, affected deadlines or actions, and renewed notifications or response opportunities. A substantive change shall receive the review warranted by its effect, rather than be hidden as a typographic correction.}

\section{Further review, reopening and continuity}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-024}{The procedure shall provide a declared route to request reconsideration of a material error, new evidence or procedural failure, with its scope, competent recipient and limitations. A reviewer of their own contested action shall not be described as independent.}
\req{C210-025}{A reopened case shall identify the earlier disposition, trigger, changed evidence or criteria, reviewer and new outcome. Later interpretation shall not silently replace the record of what was known or decided earlier.}
\req{C210-026}{If the operator lacks competence, authority or resources to complete a material step, it shall record the limitation and notify permitted recipients of the available alternative, postponement or closure basis. Passage of time alone shall not establish a finding on the substantive claim.}

\subsection{Reconsideration, procedural appeal, and reopening}
\req{C210-080}{The declaration shall distinguish reconsideration of evidence or judgment, review of procedural failure, conduct handling, and an external legal route where one is asserted. It shall state who can request each route, the grounds and scope, responsible recipient, available remedies, timing, and independence arrangements. Routing assistance shall preserve the submitted concern rather than require technical knowledge of the operator's organization.}
\req{C210-081}{A further-review request shall receive a linked reference, acknowledgement, admissibility decision with reasons, reviewer assignment, timetable, and notice of any effect on action or closure. A refusal to admit the request shall not be represented as substantive refutation and shall identify any remaining challenge route or explicit limit.}
\req{C210-082}{A person who made the challenged decision shall not be described as independent when reviewing it. An independent-review claim shall identify the reviewer's separation from the disputed handling and relevant interests. If the declared independent function is unavailable, the operator shall disclose the limitation and refrain from claiming that an independent appeal occurred.}
\req{C210-083}{The further-review authority shall assess whether disputed action needs a stay, temporary qualification, preservation measure, or other protection within its mandate. The decision shall identify urgency, consequences for affected parties, duration or review point, and reasons. Requesting review shall not be assumed either to stop every action or to leave every action unaffected.}
\req{C210-084}{Further review shall identify the record and criteria reviewed, new material admitted, opportunities for response, questions determined, reasons, remedy, and implementation responsibility. A procedural reviewer acting outside authority to decide factual merits shall remit the appropriate question rather than relabel a procedural finding as a final factual conclusion.}
\req{C210-085}{Where a remedy requires renewed notice, evidence examination, reassignment, reconsideration, or action, the operator shall record completion evidence before asserting the remedy fulfilled. The renewed disposition shall link the earlier decision and further-review grounds. Repeating the earlier conclusion without performing the required remedy shall not constitute compliance with the review outcome.}
\req{C210-086}{A reopening assessment shall distinguish newly available evidence, a material error, changed target conditions, changed categories or criteria, procedural defect, and unimplemented remedy. It shall state whether these grounds affect the past assessment, current action, or both. A later event shall not automatically prove that an earlier claim was mistaken under the evidence then available.}
\req{C210-087}{A reopened case shall preserve the earlier scope, evidence state, disposition, and consequential uses to the permitted extent and identify the new review boundary. New and prior issues may be linked or separated when the reason is recorded. Reopening shall not silently reset an overdue action or delete the history of a missed response.}
\req{C210-088}{When a service period is exceeded, the operator shall record the missed event, cause, effect, notice, revised target or authorized extension, and available escalation. An extension shall identify who authorized it and why it is consistent with the declared procedure. Repeated extensions shall trigger capacity review and a reasoned alternative, rather than indefinite pending status without accountability.}
\req{C210-089}{A handover, operator closure, or loss of a responsible function shall identify open cases, pending actions, permitted successor access, affected notices, and continuity limits. Transfer shall require a stated authority and custody record. Where no successor can accept responsibility, users shall receive the available permitted explanation and alternatives; an unavailable service shall not continue to be advertised as operational.}

\section{Protection and retention}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-027}{The operator shall specify access, retention, correction and disposal arrangements for submissions, evidence, reviewer notes, dispositions and notifications. Content and audit metadata shall be considered separately; history preservation shall not justify retaining prohibited sensitive material.}
\req{C210-028}{Disclosure, redaction, access withdrawal or disposal affecting a case shall identify its authority, scope, executor and permitted evidence of the action. The operator shall state known limits concerning copies outside its control.}
\req{C210-029}{A public summary shall distinguish the accessible grounds from material that cannot be disclosed, where that distinction is itself permitted. It shall not claim full independent inspectability when a material evidential restriction prevents it.}

\subsection{Differentiated access and protected case handling}
\req{C210-090}{The operator shall distinguish submission content, identity and contact information, evidence, internal working material, disposition, public summary, and audit information in its access and retention schedule. Access shall correspond to an identified purpose and authority. Permission to examine one component shall not be presumed to authorize onward publication or unrestricted reuse of the case.}
\req{C210-091}{Before relying on protected grounds for a consequential adverse disposition, the reviewer shall assess whether affected parties can receive a sufficient account or a credible authorized alternative examination. If protection prevents fair examination of a material issue, the operator shall narrow the finding, defer it, or state the unresolved limitation rather than claim fully contestable determination.}
\req{C210-092}{A controlled view shall identify its source case and disposition version, intended audience, material selection or transformation, and permitted limitations. If acknowledging a particular item's existence is restricted, the view shall state its scope at a safe level without exposing that existence. The protected accountability record shall remain available only within its authorized boundary.}
\req{C210-093}{A suspected unauthorized disclosure, misdirected notification, or compromise of protected evidence shall receive recorded triage, containment within authority, affected-case assessment, permitted notice, and review of whether the evidence or procedure remains usable. Correction of the public copy shall not be described as recall of copies beyond the operator's control.}
\req{C210-094}{A retention or disposal decision shall assess ongoing review, action, and reconstruction needs against the applicable protection basis, authority, and schedule. The record shall state what is retained or disposed of, when, by whom, and the permitted verification method. It shall not retain prohibited content merely because a review might theoretically occur in the future.}
\req{C210-095}{An assessor denied access to material required for an applicable finding shall state the resulting limitation. A protected assessment by another authorized competent person may be cited with its scope and independence limits, but shall not be represented as the first assessor's direct examination. Confidentiality shall not convert an unassessed obligation into fulfillment.}

\section{Conformance and assessment}
\subsection{General}
\req{C210-030}{A conformance report shall identify the procedure or case record assessed, operator, institutional context, this draft and dependency versions, applicable requirements, reviewer relationship, evidence and results. It shall identify the case's material claims as a bounded GR-200 Working Draft 0.3 package and report its applicable requirement results, including package-level obligations. A case-record assessment alone shall not establish that unobserved procedural steps occurred.}
\req{C210-031}{Each applicable requirement shall receive a supported result of fulfilled, not fulfilled or not assessed; a not-applicable result shall give its trigger-based reason. An unqualified claim of fulfillment shall not omit a failed or unassessed applicable requirement.}
\req{C210-032}{A procedure assessment shall include an ordinary case and relevant failure cases concerning conflict, unavailable evidence, missed timing, restricted disclosure and correction propagation. A case-record-only assessment shall identify the supplied case and the procedural behavior it cannot establish. Reported success shall be limited to the cases and conditions actually exercised.}

This edition defines no reduced case-record conformance profile. A case-only report is a partial documentary assessment. Performance obligations remain not assessed when only their records were examined; not applicable requires an absent stated trigger. A procedure claim covers all applicable requirements and the declared GR-200 package dependency. The multi-case rehearsal obligation in C210-032 is explicitly conditional on procedure assessment.

\subsection{Assessment plan, results, and limitations}
\req{C210-096}{Before assessment, the assessor shall define the procedure version, operator and institutional scope, period observed, cases selected, relevant failure conditions, methods, evidence access, and independence or author involvement. A sample of cases shall have an explicit selection rationale and limit; a favorable sample shall not be generalized to unexamined settings without supporting grounds.}
\req{C210-097}{The assessment register shall give each applicable requirement its own result, evidence locator, method, assessor, and unresolved action, distinguishing documentary adequacy from actual performance where both are required. A grouped test may support several results only when its evidence supports each obligation separately.}
\req{C210-098}{A procedure assessment shall use the minimum coverage schedule in Annex C and justify any additional failure cases arising from the declared context. A synthetic exercise shall identify fictional actors and unperformed external actions. A demonstration of software behavior shall not establish competent human review, meaningful participation, legal authority, or practical remedy.}
\req{C210-099}{Assessment shall include an intended user's attempt to locate the route, identify the issue, understand the notice and reasons, and determine the next available action under permitted conditions. The report shall record assistance, barriers, failures, and participant relationship to the design. Author familiarity shall not be reported as independent evidence of ordinary-user comprehension.}
\req{C210-100}{An assessment finding that results in a corrected procedure, case record, or implementation shall identify the changed version, affected requirements, corrective action, and reassessment scope. Results for unchanged material may be reused only with a recorded reason that the change does not invalidate the evidence. A prior pass shall not automatically transfer across a material change.}
\req{C210-101}{A public assessment summary shall identify the assessed scope, dependency versions, methods, failures and unassessed requirements, protected-evidence limitations, and whether actions were observed or merely documented. It shall not use a certificate, badge, or concise status to imply factual truth, institutional legitimacy, or full procedure fulfillment beyond the evidence.}

\GSPannex{informative}{Worked cases and interpretation}
\subsection{Missing attachment and changed classification}
The following case elaborates the synthetic dossier in \path{examples/validation_0.2/p1-contested-decision.md}. None of its actors, notices, decisions, or remedies represents a live institution. A fictional research fund rejects a water-monitoring application after a generated summary states that the equipment was never calibrated. Its intake extract shows only that a calibration attachment was absent. A technician later supplies testimony and a maintenance-log excerpt, while the protected original contains contact details.

Intake creates a case with three questions: whether calibration occurred, whether the application met the existing documentary rule, and whether that rule gives appropriate recognition to maintenance evidence. The first requires claim-relative assessment. The second requires application of the declared rule version. The third is referred to the function authorized to review the rule. None of these operations alone determines a legal claim or an award of funding.

The reviewer distinguishes the absent attachment from non-occurrence, the generated summary from source evidence, and the log excerpt from independent examination of its original. Testimony and a log can share a source; their number does not establish independence. The protected original receives controlled handling with a safe account of the grounds available for response. If the reviewer cannot examine calibration quality or instrument identity, the disposition preserves those limits even while revising the overbroad statement that no calibration occurred.

The record correction links the old and revised claims. A later rule change records its own effective interval rather than making the earlier decision appear to have used the new rule. Scheduling a renewed eligibility review creates an action-pending state, not an award or restoration of lost training opportunities. Practical follow-up investigates the reported interruption, the scope of attribution, and the actual effect of any later response.

\subsection{Protected grounds and unavailable independent review}
In a second synthetic case, a person disputes an adverse classification based partly on restricted testimony. The operator cannot safely disclose the witness's identity. It provides a redacted account of the material allegation and explores controlled examination by an independent reviewer. The distinction between protection and warrant matters: hiding an identity does not establish the allegation, while the need to test an allegation does not itself authorize publication of the identity.

Suppose the only available reviewer authored the classification. They can explain their original sources and conduct a disclosed reconsideration, but the operator cannot label that work independent. If a credible alternative examination cannot be arranged, the disposition identifies the unresolved procedural and evidential limits. A narrow temporary protective action can be separately considered within actual authority; it does not convert the disputed allegation into a final finding.

The relevant assessment results remain granular. A documented restriction basis can fulfill a documentary obligation. An unperformed independent review remains not assessed. A case record describing a desired notice does not establish delivery or a meaningful response opportunity. The case can close administratively under declared limits without claiming that the underlying factual disagreement has been resolved.

\subsection{Outage, deadline, and later reopening}
A third synthetic case concerns a challenge received during a service outage. The original timestamp and failed acknowledgement remain recorded. An extension notice identifies the operational cause, responsible role, revised target and escalation route. If a separate institutional deadline applies, the operator states its asserted source and whether it has authority to alter it; a service notice is not treated as changing external law.

Later material reveals that the original classification depended on a mistaken identity link. Reopening identifies this evidential trigger and the affected past assessment, while preserving the decision and its consequences during the intervening period. The remedy includes checking known dependent views and actions. An unreachable external recipient is recorded as unreachable rather than silently treated as corrected.

\GSPannex{informative}{Source rationale and unresolved profile decisions}
\subsection{Operational interpretation}
The synthetic funding case accompanying GR-110 supplies a common example. The procedure's record requirements can be implemented manually before a network protocol is needed. A later Specification would define message correlation, delivery, authorization, concurrent revision and failure semantics in response to demonstrated requirements.

The initial operational direction is recorded in ADR-0004. This expanded procedure remains a programme proposal, not a claim that the foundation manuscripts prescribe these case states, deadlines, or institutional functions. Chapter 5 treats evidence as a role relative to an epistemic target and distinguishes just participation from evidential adequacy. Those distinctions motivate separate assessment of access, method, warrant, disposition, and consequences. The record preserves both a supported conclusion and the limits of the procedure that produced it.
For the expansion decision and alternatives, see \path{decisions/ADR-0005-expanded-drafts-and-source-models.md}. The comparison record is \path{docs/references/drafting_models.md}.

The choice of explicit schedules follows the need to make a procedure exercisable by someone other than its author. Alternatives include a general right-to-comment statement, a fixed software workflow, or a single universal adjudication model. The first leaves response and remedy underspecified; the other two impose technical or institutional commitments beyond this draft's scope. The selected approach fixes inspectable obligations while profiles specify contextual authority and service arrangements.

The United Nations National Quality Assurance Frameworks Manual provides an informative comparison for separating institutional capacity, process quality, and output quality. Here, the operator and authority declaration, observed review behavior, and resulting case record receive distinct assessment evidence. This translation is a programme design choice; it does not assert that an official-statistics framework creates rights or governs the review procedure. ISO's model document and ITU-T drafting guidance inform publication structure rather than the evidential meaning of a finding.

\openissue{Service periods, independence arrangements, proof of authority, available remedies, costs and admissible disclosure depend on the institution and require a scoped profile. A synthetic procedure cannot establish their validity in a live jurisdiction.}
\openissue{Abuse handling, protection of vulnerable participants, meaningful access under low resources and the burden of keeping case histories require review with affected users before live deployment.}
\openissue{No reduced profile, universal evidential threshold, legal admissibility rule, or machine protocol is adopted. Domain-specific proof standards, representative arrangements, protected disclosure mechanisms, and independently feasible remedies require their own competent review and recorded authority.}
