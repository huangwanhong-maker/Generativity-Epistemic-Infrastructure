# GR-140 — Generativity presentation requirements

**Publication class:** Standards-track Working Draft; not an adopted Standard  
**Maturity:** Preparatory WD 0.1, 2026-09-22; proposed requirements for review  
**Scope of this edition:** Record-graph projection, historical consistency and inspectable interaction

## 1. Scope and reference basis

This bounded draft addresses how a record graph is displayed and edited without confusing its visual arrangement with claims about reality. It develops the earlier outline using GR-100/110/200 WD 0.3, GR-120 WD 0.1 and the source analysis in the [graph semantics review](../../docs/planning/graph_semantics_review.md). It does not yet cover complete trajectory reconstruction, public redaction, timeline semantics or all accessibility obligations.

## 2. Terms

**projection:** an explicitly selected view derived from a declared record revision and display policy.

**incidence view:** a projection representing a Relation as an identifiable node and its participant incidences as role-labelled connections.

**display state:** position, zoom, selection and comparable presentation preferences that do not themselves assert a change to a represented target or record.

## 3. Proposed presentation requirements

**P140-001.** A presentation shall identify the revision and projection being displayed, and distinguish a historical selection from the currently published revision.

**P140-002.** A coherent historical view shall resolve participant labels, record content, modules and associated material from the selected revision. It shall not silently substitute current values into that historical view.

**P140-003.** A projection shall preserve access to the identity, description and qualifications of each represented Relation, including when a simplified binary edge is used. The projection shall disclose the treatment of unary, repeated and higher-order participation.

**P140-004.** A projection shall distinguish neutral navigation references from stated semantic relations. Position, proximity, colour, edge direction and size shall not imply unsupported causality, certainty, normative approval or legal status.

**P140-005.** A filtered view shall disclose that filtering occurred and provide an indication of omitted records or incidences. An unavailable or uninterpreted participant shall not be silently rendered as if its absence were known to be semantically immaterial.

**P140-006.** A partial relational account shall expose its declared incompleteness and qualifications. An unstructured Relation account shall remain inspectable without invented incidences.

**P140-007.** View manipulation shall be distinguished from recorded changes. A user shall be able to identify when an action merely rearranges a display and when it proposes a retained, attributed change.

**P140-008.** A relation-creation interface shall expose participant selection, roles, reference scope and completeness sufficiently for the user to inspect the resulting account before submission. Two clicked nodes alone shall not silently determine an unqualified substantive relation.

**P140-009.** A client shall preserve a pending change after a detected revision conflict and expose a review route before resubmitting it against a new base. A historical view shall not offer an ambiguous mutation that appears to edit the past.

**P140-010.** Important graph-inspection and creation functions shall have an alternative that does not require precise pointer manipulation of the graph canvas. The declared assessment scope shall distinguish presence of controls from demonstrated accessibility.

**P140-011.** Unsupported optional modules and application limitations shall remain visible as interpretation limitations. A successful diagram render shall not be presented as whole-record validation, factual confirmation or programme conformance.

## 4. Assessment and infrastructure need

Test cases compare the visible view with selected-revision records, rename a participant in a later revision, filter a participating record, inspect higher-order and repeated relations, change layout without changing history, create a connection through both pointer and form controls, and preserve a draft across conflict. Automated browser checks are useful but do not substitute for assistive-technology and participant evaluation.

GR-SPEC-120's incidence projection supplies a reference graph representation. The pilot uses a canvas renderer and a parallel record list, participant form and inspector. No particular graph library or layout algorithm is required by this draft.

## 5. Open matters

Q-14, Q-25 and Q-40 cover evaluation and accessibility. Layout stability across large revisions, comparative projections, missing/restricted cross-project participants, redaction and comprehensive generative trajectory views remain open.
