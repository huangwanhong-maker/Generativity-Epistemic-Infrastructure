# Public Comment

**Document class:** Informative governance template  
**Status:** PROVISIONAL template for comments, dispositions, and rehearsal  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Responsible editor:** UNASSIGNED  
**Source:** `templates/public_comment.md`  
**Historical draft basis (0.1):** [Directives Part 1](../publications/archive/0.1/governance/directives_part_1.tex), version 0.1, D1-018–D1-028; [Directives Part 2](../publications/archive/0.1/governance/directives_part_2.tex), version 0.1.

The template remains an initial 0.1 aid. The expanded 0.2 normative schedules govern any assessment against 0.2; completing this template alone does not cover their additional obligations.


Free-text comments are welcome: this form is an aid, not an eligibility test. Commenters need not supply redrafted clauses, credentials, or decision identifiers. Programme staff complete receipt, disposition, and decision fields. This template establishes no live review or appointed staff role.
