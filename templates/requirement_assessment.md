## Requirement ledger

Create a row for every requirement within the declared assessment boundary, including failed and unassessed requirements. A grouped narrative does not replace individual results. Separate components when a clause's recording and behavioral obligations need different evidence.

| Requirement and edition | Trigger / applicability reason | Method and actual evidence locator | Result | Assessor / date | Limitation or required action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ID] | [condition] | [document inspection, recalculation, observed exercise, user task, expert review; exact record] | [one result below] | [actual attribution] | [text] |

Result meanings:

- **Fulfilled:** The supplied evidence supports the required outcome within the stated boundary; include reasons.
- **Not fulfilled:** A required outcome fails; identify the evidence and consequence.
- **Not assessed:** Assessment was not performed or required evidence cannot be examined. An unknown, inaccessible source or unobserved action is not automatically a pass.
- **Not applicable:** The requirement's declared trigger is absent; identify that trigger and grounds. Changing the assessment subject does not silently remove an obligation.

A draft can require recording a justified unknown without requiring the historical fact itself to be settled. In that case, assess the stated recording obligation, and keep uncertainty about the substantive fact distinct from the result.
